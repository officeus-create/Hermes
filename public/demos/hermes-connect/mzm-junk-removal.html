Job economics

Revenue is not profit

CRM should make the controllable contribution visible without inventing labor cost.

Completed job revenue − lead cost − disposal cost = contribution before labor / overhead Labor, fuel, vehicle, insurance and overhead can be added only when the business supplies an approved costing model. Until then they remain unknown rather than estimated.

Marketing attribution

One measurement chain across Website, Google, Thumbtack and social

Organic search does not always expose the exact user query. CRM stores the strongest available evidence instead of fabricating a keyword.

Paid-media gate: Audit → Organic Programming → Stable Organic Baseline → Controlled Paid Learning → Signal Gate → Offer → Funnel → CRM → Sale. Paid spend is not treated as the first step.
