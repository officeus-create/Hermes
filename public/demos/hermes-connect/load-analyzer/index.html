Route Intelligence path

This calculator is the first layer of a larger operating workflow.

The next product layers will connect selected opportunities, explain source status, compare alternatives, and hand a reviewed decision to the appropriate human workflow without turning private operational observations into public claims.

Human handoffCarrier, dispatcher, or trainee continues in the approved review process. No external booking action happens here.
