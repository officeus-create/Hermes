HERMES LOGISTICS · CARRIER ACQUISITION

Can you turn a cold conversation into a useful business relationship?

Hermes is testing a new hiring and training path for people who want to work with U.S. carriers. We care less about a polished résumé and more about whether you can listen, understand a business situation, ask useful questions, learn from feedback and apply what you learn.

IF YOU ARE NOT READY YET

A weak résumé is not the end of the path.

If a reviewer sees useful potential but not enough evidence for a supervised work test, you may be offered a Hermes Academy practice path. Academy participation does not guarantee employment; it gives you a structured way to build and demonstrate missing capabilities.

01UnderstandLearn the role and carrier context.
02PracticeScenarios, objections and call thinking.
03ReviewFeedback on observable evidence.
04Re-testReturn to a readiness gate.
05Human gateAuthorized reviewer decides live-work scope.
