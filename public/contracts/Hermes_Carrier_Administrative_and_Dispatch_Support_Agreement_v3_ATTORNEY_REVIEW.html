Appointment, Authority, and Control

The Carrier keeps the operation. Hermes supports the workflow.

1.1 Limited appointment

The Carrier appoints Hermes as a limited, non-exclusive agent solely to provide the administrative and dispatch support services selected in Appendix A. Hermes may act only within the Carrier's instructions, this Agreement, and applicable law.

1.2 Carrier approval is required

  • The Carrier may accept or reject every load, rate, broker, shipper, route, pickup, delivery, and material operating term.
  • Hermes may not bind the Carrier to transportation without Carrier approval through an agreed communication method.

1.3 Carrier remains in control

  • The Carrier exclusively controls drivers, equipment, maintenance, safety, hours of service, insurance, permits, routing, scheduling, cargo handling, and compliance.
  • The Carrier verifies that each shipment is lawful, safe, within authority, insurable, and operationally acceptable.

1.4 No brokerage or re-brokering

Hermes is not appointed to arrange transportation for the public as a broker or freight forwarder. Hermes will not re-broker freight, hold itself out as the transporting motor carrier, collect freight charges, or exercise discretion inconsistent with a bona fide carrier agent relationship.

1.5 Non-exclusive; no minimums

The Carrier may self-dispatch or use other providers. Neither party guarantees or requires any minimum loads, miles, revenue, trucks, or service hours.

Carrier-control promiseNo load moves because Hermes wants it. A load moves only after the Carrier approves it and remains able to perform it safely and lawfully.

Services and Operating Process

Clear scope, clear handoff, no hidden transfer of responsibility.

2.1 Services Hermes may provide

  • Market and load research; opportunity screening; rate and term discussion within Carrier instructions.
  • Communication with brokers, shippers, dealers, auction facilities, and other counterparties as the Carrier's identified administrative contact.
  • Preparation or coordination of rate confirmations, carrier packets, proof-of-delivery follow-up, invoicing support, and ordinary document reminders.
  • Lane planning, deadhead analysis, scheduling support, and carrier-development research when selected in Appendix A.

2.2 Services Hermes does not provide

  • Hermes does not drive, transport, load, secure, store, inspect, insure, repair, or take custody of cargo or equipment.
  • Hermes does not provide legal, tax, insurance, safety, factoring, or regulatory advice and does not guarantee counterparty creditworthiness.
  • Hermes does not receive freight proceeds unless the Carrier later gives a separate lawful written authorization for a specific payment workflow.

2.3 Load confirmation

A rate confirmation, dispatch sheet, email, text, recorded business call, portal confirmation, or other agreed record may document Carrier approval. The Carrier must promptly correct a material error before performance.

2.4 Credentials and private documents

Passwords, PINs, recovery codes, API keys, W-9s, CDL images, bank details, VIN lists, release documents, and private shipment records must not be entered into the public signing page. Later handoff must use a separately approved secure method.

2.5 No guaranteed result

Hermes does not guarantee a load, rate, mileage, lane, customer, booking, payment, response time, fuel cost, profit, gross revenue, or any other business outcome.

Freight paymentsBrokers, shippers, dealers, or factoring companies pay the Carrier or its designated factor directly. Hermes earns only the service fee stated in Appendix A.

Fees, Invoices, and Payment

The master stays flexible; the exact percentage lives in Appendix A.

3.1 Service fee

The Carrier will pay Hermes the percentage written in signed Appendix A. Unless Appendix A says otherwise, the percentage applies to linehaul actually earned on loads booked or materially arranged through Hermes, excluding separately stated pass-through charges, advances, and reimbursements.

3.2 Weekly statement and due date

Hermes may issue a weekly statement identifying loads and calculation. The invoiced amount is due within seven (7) calendar days after delivery unless Appendix A states another schedule.

3.3 Good-faith invoice review

The Carrier must send a specific written objection within five (5) business days, identifying the disputed load and reason. Undisputed amounts remain due. Failure to object is evidence of acceptance, subject to correction of fraud, duplicate billing, or clear mathematical error.

3.4 Late amounts

An undisputed amount not paid when due may accrue a late charge at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum lawful rate. Hermes may pause new work while undisputed invoices remain overdue after reasonable notice.

3.5 Collection costs

After written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure, the Carrier is responsible for reasonable, documented collection costs and attorney fees to the extent permitted by law or awarded by a court. This Agreement does not create a personal guaranty, blanket lien, UCC security interest, automatic card debit, or confession of judgment.

3.6 Optional direct-payment instruction

Only through a separate written Carrier authorization, and only when allowed by the broker, shipper, factor, contract, and law, the Carrier may instruct a third party to remit a specifically identified undisputed Hermes fee. This clause alone does not transfer freight receivables or authorize Hermes to collect freight funds.

Term, Termination, and Responsibilities

Easy to start, clear to stop, responsibilities remain where they belong.

4.1 Start and term

This Agreement begins when both parties sign an approved execution version and Appendix A. It continues until terminated.

4.2 Termination without cause

Either party may terminate on seven (7) calendar days' written notice. The Carrier may immediately stop accepting new opportunities. Hermes may finish ordinary administrative follow-up for previously approved loads unless instructed otherwise.

4.3 Immediate suspension or termination

Either party may act immediately for material breach, fraud, unsafe or unlawful instructions, inactive authority, inadequate insurance, nonpayment of undisputed amounts, credential misuse, harassment, sanctions concerns, or serious compliance risk.

4.4 Amounts surviving termination

Termination does not erase fees properly earned on loads approved before termination, confidentiality duties, audit records, dispute provisions, or obligations intended to survive.

4.5 Carrier representations

  • Maintain required authority, registrations, insurance, equipment, personnel, and qualifications.
  • Provide accurate business information and an authorized signer.
  • Do not ask Hermes to misrepresent authority, identity, equipment, insurance, capacity, location, or shipment status.

4.6 Hermes responsibilities

  • Use commercially reasonable care and follow lawful Carrier instructions.
  • Maintain reasonable records and avoid knowingly misrepresenting the Carrier.
  • Protect private business information subject to service, security, payment, and legal needs.
Simple exitThe agreement is not designed to trap the Carrier. Either party may end the relationship with notice, while completed work and earned fees are reconciled.

Risk, Confidentiality, and Legal Terms

Balanced protections without personal guarantees or hidden control.

5.1 Independent businesses

The parties are independent contractors. Nothing creates employment, partnership, joint venture, franchise, ownership interest, or authority beyond the limited agency expressly stated here.

5.2 Confidentiality and data

Each party will use non-public business information only for the relationship, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to people and providers who need it or when legally required. Electronic records may be retained in accurate, reproducible form for business, audit, and legal purposes.

5.3 Third parties

Brokers, shippers, dealers, load boards, factors, insurers, repair providers, and other third parties are independent. Hermes is not responsible for their acts, insolvency, systems, decisions, or payment failures, except to the extent caused by Hermes' breach or misconduct.

5.4 Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is liable for indirect, special, punitive, or consequential damages. Hermes' aggregate liability will not exceed service fees paid during the thirty (30) days preceding the claim, except for fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality breach, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

5.5 Indemnity

Each party will defend and indemnify the other from third-party claims to the extent caused by its own negligence, willful misconduct, legal violation, breach, or responsibility allocated to it. Transportation, cargo, driver, equipment, safety, and regulatory risks remain Carrier responsibilities.

5.6 Governing law and venue

Wisconsin law governs. Subject to mandatory law, state and federal courts serving Ozaukee County, Wisconsin have exclusive venue. The parties may first attempt good-faith resolution but do not waive lawful court rights.

5.7 Notices and electronic signatures

Notices may be sent to designated business emails or channels. The parties consent to electronic records and signatures when they affirmatively choose the process and can access and retain the resulting documents.

5.8 Entire agreement; order

The approved master, signed Appendix A, and signed amendments form the entire agreement. A later amendment controls, then Appendix A for fee and scope, then the master. Sales messages do not amend signed documents.
