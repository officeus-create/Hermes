1. Incorporation and priority

This Addendum is incorporated into the Master Agreement and Appendix A. If this Addendum conflicts with the Master Agreement, this Addendum controls as to compensation, records, Protected Accounts, non-circumvention, personnel, and survival. References to Operations + Growth mean Full Partnership.

2. Approved commercial models

  • 6% Dispatch Support: opportunity research, rate negotiation within Carrier instructions, counterparty communication, setup packets, booking coordination, and ordinary dispatch documentation.
  • 8% Full Partnership: all Dispatch Support services plus lane and route planning, direct shipper and dealer development, invoicing follow-up, reporting, and broader operational support.
  • Carrier Proposal: a proposed percentage or scope is non-binding until Hermes confirms it in writing and the parties sign matching terms.
No hidden percentage. The exact selected percentage and billing basis must appear in signed Appendix A or a signed amendment. No different fee applies merely because another booking channel, affiliate, dispatcher, or related authority is used.

3. Hermes-Supported Load and fee-earning event

A Hermes-Supported Load is a specific shipment, opportunity, route, customer movement, or transportation transaction that Hermes identifies, sources, introduces, researches, negotiates, books, dispatches, monitors, documents, invoices, follows up, or otherwise materially supports for the Carrier.

Unless Appendix A states a different written basis, the selected fee is earned when the Carrier or a Carrier-related person accepts, performs, invoices, collects, factors, receives settlement for, or otherwise receives transportation revenue from a Hermes-Supported Load. The fee remains due if the transaction is later booked, confirmed, invoiced, paid, or administered through another platform, dispatcher, affiliate, authority, owner, employee, contractor, driver, factor, or intermediary.

The fee applies to linehaul or other transportation revenue identified in Appendix A, excluding separately stated pass-through charges, advances, reimbursements, and taxes unless Appendix A expressly includes them.

4. Weekly statements and payment

The regular work period is Monday through Saturday. Hermes may issue the weekly statement or invoice on Saturday, identifying covered loads and the calculation. Unless Appendix A states another schedule, the invoiced amount is due within seven calendar days after the invoice date.

The Carrier must send a specific written objection within five business days, identifying the disputed load, amount, and reason. Undisputed amounts remain due. Failure to provide a timely specific objection is evidence of acceptance, subject to correction of fraud, duplicate billing, or clear mathematical error.

An undisputed overdue amount may accrue a late charge at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum lawful rate. Hermes may pause new work after reasonable written notice while undisputed invoices remain overdue.

5. Protected Accounts

A Protected Account is a shipper, dealer, auction, fleet, customer, broker program, or direct commercial contact first introduced to the Carrier or materially developed for the Carrier by Hermes and identified in a Protected Account Notice, email, CRM record, message, meeting record, or other dated business record.

The Carrier may object within five business days after notice by providing dated evidence of a pre-existing active relationship. Failure to object creates a rebuttable presumption that the notice is accurate.

During the Agreement and for twenty-four months after termination, the selected fee applies to transportation revenue the Carrier or a Carrier-related owner, affiliate, authority, factor, employee, contractor, driver, or designee receives from a Protected Account, regardless of booking or payment channel.

6. Non-circumvention

The Carrier may work with a Protected Account, but may not conceal covered revenue or route business through another MC, authority, affiliate, owner, driver, employee, contractor, dispatcher, factor, or intermediary for the purpose of avoiding Hermes's agreed compensation. The Carrier will not direct a Protected Account away from Hermes solely to defeat the fee.

7. Records, accounting, and verification

The Carrier will maintain and, on reasonable request, provide records reasonably necessary to verify covered compensation, including relevant rate confirmations, bills of lading or proof of delivery, carrier invoices, settlement statements, factor reports, and payment records. Requests must be limited to covered transactions and handled confidentially.

Hermes may request a limited reconciliation no more than once per calendar quarter, except where a specific discrepancy, nonpayment, or suspected circumvention exists. If required records are withheld after written notice, Hermes may issue a reasonable provisional calculation based on available records, subject to prompt reconciliation when complete records are supplied.

If a reconciliation shows an underpayment greater than five percent for the reviewed period, the Carrier will reimburse reasonable documented third-party review costs in addition to the unpaid fee.

8. Targeted protection of Hermes personnel

During the relationship and for twelve months after it ends, the Carrier will not knowingly solicit, induce, hire, directly contract with, or route substantially similar services around Hermes through a Hermes employee, agent, dispatcher, assistant, or contractor who materially served the Carrier and had direct business contact with the Carrier.

This restriction does not cover general advertisements not targeted at Hermes personnel, relationships documented before Hermes's introduction, or contact unrelated to Hermes services.

9. Remedies without an automatic penalty

Available remedies include unpaid contractual fees on covered revenue, an accounting, actual proven damages, reasonable documented collection costs and attorney fees to the extent permitted by law or awarded by a court, and lawful equitable relief when money damages are inadequate. The parties do not agree to an automatic punitive or arbitrary fixed penalty.

10. Survival

Termination does not erase fees already earned or later earned from pre-termination Hermes-Supported Loads. Protected Account obligations survive for twenty-four months; the targeted personnel restriction survives for twelve months; and confidentiality, records, accounting, payment, collection, electronic-record, venue, indemnity, limitation, and dispute terms survive according to their nature.

11. Carrier control and broker boundary

The Carrier retains final approval of every load and exclusive control of drivers, equipment, safety, insurance, routing, compliance, and transportation performance. Hermes acts only for the identified Carrier within Carrier instructions. If a transaction requires broker authority or arranging transportation for the public, it must be handled through a separately authorized and properly licensed broker.

12. Signatures

By signing, each signer confirms authority to bind the identified business and agrees that this Addendum is part of the Master Agreement and Appendix A.
