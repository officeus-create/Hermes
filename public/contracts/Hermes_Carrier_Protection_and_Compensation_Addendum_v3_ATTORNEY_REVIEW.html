1. Incorporation and priority

This Addendum is incorporated into the Master Agreement and Appendix A. It controls conflicting language concerning compensation, records, Protected Accounts, non-circumvention, remedies, personnel, and survival. References to Operations + Growth mean Full Partnership.

2. Approved commercial models

  • 6% Dispatch Support: opportunity research, negotiation within Carrier instructions, counterparty communication, setup packets, booking coordination, and ordinary dispatch documentation.
  • 8% Full Partnership: Dispatch Support plus lane planning, direct shipper and dealer development, invoicing follow-up, reporting, and broader operational support.
  • Carrier Proposal: non-binding until Hermes confirms it in writing and both parties sign matching terms.
No hidden percentage. The exact percentage and billing basis must appear in signed Appendix A or a signed amendment.

3. Hermes-Supported Load and earned fee

A Hermes-Supported Load is a shipment, opportunity, route, customer movement, or transportation transaction Hermes identifies, sources, introduces, researches, negotiates, books, dispatches, monitors, documents, invoices, follows up, or otherwise materially supports for the Carrier.

Unless Appendix A states another written basis, the selected fee is earned when Carrier or a Carrier-related person accepts, performs, invoices, collects, factors, receives settlement for, or otherwise receives transportation revenue from a Hermes-Supported Load. The fee remains due if another platform, dispatcher, affiliate, authority, owner, employee, contractor, driver, factor, or intermediary is later used.

4. Weekly invoice and payment

The regular work period is Monday through Saturday. Hermes may issue the weekly invoice on Saturday. Unless Appendix A states another schedule, payment is due within seven calendar days after the invoice date. Carrier must send a specific written objection within five business days; undisputed amounts remain due.

An undisputed overdue amount accrues a simple late charge at the lesser of 1.5% per month (18% annually) or the maximum lawful rate, without compounding. The parties expressly agree to this written rate in the signed contract; it is not added only by a later invoice.

5. Protected Accounts

A Protected Account is a shipper, dealer, auction, fleet, customer, broker program, or direct commercial contact first introduced to Carrier or materially developed for Carrier by Hermes and identified in a dated notice or business record. Carrier may object within five business days with dated proof of a pre-existing active relationship.

During the Agreement and for twenty-four months after termination, the selected fee applies to covered transportation revenue received by Carrier or a Carrier-related person, regardless of booking or payment channel.

6. Non-circumvention

Carrier may work with a Protected Account but may not conceal covered revenue or route business through another MC, authority, affiliate, owner, driver, employee, contractor, dispatcher, factor, or intermediary for the purpose of avoiding Hermes's agreed compensation.

7. Wisconsin liquidated-damages formula

The parties acknowledge that concealed transactions and diverted account revenue can be difficult to measure. If Carrier intentionally circumvents Hermes or fails to provide required records after written notice, Hermes may elect either actual proven damages or liquidated damages equal to the unpaid selected fee on affected revenue.

The calculation must use actual records when available. If Carrier withholds them, Hermes may use a reasonable, rebuttable estimate based on available rate confirmations, settlement data, load counts, comparable transactions, and ordinary business records. Carrier may rebut the estimate with complete records. Hermes may not recover duplicative actual damages and liquidated damages for the same loss. No arbitrary fixed minimum fine applies.

8. Records and verification

Carrier will provide records reasonably necessary to verify covered compensation, limited to relevant rate confirmations, proof of delivery, invoices, settlement statements, factor reports, and payment records. A limited reconciliation may occur no more than once per quarter unless a specific discrepancy, nonpayment, or suspected circumvention exists. If underpayment exceeds five percent, Carrier reimburses reasonable documented third-party review costs.

9. Hermes personnel and lawful mobility

Carrier is free to hire or contract with any person. For twelve months after a person materially served Carrier, Carrier will not knowingly use that person to divert Hermes accounts, misuse Hermes confidential information, or continue Hermes services in breach of that person's written duties to Hermes for the purpose of avoiding Hermes's fee. General recruiting and lawful hiring without such misuse are allowed. There is no automatic hiring fee or no-hire penalty.

10. Collection and attorney fees

After written cure notice, Carrier must reimburse reasonable documented pre-suit collection costs. In an action or proceeding to enforce payment, Protected Account, non-circumvention, records, or confidentiality obligations, the substantially prevailing party may recover reasonable attorney fees and court costs, subject to Wisconsin law, including Wis. Stat. § 814.045.

11. Survival and control

Protected Account obligations survive for twenty-four months. Confidentiality, records, accounting, payment, collection, electronic-record, venue, indemnity, limitation, and dispute terms survive according to their nature. Carrier retains final approval of every load and exclusive control of drivers, equipment, safety, insurance, routing, compliance, and performance. Work requiring broker authority must be handled through a separately authorized and properly licensed broker.

12. Signatures

By signing, each signer confirms authority to bind the identified business and agrees this Addendum is part of the Master Agreement and Appendix A.
