Carrier review and objection

The Carrier may object within five business days after receipt by sending dated evidence of a pre-existing active relationship with the identified account. Failure to object creates a rebuttable presumption that the notice is accurate. A good-faith objection does not waive undisputed fees or other obligations.

Covered revenue and non-circumvention

The applicable fee covers transportation revenue the Carrier or a Carrier-related owner, affiliate, authority, factor, employee, contractor, driver, or designee receives from the Protected Account, regardless of booking or payment channel. The Carrier may not conceal covered revenue or route business through another MC, authority, affiliate, dispatcher, contractor, or intermediary for the purpose of avoiding the agreed fee.

Wisconsin remedy and records

If intentional circumvention or withholding of required records makes the loss difficult to measure, Hermes may elect actual proven damages or liquidated damages equal to the unpaid selected fee on affected revenue, but not both for the same loss. The calculation uses actual records when available; otherwise Hermes may make a reasonable estimate from available rate, settlement, load-count, and comparable-transaction records, subject to rebuttal and reconciliation.

Acknowledgment

Carrier acknowledgment is useful evidence of receipt but is not the only method of establishing a Protected Account when the signed Agreement, Addendum, and dated business records establish the introduction or material development.
