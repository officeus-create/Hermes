## Reopen criteria

A new Design task may enter the queue only when at least one is true:

1. a reproducible production UX/accessibility/design regression exists;
2. the owner explicitly requests a bounded design change/rebrand decision;
3. a real product change creates a new UI state that cannot be represented by the current Design OS.

Before reopening, check current `main` for duplicates and existing implementation evidence.

Do **not** reopen Design for:

- decorative experimentation without product value;
- old AI opinions or stale task numbers;
- unsupported conversion/performance claims;
- unverified locations/pricing/availability/live status;
- new logo candidates competing with Option №02;
- a fifth Beauty color without owner approval;
- SEO/GEO work that belongs to the search lane.
