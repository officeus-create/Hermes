Your role is to **continue, refine, and strengthen the already approved brand direction** — not to restart the brand from zero.

Before making proposals, read `APPROVED_BRAND_SYSTEM_V1.md` in this same folder and treat it as the source of truth.

## 2. Approved brand direction
Treat **Approved Hermes Connect Brand System V1** as locked unless the owner explicitly reopens it.

Core positioning:
**Hermes Connect = AI Operating System for Business**

Core hero line:
**Run your business with AI**

Brand perception:
- premium
- intelligent
- calm
- modern
- global
- connected
- operational
- scalable
- easier than legacy software
- broader than a narrow CRM or one-industry tool

Approved visual architecture:
1. Pearl Light marketing environment — warm premium canvas, whitespace, large clear typography, subtle depth.
2. Anthracite / Obsidian operating environment — dark command-center UI for dashboards, analytics, AI activity and operations.
3. Light + dark continuity — one product, not two brands.
4. Restrained translucent 3D sculptural hero object that supports the product story.
5. Elegant digital flow-waves / connected flow lines as a signature motif.
6. Controlled editorial industry art directions that remain inside one Hermes system.

Approved logo direction:
**small interconnected loop / intelligent knot / continuous connection symbol**

It should communicate connection, continuity, orchestration, intelligent flow and multiple business processes working as one.

Avoid generic crypto/Web3 symbols, random startup knots, trucking clichés, robot-head AI logos and circuit-brain clichés.

Candidate production palette:
- Pearl Light — `#F7F6F3`
- Anthracite Dark — `#0B0D12`
- Iris Soft Violet — `#7C5CFF`
- Ocean Blue — `#5AC8FA`
- Sage Mint — `#7FD1B6`
- Graphite Neutral — `#1C1E26`
- Sand Warm Neutral — `#F3E9DC`

Tokens may be refined for implementation, accessibility and optical quality, but do not replace the palette with a new visual world without approval.

Approved industry art-direction families:
- **Monochrome** — consulting / agencies / premium B2B
- **Color-block** — real estate / architecture / modern service business
- **Podium** — wellness / health / premium personal service
- **Technicolor** — beauty / lifestyle / fashion-forward categories

Approved AI UX principle:
The product should feel like **one unified Hermes Intelligence**, not many disconnected bots. Hermes may delegate internally to Sales, Marketing, Reception, Finance, Operations, Recruiting and Logistics while keeping the main user experience unified.

## 3. Change classification
Every proposal must be classified as one of:
- **Refinement** — improve the approved system without changing its identity.
- **Extension** — add a screen, module, template, motion pattern or vertical inside the approved system.
- **Experiment** — test an alternative without replacing the approved system.
- **Rebrand Proposal** — change the core identity; requires explicit owner approval.

**Default mode = Refinement.**

## 4. Before proposing changes
Always distinguish:
- Approved
- Exploration
- Suggestion
- Risk
- Open question

Analyze what is already approved, what remains flexible, what is unresolved, what should be refined instead of replaced, and what business objective the design must support.

## 5. Design goal
Hermes Connect should feel like the operating system from which a company can run and grow its business with AI — not merely a CRM, booking tool, dashboard, logistics app or chatbot.

The design should communicate AI orchestration, adaptive setup, business growth, intelligent automation, premium clarity, trust, scale and modern operations.

## 6. UX principles
- clarity over decoration
- hierarchy over noise
- premium minimalism over feature clutter
- usefulness over trend-chasing
- restrained motion over flashy motion
- accessible contrast over weak aesthetics
- one coherent system across website, mobile app, web app, presentations, social media, onboarding and vertical modules

## 7. Avoid
- generic logistics clichés
- truck-heavy identity
- crypto/Web3 resemblance
- random trendy gradients
- uncontrolled glassmorphism
- excessive neon
- robot-head AI branding
- generic circuit-board brains
- visual clutter
- overdesigned dashboards
- separate visual systems for every module
- restarting the logo from zero without justification

## 8. Scope you may improve
Logo refinements, favicon, app icon, wordmark spacing, color tokens, component library, website hero, landing sections, dashboards, mobile UI, onboarding, motion, presentations, social templates, advertising, industry cards, product demos, Hermes Intelligence UX, iconography, typography and accessibility.
