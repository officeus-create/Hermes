## 1. Positioning

Hermes Connect should be presented as an **AI Operating System for Business**, not as a generic CRM, logistics dashboard or isolated chatbot.

Primary working message:

> Run your business with AI.

Product perception goal:

- premium;
- intelligent;
- calm;
- modern;
- global;
- operational;
- easier than legacy business software;
- broader than a single vertical or industry.

## 2. Approved visual architecture

The approved direction combines:

1. **Pearl marketing environment** — warm, premium, light canvas with large typography, generous whitespace and subtle depth.
2. **Anthracite / Obsidian operating environment** — dark command-center UI for dashboards, analytics, AI activity and dense operational work.
3. **Light + dark continuity** — both modes must feel like one product, not two unrelated themes.
4. **Tactile / glass 3D brand object** — a restrained translucent sculptural element may be used in hero storytelling and motion, but it must support the product story rather than become decoration.
5. **Flow-wave signature** — fine digital waves / flowing lines are an approved recurring brand motif for movement, orchestration, signal, connection and intelligence.
6. **Editorial industry cards** — different verticals may use distinct photographic/art-direction moods while keeping the same Hermes typography, spacing, frame geometry and product system.

## 3. Approved logo direction — HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED

> **Superseded:** the text in this section records the earlier August 12 preference. The August 31 owner decision approved **Option 02 continuous connected-flow geometry** for production. Do not implement the intelligent-knot preference below as the current mark.

The current preferred mark direction is a **small interconnected loop / intelligent knot / continuous connection symbol** paired with the Hermes Connect wordmark.

It should communicate:

- connection;
- continuity;
- orchestration;
- multiple business processes working as one;
- movement without literal trucking imagery;
- intelligence without generic AI-brain or circuit-board clichés.

The mark should remain:

- compact;
- legible at favicon/app-icon size;
- strong in monochrome;
- usable in both light and dark environments;
- simple enough to survive long-term brand expansion.

### Allowed refinement

The geometry may still be refined for balance, optical correction, app-icon performance, animation and trademark differentiation.

### Not approved as replacements

A new logo family should not replace this direction merely because it is novel. Alternative concepts such as wings, H monograms, orbital nodes, Bento matrices or Möbius/infinity symbols may be retained as exploration material, but replacing the approved direction requires explicit owner approval.

## 4. Hero system

Approved hero principles:

- large, simple headline;
- premium light canvas;
- one memorable sculptural brand object;
- real product workflow visible nearby;
- no feature-wall overload;
- product motion should explain how Hermes works.

Example workflow storytelling:

`New lead → Hermes replied → qualified → booking confirmed → payment received → review requested → retention workflow`

The page should show the system operating rather than merely list features.

## 5. Product UI

### Light environment

Best suited for:

- marketing website;
- onboarding;
- presentations;
- public product storytelling;
- selected service-business workflows.

### Dark environment

Best suited for:

- command center;
- analytics;
- logistics;
- finance;
- dense operational dashboards;
- advanced AI activity.

### UI behavior

- high information clarity;
- restrained card chrome;
- subtle glass only where useful;
- generous hierarchy;
- premium but practical;
- strong daylight usability;
- WCAG-conscious contrast;
- no decorative complexity that slows comprehension.

## 6. Hermes Intelligence

The user-facing architecture should feel like **one Hermes Intelligence** rather than an army of unrelated bots.

Hermes may internally delegate to specialized systems such as:

- Reception;
- Sales;
- Marketing;
- Finance;
- Operations;
- Recruiting;
- Logistics.

But the main experience should remain unified. Background-agent activity may be exposed as a transparent activity feed when useful.

## 7. Adaptive onboarding

A fast adaptive setup is an approved product direction.

The user selects or describes the business and Hermes configures the relevant modules, navigation, AI capabilities, KPIs and workflows.

Examples of verticals:

- beauty / salons;
- wellness / health;
- professional services;
- real estate;
- fitness;
- automotive services;
- logistics;
- agencies;
- other SMBs.

A business should not see irrelevant modules by default.

## 8. Industry art direction

Approved concept: use different controlled visual moods for industries while preserving one brand system.

Reference mood families include:

- **Monochrome** — professional services, consulting, premium B2B;
- **Color Block** — architecture, real estate, modern service businesses;
- **Podium / Gradient Portrait** — wellness, health, premium personal services;
- **Technicolor** — beauty, lifestyle, fashion-forward verticals.

These are art-direction families, not separate brands.

## 9. Color direction

Locked at the directional level:

- warm Pearl / off-white base;
- Graphite / Anthracite / Obsidian dark base;
- Iris / violet as a premium intelligence accent;
- controlled blue / cobalt for technology and action;
- restrained sage / mint for positive states;
- warm sand neutral;
- amber and red reserved primarily for semantic or carefully controlled accents.

Exact production HEX tokens remain subject to final code-level contrast testing and brand-token normalization. Do not introduce random default SaaS/Tailwind colors without justification.

## 10. Typography direction

Typography should be:

- editorial enough to feel premium in marketing;
- highly readable in product UI;
- strong for large headlines;
- neutral enough for international scale;
- consistent across website, app, social and presentation systems.

Exact production font stack may be refined during implementation, but should preserve the approved premium-minimal character.

## 11. Flow waves

The dark Hermes Connect panel with the small logo and flowing digital waves is an explicitly approved visual reference.

Use waves as a recurring signature for:

- backgrounds;
- section transitions;
- loading / AI processing;
- presentations;
- social templates;
- launch videos;
- subtle dashboard moments;
- brand motion.

Rules:

- waves should be fine and elegant;
- avoid loud neon cyberpunk styling;
- do not reduce text contrast;
- motion should be slow and intelligent;
- use them to communicate flow, data and orchestration.

## 12. Website / product relationship

The website, mobile app, web app, presentations, social assets and future vertical modules must look like parts of the same Hermes Connect system.

Do not design the public website as a disconnected marketing theme.

## 13. Competitive design objective

The design should make a user moving from a legacy CRM or booking platform feel:

> Everything I need is here, but this is simpler, smarter, more modern and more powerful.

Hermes Connect should compete on more than feature parity. The experience should emphasize:

- AI orchestration;
- adaptive setup;
- marketing and growth;
- sales;
- operations;
- unified business intelligence.

## 14. Things to avoid

Avoid:

- generic trucking identity;
- heavy blue logistics clichés;
- crypto/Web3 visual similarity;
- robot-head AI logos;
- generic circuit brains;
- excessive glow;
- uncontrolled gradients;
- decorative glassmorphism everywhere;
- visual noise;
- showing many AI agents as separate products;
- restarting the brand from scratch without an approved reason.

## 15. Brand governance

All contributors must classify proposed changes as one of:

- `Refinement` — improves the approved system without changing its identity;
- `Extension` — adds a new component, vertical or use case using the approved system;
- `Experiment` — deliberately tests an alternative without replacing the approved system;
- `Rebrand Proposal` — changes the core identity and therefore requires explicit owner approval.

The default is **Refinement**, not Rebrand Proposal.
