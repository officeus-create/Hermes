# Hermes Connect — Owner Logo Decision

Date: **2026-08-31**
Status: **APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION / LAUNCH**
Owner: Vladimir / Hermes

## Decision

The original Hermes Connect **Option №02** from Brand Exploration V2 is selected as the production logo direction.

Source concept id: `continuous_loop`
Historical concept name: `Infinity Workflow Loop / Петля Непрерывности`

The selected visual is the simple horizontal continuous connected-flow / infinity-like loop. It is preferred over the current production Intelligent Knot and over later AI-generated reinterpretations.

## Implementation instruction

- Replace current Hermes Connect website/product-entry marks with Option №02.
- Do not wait for additional AI/designer comments before implementation.
- Do not revive or wholesale-merge the historical `feature/brand-exploration-v2` branch; productionize the approved mark from fresh `main`.
- Preserve the existing Pearl / Obsidian Hermes Connect environment, product positioning, flow-wave language and premium restrained design system.
- Later logo variants that introduced winged H marks, circuit nodes, orbital motifs, unrelated ribbon-H shapes or other new families are **not** approved replacements.

## Color boundary for this launch

This launch uses the core Hermes Connect blue / cobalt / violet spectrum.

The historical division-color behavior for Logistics / Marketing / Academy / IT is deliberately deferred to the next design phase. Do not block this launch on that experiment and do not randomly recolor the mark per route yet.

## Screenshot/design review instruction

Screenshots supplied by the owner may mix defects and positive visual references. Preserve strong existing sections and repair obvious inconsistencies instead of redesigning the entire site.

Current positive reference quality includes the strongest Hermes Connect, Technology, Academy and selected Marketing sections. The Logistics directory collapse shown in the 2026-08-31 screenshots is a defect and should be repaired as part of this implementation cycle.

## Governance impact

This owner decision supersedes older Brand System V1 language **only where that language says Möbius/infinity/continuous-loop alternatives are unapproved replacements**. The rest of the approved Hermes Connect system remains in force.
