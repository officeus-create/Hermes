## Expansion rule

Do not publish bulk city, state, equipment or thin semantic pages merely to increase URL count. New pages require a distinct search intent, useful first-party information, an owned conversion path, internal links, canonical/sitemap ownership, regression coverage and a measurement plan.

## Evidence rule

Never convert illustrative examples, private operational records, modelled directory data, unverified claims or AI-generated estimates into public proof. Label evidence class, source, date, scope and limitations.
