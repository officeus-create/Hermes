# Hermes AI Ecosystem — Current State

Last updated: 2026-08-06

## Objective

Build a shared, version-controlled knowledge and decision system where multiple AI assistants and humans can review the same context, contribute proposals, challenge assumptions and preserve the reasoning behind decisions.

## Active systems

- Hermes Logistics corporate website and commercial SEO pages.
- Hermes Connect product concept: AI logistics platform, CRM, dispatch, carrier/customer portals, tracking, pricing and automation.
- Logistics operations, carrier onboarding, recruiting and academy workflows.
- ProgressoPro marketing and sales systems.

## Current active design discussion

Hermes Connect brand and mobile application identity.

Current direction:

- Dark technology-oriented visual system aligned with the Hermes website.
- Blue/cyan/teal light effects used carefully, with strong contrast and readable mobile UI.
- Logo work initially explored the letter `H`, chain links and connected geometry.
- The strongest recent direction is a compact connected-network symbol suitable for an app icon.
- The current concepts are promising but not yet considered “wow” or approved.
- Further exploration should test both refined connected-H concepts and non-letter symbols based on routes, nodes, networks, logistics and an ecosystem.
