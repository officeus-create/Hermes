# IT Development Service and Pricing Rationale

Updated: 2026-07-13

## Positioning

Hermes IT Development builds business systems, not isolated screens. The public offer combines websites, CRM and operations control, AI-assisted workflows, automation, integrations, and decision visibility.

The Hermes corporate website is the first public case study. It was led by Vladimir and delivered through a coordinated workflow using ChatGPT / Digital CEO for product strategy, Codex for engineering and deployment, Claude for independent QA, and specialist AI tools for bounded research and review.

## Published Planning Ranges

| Package | Timeline | Planning range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital Presence System | 6-10 weeks | $12,000-$25,000 |
| CRM and Operations Control | 10-18 weeks | $25,000-$60,000 |
| AI Assistant and Workflow | 6-12 weeks | $15,000-$35,000 |
| Connected Business Platform | 16-28 weeks | $45,000-$120,000 |

Timelines deliberately include discovery, approvals, revisions, QA, launch, and handoff rather than extrapolating only from coding time. Final estimates require discovery and depend on integrations, migration, compliance, content, and third-party services.

## Market Benchmarks

- Clutch's July 2026 software development pricing guide reports U.S. development company rates around `$100-$149/hour` and reviewed custom software projects commonly in the `$10,000-$49,000` range: https://clutch.co/developers/pricing
- Clutch's U.S. web development overview reports materially higher ranges for fully custom websites and MVP web applications: https://clutch.co/us/web-developers
- TechRadar's 2025 website cost review places agency or custom-coded website work broadly around `$5,000-$30,000`, with additional functionality increasing cost: https://www.techradar.com/news/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-a-website

These references are directional market context, not promises or rate cards. Hermes planning ranges are intentionally broad until the operating workflow is understood.
