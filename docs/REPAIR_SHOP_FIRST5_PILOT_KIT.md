# Hermes Connect — First-5 Repair Shops Onboarding & Sales Pilot Kit

## Pilot Positioning Statement
> **"Hermes Connect helps independent truck and auto repair shops create a digital service profile, share one instant booking link or QR code with customers, receive scheduled appointments with vehicle details, and manage client history without subscription cost during our pilot phase."**

---

## A. 1-Page Pilot Offer
- **What You Get**: Custom online booking link, print-ready QR code for your counter, automated schedule slots, and digital customer CRM.
- **Cost During Pilot**: $0. No credit card required.
- **What We Ask**: 15 minutes to set up your profile, share your booking link with 3 real customers, and give us honest feedback on whether it saves your shop time.

---

## B. Owner Onboarding Checklist
- [ ] Step 1: Register specialist account (`email` + `password`).
- [ ] Step 2: Complete Shop Profile (`name`, `address`, `city`, `state`, `zip`, `phone`, `timezone`).
- [ ] Step 3: Add at least 2 common services with durations (e.g. "Brake Inspection — 30 min", "Oil Change & Inspection — 45 min").
- [ ] Step 4: Set weekly opening and closing hours.
- [ ] Step 5: Copy public booking link and download QR code.

---

## C. 5-Minute Setup Flow
1. Open `https://connect.hermeslogisticsus.com/services/hermes-connect/repair-shops/auth/?mode=register`
2. Enter shop details and click **Save Shop Profile**.
3. Under **Services**, click quick-add presets or type custom services.
4. Under **Scheduling**, toggle open days and set operating hours.
5. Click **Copy Link** / **Open Link** to verify your live customer booking page.

---

## D. QR & Sharing Instructions
- **Digital**: Add your booking URL to your Google Business Profile, Facebook page, or email signature.
- **Counter / Front Desk**: Print the QR code generated in your workspace and place it at your service counter for drop-in clients.
- **Text / Phone**: Text your booking link directly to customers calling to schedule non-emergency service.

---

## E. First-Booking Verification Checklist
- [ ] Customer opens booking link.
- [ ] Customer selects service, date, and available time slot.
- [ ] Customer enters vehicle year, make, model, VIN/mileage, and contact info.
- [ ] Appointment appears immediately in Owner Workspace inbox.
- [ ] Owner updates status from `pending` to `confirmed` or `completed`.

---

## F. 7-Day Follow-up Script
> *"Hi [Owner Name], this is [Your Name] from Hermes Connect. It's been 7 days since we set up your booking link. I wanted to check in: Have you had a chance to share the link or QR code with any customers? Did any appointments come through your inbox, and how did the vehicle information look to your service tech?"*

---

## G. Willingness-To-Pay Interview Framework
1. **Time Saved**: *"What part of the booking or customer lookup saved your front desk the most time this week?"*
2. **Business Impact**: *"Did the booking link help you capture any appointment after hours or when your phone line was busy?"*
3. **Product Dependency**: *"If Hermes Connect disappeared tomorrow, what part would you miss the most?"*
4. **Feature Priority**: *"Which upcoming feature (automated SMS reminders, multi-tech calendar, fleet invoicing) would be most valuable to your shop?"*
5. **Value Assessment**: *"What monthly price would feel fair for a system that handles your online booking and customer history?"*
