# Hermes Connect — Approved Brand Direction v1
**Status**: APPROVED & LOCKED
**Project**: Hermes Connect (AI Operating System for Business)
**Authority**: CEO & Lead Product Designer

---

## 1. General Positioning & Core Identity
- **Positioning**: **Hermes Connect = AI Operating System for Business**
- **Core Mission**: Not just a CRM, but an intelligent OS that helps manage, automate, and scale small-to-enterprise businesses 24/7.
- **Hero Headline**: **"Run your business with AI"**
- **Core Narrative**: *"Everything you need to manage, grow and scale your business. AI agents working 24/7 so you can focus on what matters."*

---

## 2. Hero Structure & Visual Storytelling
- **Light Pearl Storytelling Canvas**: Hero marketing & landing section uses a warm Pearl / off-white (`#F7F6F3`) background.
- **Live Dark Operating Dashboard**: Adjacent to the hero text, displaying the live dark command center (`#0B0D12`).
- **3D Knot Brand Art Asset**: A translucent glass 3D interconnected loop sculpture at the center of the hero canvas.
- **5-Step Automated Lead Stream**:
  1. *New Lead* (e.g. Website / Instagram)
  2. *AI Replied / Receptionist Answered*
  3. *Booking Confirmed*
  4. *Payment Received*
  5. *Review Requested / Received*

---

## 3. Logo Mark & Signature Digital Flow Waves
- **Logo Mark**: Small interconnected loop / continuous connection / intelligent knot mark on top left.
- **Typography**: Inter / SF Pro Display with clean optical tracking.
- **Digital Flow Waves**: Subtle flow lines under brand cards and assets representing signal, flow, system orchestration, and connected movement.

---

## 4. Master Color System
- **Pearl Light Canvas**: `#F7F6F3` (Primary Light Surface)
- **Anthracite Cosmic Dark**: `#0B0D12` (Primary Dark Operating Dashboard & Command Center)
- **Signature Accents**:
  - **Iris / Soft Violet**: `#7C5CFF`
  - **Ocean Blue**: `#5AC8FA`
  - **Sage Mint**: `#7FD1B6`
  - **Graphite Neutral**: `#1C1E26`
  - **Sand Warm Neutral**: `#F3E9DC`

---

## 5. Niche Card Mood Directions (4 Industry Styles)
1. **Monochrome**: Professional Services / Consulting / Agencies (high-contrast black & white photography).
2. **Color-block**: Real Estate / Modern Service Business (architectural pastel structures & spatial depth).
3. **Podium**: Wellness / Health / Premium Service (soft gradient podium presentation).
4. **Technicolor**: Beauty / Lifestyle / Fashion-forward niches (vibrant direct studio light).

---

## 6. UI Language & Component Contracts
- **UI Language**: Clean, spacious premium SaaS with sub-pixel glass overlays and subtle borders.
- **Primary Buttons**: Full-pill (`border-radius: 9999px`), primary dark (`#0B0D12`) or vibrant gradient.
- **Secondary Buttons**: Light glass pill (`border-radius: 9999px`) with subtle borders.
- **Bento Cards**: `8px` corner radii with subtle glass stroke and hover elevational lift.
- **Hermes Intelligence**: Unified single point of AI orchestration (avoids fragmented multi-bot cognitive overload).

---

## 7. Operational Guidelines for All AI Agents & Designers
This document is the **Canonical Approved Hermes Connect Brand Direction v1**.
- **DO NOT** redesign from scratch or switch brand worlds.
- **DO NOT** change the core palette (Pearl + Anthracite + Iris/Ocean/Sage).
- Refinements are permitted ONLY as micro-improvements to geometry, motion, typography, mobile responsiveness, and component detailing.
