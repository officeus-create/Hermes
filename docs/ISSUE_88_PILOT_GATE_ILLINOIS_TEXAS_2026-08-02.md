# Issue #88 pilot gate: Illinois and Texas

Reviewed: 2026-08-02

## Purpose

This is a clean-room research gate for the first two possible non-Wisconsin logistics SEO pilots. It does **not** authorize publication, create a route, modify a sitemap, or claim current Hermes demand, routes, capacity, pricing, offices, yards, customers, or completed transportation history.

Current private load-board observations and Shipment History are excluded. Public search observations are research inputs only.

## Evidence model

A proposed page is reviewed on eight dimensions: distinct intent, dated demand, competition opportunity, Hermes service fit, unique local value, conversion path, internal-link role, and claim safety.

Public freight or population data provides market context only. It does not prove Hermes demand, live routes, load availability, rates, or capacity.

## Illinois

### Public evidence

The Illinois Department of Transportation describes Illinois as a major multimodal freight hub. Its 2023 freight-plan summary reports approximately 1.6 billion tons and $2.5 trillion in freight flowing into, out of, and through the state in 2019, with Illinois ranked third by freight value and fourth by tonnage.

Official source:

- https://idot.illinois.gov/programs-and-projects/rail-and-freight/illinois-state-freight-plan.html

The U.S. Census Bureau estimates Illinois population at 12,719,141 on July 1, 2025.

Official source:

- https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/IL

### Public competition observations

Public results show Illinois-based pages using several models: national vehicle shipping, direct-carrier corridor positioning, equipment-specific carrier pages, and dealer/auction/broker-facing service pages.

Examples reviewed for page type and intent only:

- https://www.vehiclehaul.com/
- https://ariessl.com/
- https://moosefreight.com/
- https://deltransgroup.com/
- https://miliva.net/

Hermes must not imitate facility, fleet, corridor, insurance, availability, partner, or performance claims without its own approved evidence.

### Preferred candidate intent

`Illinois vehicle transport coordination for dealers and carriers`

The candidate must support rather than compete with:

- `/logistics/car-hauling-dispatch/`
- `/logistics/dealer-vehicle-transportation/`
- `/load-board/`

Potential unique value, subject to approval:

- explain how Illinois freight geography affects planning questions;
- separate dealer/shipper intake from carrier dispatch intake;
- link auction-pickup and capacity-checklist resources;
- route users to the correct reviewed intake;
- state clearly that loads, rates, lanes, response time, and capacity are not guaranteed.

## Texas

### Public evidence

TxDOT states that reliable movement of goods to, from, and within Texas is essential to the state economy. TxDOT is developing the 2027 Texas Freight Mobility Plan as an update to the 2023 plan, with freight-network, supply-chain, facility, bottleneck, and operations analysis scheduled through 2026–2027.

Official sources:

- https://www.txdot.gov/projects/planning/freight-planning/texas-freight-mobility-plan.html
- https://www.txdot.gov/government/partnerships/freight-planning/texas-freight-mobility-plan.html

The U.S. Census Bureau estimates Texas population at 31,709,821 on July 1, 2025, up 8.8% from the 2020 estimate base.

Official source:

- https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/TX

### Public competition observations

Public results include Texas-based auto-hauling carriers, nationwide providers with Texas positioning, automotive broker pages focused on dealers/auctions/fleets, and regional vehicle-transport service pages.

Examples reviewed for intent and page type only:

- https://texasautohaul.com/
- https://logistixintel.com/
- https://www.rgvautotransport.com/our-services/
- https://openwaylogistics.com/

Hermes must not reproduce facility, authority, pickup-timing, route, carrier-verification, equipment, or performance claims without current approved evidence.

### Preferred candidate intent

`Texas vehicle transport coordination for dealers and carriers`

The page must not claim a Texas office, terminal, yard, live capacity, or named lane. It should support:

- `/logistics/car-hauling-dispatch/`
- `/logistics/dealer-vehicle-transportation/`
- `/load-board/`

Potential unique value, subject to approval:

- link auction-pickup and capacity-planning resources;
- explain that route, timing, price, load availability, and capacity require current human review;

## Draft page brief template

Use only after a candidate reaches at least 7/10.

### Title

`[State] Vehicle Transport Coordination | Hermes Logistics`

### H1

`Vehicle transport coordination for [State] dealers and carriers`

### Required sections

1. Audience split: dealer/shipper versus carrier.
2. What Hermes reviews before responding.
3. Equipment and request-scope categories without availability claims.
4. Auction/dealer pickup planning resources.
5. Current-review boundary for timing, rate, route, and capacity.
6. Links to the national commercial owner and reviewed intake.
7. State-specific public context with dated citations.
8. FAQ based on actual query evidence.

### Allowed schema

- `WebPage`;
- `BreadcrumbList`;
- `Service` only when visible content and approved business facts support it;
- `FAQPage` only when every question and answer is visible.

Do not add `LocalBusiness`, `Offer`, `Review`, `AggregateRating`, route, live inventory, or capacity schema.

## Cannibalization and doorway assessment

Risk is currently **medium to high** because existing national pages already own the commercial conversion intent. Stop implementation if:

- title/H1 differs only by replacing a state name;
- the same body can be reused for another state;
- local value relies only on population or generic freight statistics;
- the page competes with `/logistics/car-hauling-dispatch/` or `/logistics/dealer-vehicle-transportation/` for the same primary query;
- there is no distinct internal-link role;
- Search Console selects another canonical owner.
