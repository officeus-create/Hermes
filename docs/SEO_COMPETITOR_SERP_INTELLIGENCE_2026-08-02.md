### Repeated SERP patterns

- Owner-operator and small-fleet wording.
- High-paying-load and revenue-growth claims.
- 24/7 support claims.
- Dedicated dispatcher positioning.
- Load finding, negotiation, broker setup, paperwork, invoicing.
- Equipment selectors and short onboarding forms.
- New-authority messaging.
- Pricing or percentage-of-gross visibility on some sites.
- Weak differentiation beyond speed, rates, and support availability.

### Hermes differentiation

- Carrier approves every load before booking.
- Qualification before operational promises.
- MC/USDOT, authority, insurance, equipment, capacity, home area, lanes, and availability reviewed together.
- One-car through multi-car equipment context without doorway pages.
- Privacy-safe measured funnel: commercial CTA → intake start → preview ready → explicit handoff.
- No guaranteed load, rate, lane, mileage, utilization, revenue, or response-time claims.
- Free operational checklists that carriers can use even before becoming clients.

## 2. Website-development SERP strategy

The market is split between low-cost builders, template agencies, custom development agencies, conversion/SEO-led partners, ecommerce specialists, and local web-design providers.

Hermes should compete on website architecture tied to business goals, search-ready technical foundations, measurable inquiry paths, analytics, integrations, staged releases, QA, and ongoing improvement—not only design or development hours.

### Priority comparison and guide opportunities

1. Website builder vs custom website development.
2. New website vs website redesign decision guide.
3. Website development with SEO foundation vs design-only build.
4. What a small-business website project brief should include.

6. How to preserve SEO during a redesign.
7. Website development cost drivers without false fixed-price claims.
8. What to prepare before hiring a web-development company.
9. Astro/static website vs WordPress for service businesses.
10. Website + CRM + automation planning guide.

Canonical commercial owners: `/services/website-development/` and `/services/website-redesign/`. Do not create a competing combined service page until Search Console demand and cannibalization evidence justify it.

## 5. Outreach categories and rules

Target car-hauling associations/communities, owner-operator media and podcasts, auto-auction/dealer/insurance/factoring/compliance/equipment partners, small-business media, web/SEO podcasts, logistics technology publications, chambers and legitimate directories, and real existing partners.
