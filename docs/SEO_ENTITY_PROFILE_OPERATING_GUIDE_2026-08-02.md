# SEO Entity and Profile Operating Guide

Reviewed: 2026-08-02

## Objective

Create one verifiable public identity for each real Hermes business direction, correct conflicting third-party information, and build legitimate free/basic profiles that can produce discovery, branded search, reviews, referrals, and qualified inquiries.

This guide does not declare an address, phone, legal officer, FMCSA identifier, storefront, service area, or current employee relationship unless the owner verifies it from an authoritative or owner-controlled source.

## 1. Entity model

Do not collapse every Hermes direction into one local business listing.

### Hermes Logistics LLC

Potential public entity for:

- logistics services;
- dispatch and carrier-support services;
- vehicle transportation coordination;
- qualifying real U.S. location or service-area profiles;
- official legal and FMCSA relationships when verified.

### Hermes IT Development

A service department or approved service brand for:

- website development;
- CRM and automation;
- integrations and operational systems;
- public GitHub/case-study presence;
- B2B directories such as Clutch when the brand relationship is clear.

### ProgressoPro / Marketing & SEO

A service direction or approved service brand for:

- SEO;
- website growth;
- social and content systems;
- B2B service profiles and cases.

### Hermes Business Academy

A distinct education/training direction. It must not inherit logistics licenses, addresses, testimonials, employment claims, or local profiles automatically.

The website Organization schema may describe the ecosystem and departments. Local profiles, legal records, reviews, licenses, and sameAs links must match the specific real entity they represent.

## 2. Canonical identity record

Before claiming or correcting profiles, create an owner-controlled private record with:

- exact legal name;
- approved public brand name;
- jurisdiction and registration status;
- registered address kept private unless appropriate;
- real customer-facing address or eligible service-area model;
- approved public phone;
- approved public email;
- approved website/domain;
- categories and services;
- hours and response model;
- leadership and authorized profile managers;
- verified FMCSA relationship where relevant;
- approved logo and short/long descriptions;
- controlled profile URLs;
- verification dates and evidence owner.

Do not put private registration documents, IDs, passwords, recovery codes, or unredacted customer records in the public repository.

## 3. Platform decisions

### Google Business Profile

Create or claim a profile only when the represented business makes in-person contact with customers during stated hours through a real storefront or an eligible service-area model. Online-only businesses and virtual offices are not eligible. A service-area business must accurately represent the real operating base and service area; an address should not be displayed when customers are not served there.

Decision:

- Hermes Logistics: `ELIGIBILITY_REQUIRED` until the real customer-facing or field-service model is documented.
- Website Development and SEO: `DO_NOT_CREATE_AS_ONLINE_ONLY_LOCAL_PROFILES` unless a genuine in-person business model is verified.
- Never create city profiles from virtual offices or mailboxes.

Official guidance:

- https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481

### Apple Business Connect

Apple Business Connect is a free company/brand tool. It can support verified brand identity and Apple Mail branding even when no physical Maps place is available. Maps place cards still require accurate, verified location information.

Decision:

- verify the approved company/brand identity;
- add approved logo/domain and branded-mail assets;
- add or claim a Maps place only for a verified real location;
- keep logistics, IT, marketing, and academy relationships explicit.

Official guidance:

- https://businessconnect.apple.com/
- https://businessconnect.apple.com/promote/assets/getting-started.pdf

### Bing Places

Bing Places permits businesses to add or claim listings for free. Use it only after canonical NAP and the real eligible business model are confirmed.

Official source:

- https://www.bingplaces.com/

### Yelp Business

A Yelp Business Page can be added or claimed for free. It should represent a real business/service model with accurate identity, categories, contact routes, and service facts. Do not purchase, trade, or manufacture reviews.

Official source:

- https://business.yelp.com/products/business-page/

### Clutch and The Manifest

Clutch offers a free Basic company profile. A service provider can publish approved company information, services, cases, and real reviews. Paid verification or promotion is optional and must not be treated as required SEO spend. The Manifest presence may follow from Clutch.

Decision:

- first candidate: approved Hermes IT Development / Marketing & SEO service-provider identity;
- use real cases only;
- obtain explicit client permission before submitting references or reviews;
- do not invent clients, project values, results, employees, awards, or locations.

Official sources:

- https://clutch.co/get-listed
- https://clutch.co/service-providers

## 4. Conflicting listings

Public search currently surfaces multiple same-name or third-party records. Some appear to describe Hermes; others are unrelated companies with similar names. At least two business directories appear to show conflicting identity/contact information for a Hermes Logistics LLC listing. Employment profiles may also show older scale or operating claims.

Use this workflow:

1. Capture source, date, listing URL, visible name, and conflict category.
2. Do not copy the data into website schema.
3. Compare it with the private canonical identity record.
4. Confirm that the listing actually represents this Hermes entity.
5. Request correction, claim, or suppression through the platform's official process.
6. Record submission date, case number, status, and final verified values in the private operating record.
7. Update public sameAs/schema only after control and accuracy are confirmed.

Do not publicly accuse a directory or individual of publishing false data when the company identity has not yet been verified.

## 5. Reviews and reputation

Allowed:

- request an honest review after real completed work;
- provide direct links to the appropriate controlled profile;
- respond to every genuine review professionally;
- obtain permission before turning a review into a case study;
- record review source, date, project type, permission, and response status.

Prohibited:

- buying reviews;
- employee/friend reviews presented as clients;
- review swaps;
- incentives conditioned on a positive review;
- copied reviews across profiles;
- invented reviewer names, companies, projects, numbers, or outcomes;
- creating a profile solely to host fake location reviews.

## 6. sameAs and schema policy

Add a profile to `sameAs` only when:

- the profile is controlled or verified;
- it represents the same legal/brand entity as the schema node;
- name, website, description, phone/address where used, and business relationship are consistent;
- the profile is public and stable;
- the owner approves publication.

Do not use:

- employee resumes as Organization sameAs;
- unrelated same-name companies;
- unclaimed directories with conflicting data;
- search-result URLs;
- temporary campaign/social URLs;
- virtual-office profiles;
- FMCSA records that belong to another operating entity.

## 7. Execution order

### P0 — identity truth

1. Verify legal name and current registration.
2. Verify public phone, email, domain, and real service/location model.
3. Verify any FMCSA relationship.
4. Review Google account security and authorized profile managers.
5. Correct or remove unsupported claims on owner-controlled employment/company profiles.
6. Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

### P1 — free controlled profiles

1. LinkedIn company page.
2. Apple Business Connect brand.
3. Bing Places when eligible.
4. Yelp page when eligible.
5. Clutch Basic for verified B2B service identity.
6. GitHub organization/profile.
7. Relevant logistics associations/directories after editorial-quality review.

### P2 — reputation and partnerships

1. review-request workflow;
2. client-approved cases;
3. partner profiles;
4. chamber or association opportunities;
5. unlinked-mention reclamation;
6. interview/podcast/expert-comment outreach.

## 8. KPI

Track monthly:

- controlled profiles verified;
- conflicting listings corrected;
- unresolved entity conflicts;
- real reviews requested/received/responded to;
- referral visits by profile;
- profile CTA/contact activity;
- branded-search impressions and clicks;
- unlinked mentions reclaimed;
- qualified inquiries and signed revenue attributed to profiles;
- suspensions, duplicate listings, or policy warnings.

A profile is not successful merely because it exists. It must be accurate, controlled, policy-compliant, discoverable, and connected to a qualified inquiry path.
