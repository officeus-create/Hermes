## What Hermes actually looks like today

Hermes is a **public Astro/Cloudflare website plus a growing product/runtime portfolio**, governed by HOS/HUEG and GitHub evidence. The operational centre is not yet a single One Brain runtime.

1. **Logistics:** dispatch, carrier onboarding/contract-review, vehicle transport and resource/Load Board flows; public routes are reachable. Legal signature and true operational outcomes remain gated.
2. **Marketing / SEO / GEO:** substantial public content, page-owner, evidence and measurement contracts; the limiting factor is verified platform/funnel evidence, not a shortage of pages.
3. **Hermes Connect:** canonical service/product family with Repair Shops as its active vertical, plus Academy workflows and demos. The P0 legacy-bridge containment gate prevents real pilot outreach/activation from being promoted.
4. **Academy / recruiting:** one identity, lesson, submission, reviewer, support and progression architecture exists. Human eligibility, reviewer and commercial-entitlement decisions remain necessary.
5. **IT / Website Development:** a commercial service and shared implementation capability, not yet a separately evidenced internal work-management system.
6. **Sales, carrier operations and finance:** represented through lead receiver, qualified-inquiry and manual-close contracts. Their private system of record and outcome data were not inspected.
7. **AI infrastructure:** multiple local AI runtimes coexist. Hermes Codex/FCC is isolated and governed, but provider fallback is still unproven and no machine-readable corporate task/agent/event registry exists.

## Top 10 corporate findings

1. **P0 security first:** the documented legacy bridge has an arbitrary-command/public-write risk. No pilot, AI workforce or new connector should bypass its containment gate.
2. **The company has a repository-level brain design but not yet a shared operational graph.** HOS/HUEG is strong governance; it is not a knowledge/event/task system by itself.
3. **Knowledge is fragmented across four full code/product trees, private departmental folders, AI runtimes, GitHub, and future provider surfaces.** Preserve first; classify before merge/archive/delete.
4. **Repository freshness is a real corporate-control defect.** A project-state snapshot pre-dates newer Connect security/runtime decisions, which can cause agents to act on stale reality.
5. **The public surface is coherent at its core.** Eight representative key pages returned HTTP 200 with matching canonicals; the Connect compatibility host redirects to the canonical Connect page. This does not validate protected/business flows.
6. **The currently active GitHub work is limited and identifiable:** six open PRs with clean CI, distinct file ownership and no reason for a parallel One Brain control-centre implementation. PR #865 is the relevant existing control-surface owner.
7. **Measurement breaks between interaction and commercial outcome.** Code contracts exist for consent-safe events, but this audit cannot verify GA4/GSC/Bing or receiver→qualified→won reconciliation.
8. **Academy has an unusually strong reusable foundation.** Do not build another identity, enrollment, submission, review, or progression stack; connect verified course assets to the existing one.
9. **AI tools must share evidence, not raw conversation/session/auth state.** A One Brain must use sanitized, provenance-tagged handoffs and registries rather than copy private local AI memories.
10. **Most apparent backlog is not implementation-ready.** It is already completed, owned by a current writer, needs fresh measurement, or is blocked by a legitimate owner/access/legal/commercial gate.
