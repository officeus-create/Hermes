# 1. US Logistics Training

## Recommended route

`/academy/logistics-training/`

## Search intent

Commercial + career-transition intent from people looking for practical training in US trucking and logistics, especially dispatcher, carrier sales, driver recruiting, shipper/dealer communication, and broker support roles.

## Primary audience

- Ukrainians and Russian-speaking people worldwide with practical English;
- beginners changing careers;
- salespeople entering logistics;
- candidates seeking remote US-facing work;
- people considering dispatcher, carrier sales, driver recruiter, shipper/dealer manager, or broker assistant roles.

## Primary keyword themes

- US logistics training
- truck dispatcher training
- freight dispatcher course
- logistics training for beginners
- remote logistics training
- carrier sales training
- driver recruiter training
- how to work in US trucking remotely

## Proposed SEO title

US Logistics Training for Remote Careers | Hermes Academy

## Proposed meta description

Learn practical US logistics workflows, communication, sales, dispatch, recruiting, and carrier operations with a structured path from training to demonstrated work readiness.

## H1

Practical US Logistics Training for Remote Careers

## Hero promise

Learn how US trucking operations work, practise the communication and decision-making required in real roles, and build evidence of readiness for the next step.

Do not promise guaranteed employment or income.

## Required sections

1. Who this program is for
2. How US logistics works
3. Role paths inside the program
4. Practical skills taught
5. English and schedule expectations
6. Practice and feedback model
7. From training to measurable readiness
8. Possible next roles after demonstrated results
9. FAQ
10. Application CTA

## Role paths to show

- Logistics Dispatcher
- Carrier Sales Representative
- Driver Recruiter
- Shipper/Dealer Communication
- Broker Assistant
- Operations Support

## Skills to cover

- US trucking ecosystem and terminology;
- load boards and freight workflows at a conceptual level;
- carrier onboarding and MC/DOT fundamentals;
- scripts, SPIN questions, objections, and follow-up;
- driver and carrier communication;
- rate, lane, deadhead, RPM, and basic load evaluation;
- car hauling, hotshot, box truck, cargo van, power only, reefer, van, flatbed, and step-deck context;
- call review and reporting;
- handoff, escalation, and team coordination.

## Trust blocks

Use only verified statements. Possible evidence modules:

- sample workflow or training checklist;
- anonymized practice assignment;
- mentor review process;
- realistic role comparison;
- dated case context where approved.

# Cross-page implementation rules

## Canonical and cannibalization

Each program page must own one primary search intent. Do not create overlapping variants such as multiple near-identical dispatcher or SMM pages before Search Console data justifies expansion.

## Structured data

Use only schema types that match visible content. Likely candidates:

- `Course` when the page presents a real, current training program;
- `FAQPage` only when FAQs are visible;
- `BreadcrumbList` for all program and role pages;
- `Organization` remains sitewide rather than duplicated inconsistently.

## Conversion tracking

Recommended GA4 events:

- `academy_program_view`
- `academy_program_select`
- `academy_apply_start`
- `academy_apply_complete`
- `academy_consultation_start`
- `academy_role_select`
- `academy_language_select`

Do not send sensitive form content to analytics.

## Claims and evidence

Before publication, verify:

- current program duration;
- pricing or scholarship rules;
- mentor availability;
- schedule expectations;
- active hiring paths;
- employment language;
- quantitative case claims;
- geographic availability.

## Definition of done

A page is not complete until:

- intent and route are unique;
- title, description, H1, and canonical are present;
- visible content supports any schema;
- claims are approved;
- internal links are implemented;
- CTA uses the existing privacy-safe lead architecture;
- desktop and mobile are tested;
- build, unit, and Playwright tests pass;
- the work is recorded in `docs/AI_HANDOFF.md`.
