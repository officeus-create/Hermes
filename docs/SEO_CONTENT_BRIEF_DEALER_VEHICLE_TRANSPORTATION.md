### 2. Common dealer transportation requests

Use substantive request categories rather than thin keyword blocks.

#### Dealer-to-dealer transfers

Move vehicles between dealership locations, partner dealerships, storage yards, reconditioning facilities, and regional inventory hubs.

#### Auction-to-dealer transportation

Coordinate pickup from qualified auctions after release details, gate procedures, buyer information, operability, and deadlines are confirmed.

#### Port-to-dealer or port-to-storage movement

Handle qualified port-related requests after release status, customs, terminal access, storage or demurrage exposure, TWIC requirements where applicable, and exact pickup procedures are confirmed.

#### Multi-vehicle inventory movement

Qualify the number of vehicles, dimensions, operability, origin access, delivery access, loading sequence, deadlines, and suitable equipment.

#### Customer delivery

Support qualified dealership-to-customer requests after final destination access, timing, contact information, vehicle condition, and handoff requirements are confirmed.

#### Luxury, classic, and specialty vehicles

Clarify whether enclosed transportation, soft straps, lift-gate capability, low-clearance handling, non-stacking, or other special handling may be required.

### 3. Open versus enclosed transportation

#### Open transport

Suitable for many standard dealership and inventory moves, subject to vehicle condition, route, timing, and carrier availability.

#### Enclosed transport

May be appropriate for luxury, exotic, classic, collectible, high-value, low-clearance, restored, or weather-sensitive vehicles.

Do not state that enclosed transport is always safer or automatically available. Explain the operational differences and qualification requirements.

### 5. How the process works

#### Step 1 — Request review

Hermes reviews the shipment details and identifies missing operational information.

#### Step 2 — Route and equipment qualification

The team evaluates origin, destination, vehicle count, operability, equipment need, access, timing, and special handling.

#### Step 3 — Carrier verification

Any proposed carrier should be checked for relevant authority, insurance, equipment, communication reliability, operational fit, and current capacity.

#### Step 4 — Rate and terms confirmation

The customer reviews the proposed rate, scope, timing expectations, and any known accessorial conditions before the move proceeds.

#### Step 5 — Pickup and delivery coordination

Hermes coordinates the operational handoff, required documents, pickup communication, delivery updates, and exception management within the confirmed service scope.

## FAQ

### Do you transport one vehicle or multiple vehicles?

Hermes can review requests for one vehicle or multiple units. Acceptance depends on vehicle details, route, timing, equipment requirements, access, and available qualified carrier capacity.

### Can you move vehicles between dealerships?

Yes, dealer-to-dealer and dealer-group transfers can be reviewed after pickup, delivery, vehicle count, readiness, access, timing, and handling requirements are confirmed.

### Can you pick up vehicles from auctions?

Auction requests can be reviewed after release status, buyer information, gate procedure, vehicle condition, operability, storage exposure, and pickup deadline are confirmed.

### Do you provide open and enclosed transportation?

Hermes can review requests for open or enclosed transportation. The appropriate option depends on the vehicle, customer preference, handling requirements, budget, route, timing, and carrier availability.

### Can you transport inoperable vehicles?

Potentially. The request must specify why the vehicle is inoperable, whether it rolls, steers, and brakes, and what loading equipment or access may be required.

### Can you handle luxury or classic vehicles?

Hermes can review luxury, classic, exotic, collectible, restored, and specialty-vehicle requests. Carrier experience, equipment, insurance, access, and handling requirements must be verified.

### Can you move vehicles from ports?

Qualified port-related requests can be reviewed after release status, customs, terminal procedure, pickup access, deadlines, storage exposure, and any TWIC or escort requirements are confirmed.

### Do you guarantee pickup or delivery dates?

No absolute guarantee should be stated. Timing depends on confirmed scope, readiness, access, weather, traffic, carrier capacity, and other operating conditions. Any committed service terms must be documented for the specific move.

### How is pricing determined?

Pricing may depend on route, distance, vehicle count, operability, equipment type, open or enclosed service, timing, access, market conditions, fuel, tolls, storage, and other accessorial requirements.

### Can dealerships arrange recurring transportation?

Yes, repeat lanes can be reviewed and documented. Consistency depends on verified demand, schedule, route, equipment, service requirements, and qualified capacity.

### How are carriers verified?

Verification may include MC/DOT, authority, insurance, equipment, safety context, service history, communication, route fit, and special-access capability as applicable.

### Does Hermes own the transporting trucks?

The page should accurately describe the current operating model. Do not imply asset ownership unless verified. State that Hermes coordinates qualified transportation requests with suitable carrier capacity within the confirmed service scope.
