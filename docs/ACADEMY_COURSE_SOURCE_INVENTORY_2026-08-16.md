## Historical practical training model to preserve when productizing

Existing Hermes training practice has used a practical progression rather than passive reading: orientation/readiness, awareness, understanding, exercises/application, feedback/correction, and a human progression decision. Prior training work has also used calls, recordings, scripts/scenarios, document review, operating assignments, deadlines, reporting, and mentor/reviewer feedback where the cohort format supported them.

## Productization rule

A specialization becomes a sellable Academy course only when all of the following are explicit:

1. learner outcome;
2. target learner/readiness;
3. modules;
4. lessons/resources;
5. practical assignments;
6. review/feedback mechanism;
7. progression/completion rule;
8. access model;
9. price/commercial terms;
10. mobile/desktop learning path;
11. support route;
12. measurement for enrollment, activation, progress, completion, conversion, and retention.
