# Hermes Business Academy — Program and Market Qualification Gates

Purpose: limit Academy growth to two programs, define who may be targeted, separate paid enrollment from free practice, and prevent unsupported pricing, employment, and income claims.

## 1. Approved program families

Only two public program families are in scope for this sprint:

1. U.S. Logistics Program.
2. Marketing Program.

Additional disciplines require a separate owner-approved scope and evidence review.

## 2. U.S. Logistics Program scope

Potential curriculum categories, subject to final published syllabus:

- U.S. logistics market orientation;
- carrier, owner-operator, broker, shipper, and dealer roles;
- car hauling and relevant equipment categories;
- dispatch workflow;
- load qualification;
- communication and negotiation practice;
- broker setup and documentation;
- BOL, POD, rate confirmation, W-9, COI, MC, and DOT terminology;
- pickup/delivery coordination;
- reporting and CRM discipline;
- carrier sales and relationship development;
- dealer/shipper outreach;
- ethics, claim boundaries, and data privacy;
- supervised practice where available.

The program does not issue FMCSA authority, insurance, professional licensing, guaranteed employment, guaranteed clients, guaranteed loads, or guaranteed income.

## 3. Marketing Program scope

Potential curriculum categories, subject to final syllabus:

- business and audience research;
- positioning and offer structure;
- website planning;
- SEO and Local SEO;
- content strategy;
- social media marketing;
- Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn where relevant;
- lead generation and qualification;
- sales-process coordination;
- CRM and analytics basics;
- AI-assisted research and content workflows;
- client communication;
- portfolio and practical work boundaries.

The program does not guarantee followers, views, rankings, leads, clients, employment, or income.

## 4. Paid-course audience rules

Paid enrollment may be researched for adults who:

- prefer Russian or Ukrainian for instruction/coordination;
- live in an eligible country;
- can legally pay and receive the service;
- have stable internet and suitable equipment;
- can meet the published schedule;
- understand the required English level for the logistics program;
- accept written program, payment, refund, conduct, and privacy terms;
- are not promised a job, income, client volume, or business result.

Public targeting must be based on language, learning need, eligibility, and market fit—not nationality, ethnicity, or assumed wealth.

## 5. Free practice / scholarship track

Free practice is a separate selective track and must not be represented as the paid program at no cost.

Possible criteria:

- language ability relevant to a target market;
- reliable internet and equipment;
- schedule availability;
- completion of assigned exercises;
- communication discipline;
- ability to produce practical work;
- willingness to follow confidentiality and data rules;
- market knowledge useful to an approved Hermes project;
- acceptance that participation does not guarantee hiring, partnership, clients, or payment.

Every free-practice offer must state:

- duration or review period;
- weekly expectations;
- learning/practice scope;
- whether any real customer/carrier data is involved;
- supervision and access boundaries;
- removal/inactivity rules;
- no-employment and no-income guarantee.

## 6. Pricing publication gate

The discussed pricing concepts—`$999 for three months`, `$400 monthly`, and `from $600 monthly for corporate/employee training or consultation`—remain `OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE` until every fact below is approved in writing.

Required before publication:

- exact program name;
- start/end dates or rolling-enrollment rule;
- lesson/practice hours;
- live versus recorded format;
- instructor/mentor roles;
- included materials;
- support channels;
- assessment method;
- certificate wording, if any;
- refund/cancellation policy;
- missed-session policy;
- payment schedule;
- taxes/fees;
- contract entity and invoicing;
- country eligibility;
- privacy terms;
- minimum English requirement;
- corporate package deliverables;
- consultation versus training distinction;
- employment and outcome disclaimer.

Until complete, public pages may say `Program details and pricing will be published for each enrollment period.`

## 7. Logistics-course market research brief

Research objective: identify eligible markets where adults have strong enough English, reliable internet, interest in U.S. logistics, and a meaningful economic reason to pursue remote professional skills.

Do not target solely because a country has a low average salary. Required evidence:

- current English-proficiency or practical business-English indicators;
- internet reliability and access;
- payment availability;
- sanctions/compliance eligibility;
- remote-work or logistics-learning demand;
- ability to attend the required time zone;
- weak competition for the exact program/language query;
- local cultural and consumer-protection considerations.

Market status:

- `eligible_for_research`;
- `requires_compliance_review`;
- `not_currently_eligible`.

No country is approved in this document without dated external research.

## 8. Marketing-course market research brief

Primary audience: Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking adults, business owners, employees, and career changers in eligible international markets.

Research intent families:

- practical digital marketing course in Russian/Ukrainian;
- website and SEO training;
- social media marketing training;
- international/U.S. market marketing skills;
- marketing training for business owners;
- corporate employee marketing training.

A city/country page requires language demand, payment/compliance approval, real search intent, weak competition, unique local value, and a 7/10 publication score.

## 9. Course eligibility and internet readiness

Minimum technical readiness:

- stable broadband suitable for calls and screen sharing;
- computer capable of browser, documents, CRM, and communication tools;
- working microphone and headphones;
- backup mobile internet where practical;
- access to approved communication platforms;
- ability to protect accounts and use multi-factor authentication;
- no sharing of passwords or client credentials in training groups.

Readiness test may include:

- speed/latency check;
- microphone test;
- screen-sharing test;
- document upload/download;
- time-zone confirmation;
- short attendance trial.

## 10. English-level screening for logistics

English is assessed by task, not only by a self-reported CEFR label.

Screening categories:

- understand a carrier/broker introduction;
- ask and answer route/equipment questions;
- confirm numbers, dates, cities, rates, and documents;
- understand objections and request clarification;
- write a short professional follow-up;
- summarize a call accurately;
- avoid inventing information when comprehension is incomplete.

Suggested decision states:

- `ready_for_logistics_program`;
- `conditional_with_english_support`;
- `marketing_program_may_fit_better`;
- `not_ready_yet`.

A minimum level must be owner-approved for each cohort before publication.

## 11. Corporate employee-training intake

Collect:

- company and country;
- employee role(s);
- number of participants;
- logistics or marketing track;
- current skill level;
- required language;
- target workflow or business problem;
- desired schedule;
- confidentiality needs;
- systems/tools used;
- assessment/reporting expectation;
- decision-maker and billing contact;
- whether consultation, training, implementation, or all three are requested.

A corporate inquiry begins discovery. It does not confirm the `$600/month` concept, scope, participant count, schedule, certification, or outcome.

## 12. Required public disclaimers

### Employment

`Enrollment, practice, assessment, or completion does not guarantee employment, an interview, a contract, an internship, or partnership with Hermes or another company.`

### Income and business results

`The program does not guarantee income, clients, loads, rankings, views, leads, sales, or business results.`

### Logistics decisions

`Carriers, brokers, shippers, dealers, insurers, regulators, and other third parties control their own approvals, terms, decisions, and availability.`

### Program changes

`Dates, curriculum, mentors, tools, assignments, and availability are governed by the written terms for the specific enrollment period.`

## 13. Publication architecture

Start with:

- one logistics program hub;
- one marketing program hub;
- one eligibility/application route;
- one corporate-training inquiry route.

Do not generate pages for every country. Add market pages only after dated research, compliance review, language demand, unique local value, and a 7/10 score.
