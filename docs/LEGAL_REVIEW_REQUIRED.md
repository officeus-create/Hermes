# Legal Review Required Before Specific Launches

The following items require business-specific legal review before they are represented as fully compliant or activated:

- whether CCPA/CPRA or another U.S. state privacy statute applies to the relevant Hermes business;
- statutory request methods, verification, deadlines, appeals, sensitive-data rules, and opt-out signal handling;
- EEA/UK GDPR lawful basis, consent, controller/processor roles, cookies, international transfers, and representative/DPO requirements where applicable;
- payment/refund/cancellation and automatic-renewal requirements for each selling jurisdiction;
- regulated logistics authority, brokerage, carrier, insurance, or representation disclosures;
- employment/applicant privacy notices where required;
- final retention schedule tied to accounting, tax, claims, litigation, carrier, customer, and employment records.

Engineering must not convert the baseline privacy language into an unconditional claim of legal compliance without this review.
