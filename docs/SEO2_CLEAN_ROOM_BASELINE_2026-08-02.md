# SEO-2 Clean-Room Baseline and Money-Query Plan

Reviewed: 2026-08-02

## Goal

Increase qualified organic inquiries for:

1. car hauling dispatch;
2. car hauling loads and carrier access;
3. SEO for logistics companies;
4. SEO for independent auto dealers;
5. website development for logistics companies.

The KPI is not page count or raw traffic. The KPI is the privacy-safe funnel through explicit handoff and the manually reviewed qualified-lead, proposal, contract, and revenue stages.

## Clean-room method

This research used only public search results and public competitor pages. No Serpstat, keyword platform, search engine, or backlink tool was connected to CRM, Shipment History, private repositories, email content, customer records, credentials, or internal operational data.

Exact keyword difficulty and CPC values are **not** claimed. The opportunity file uses:

- `kd_proxy`: relative competition inferred from the number and specialization of visible ranking pages, exact-intent coverage, and the presence of established vertical agencies or platforms;
- `cpc_signal`: commercial value proxy inferred from service/demo/quote intent, visible competing vendors, pricing/demo CTAs, and the likelihood that the query can lead to a paid engagement.

Before implementation with a paid keyword tool, replace proxies only with dated exported evidence and preserve the same privacy boundary.

## Public SERP observations

### Car hauling dispatch and loads

Visible competitors commonly lead with high-paying loads, 24/7 support, load search, negotiation, broker communication, paperwork, billing, safety, and compliance. Some publish percentage pricing. Several pages are specialized, but many rely on broad claims, weak qualification, unclear carrier-control boundaries, or a generic contact form.

Hermes differentiators to preserve and strengthen:

- car-hauling-specific equipment and capacity review;
- authority-age and insurance-readiness boundaries;
- carrier-controlled booking and final operating decision;
- transparent preview mode and no automatic load/account creation;
- load evaluation by equipment, deadhead, timing, route, source, requirements, and paperwork;
- direct fallback plus a structured carrier workspace.

### SEO for logistics companies

Results include logistics-only agencies, general B2B agencies with transportation pages, and generic SEO vendors. Common weaknesses include unsupported ranking/revenue claims, unrelated testimonials, shallow logistics entity coverage, and little explanation of privacy, evidence, query ownership, or conversion qualification.

Hermes should emphasize first-party experience with carrier/customer paths, dispatch and vehicle-transport entities, public/private data boundaries, small tested releases, and search-to-qualified-inquiry measurement.

### SEO for auto dealers

Broad automotive SEO is more competitive, with established specialist vendors. The narrower independent-dealer opportunity is stronger when focused on inventory expiration/canonical rules, evergreen content, local eligibility, mobile inquiry paths, trade-in/financing boundaries, auction pickup, and vehicle transport.

Do not compete through invented inventory, generic city pages, fake reviews, or promises to outrank marketplaces.

### Website development for logistics companies

The SERP mixes:

- logistics UX/design studios;
- general agencies with logistics landing pages;
- trucking-specific website/SEO providers;
- freight-broker website builders and managed platforms;
- low-cost template builders.

This is the clearest candidate for one future niche commercial page, but only after the national Website Development owner is strengthened and Search Console/cannibalization evidence confirms that a separate page is useful.

## New-page gate

A new page requires all of the following:

- distinct commercial or problem-solving intent;
- real service capability and approved evidence;
- no competing canonical owner;
- useful content beyond changing a niche/location/equipment name;
- clear CTA and qualification path;
- internal links from relevant hubs;
- metadata, schema, sitemap ownership, and tests;
- Search Console or equivalent demand evidence when available;
- explicit cannibalization review.

Maximum for this SEO-2 cycle: one niche Website Development page and, only if separately proven, one problem-solving resource. No city, state, language, route, or equipment-page multiplication.

## Search and profile checks

Required operating checks:

- Google and Bing: verified webmaster properties, submitted sitemaps, selected canonicals, queries, clicks, CTR, coverage, and CWV;
- DuckDuckGo and Yahoo: sampled discoverability and brand/result consistency, not separate doorway optimization;
- Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Clutch, The Manifest, LinkedIn, and relevant logistics directories: accurate entity data and legitimate free/basic profiles;
- Google Business Profile and Apple/Maps location features: only after real storefront or eligible service-area status is confirmed;
- unlinked mentions and conflicting NAP/entity records: record in the outreach CRM before correction.

## Measurement after every release

Record before and after:

- current-head CI status;
- indexable URL and sitemap ownership changes;
- title/H1/canonical/schema/internal-link changes;
- organic impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, and selected canonical when verified access exists;
- commercial CTA clicks;
- intake starts;
- previews ready;
- explicit handoffs;
- manually reviewed and qualified inquiries;
- proposals, contracts, and revenue;
- cannibalization or index-loss warnings.

Stop only the affected package when there is a credible penalty, indexation, canonical, privacy, or cannibalization risk. Continue independent safe tasks.
