# U.S. SEO Query-to-Page Map — Commercial Priorities

**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Scope:** United States  
**Primary outcomes:** qualified carrier inquiries, website-project inquiries, and SEO-service inquiries  
**Status:** initial ownership map; Search Console and keyword-volume validation still required

This document assigns each important search-intent family to one preferred Hermes URL. It is not a ranking or traffic claim and does not authorize mass page creation.

## Operating rules

1. One primary commercial intent should have one preferred canonical owner.
2. Audience pages, resources, case studies, and location pages support the commercial owner; they do not compete with it.
3. Equipment capacity and service details should usually be sections or FAQs unless evidence shows a materially distinct search intent and conversion path.
4. No page may promise loads, rates, revenue, rankings, leads, traffic, or response times.
5. Search snapshots below are directional SERP observations, not keyword-volume data.

# 1. Car-hauling dispatch services

## SERP snapshot

Current results repeatedly use a national service-page pattern aimed at **car haulers, owner-operators, and small fleets**. Common qualification language includes trailer/equipment type, MC authority, insurance, preferred lanes, operating days, and dispatch/back-office scope. Competitors often combine load search, broker communication, paperwork, invoicing, and carrier approval into one commercial page.

Examples reviewed:

- [OnePro Dispatch — Car Hauler Dispatch Service for Owner-Operators](https://www.oneprodispatch.com/)
- [Carhaulerdispatcher.com — Services](https://www.carhaulerdispatcher.com/car-hauler-dispatch-services/)
- [Resolute Logistics — Car Hauler Dispatch Service](https://resolute-logistics.com/car-hauling-dispatch-service)
- [VNDS Logistics — Car Hauler Dispatch Service](https://vndslogistics.com/)

The snapshot supports one national commercial owner rather than separate thin pages for every trailer capacity.

## Conversion requirement

The primary CTA must lead to a structured carrier intake, not directly to email. Email and phone remain fallbacks.

Minimum qualification context:

- carrier role;
- active USDOT and MC/authority details;
- authority status and age;
- insurance status;
- equipment type;
- vehicle capacity from 1 through 10;
- fleet size;
- home base;
- preferred regions or lanes;
- availability;
- current dispatch arrangement;
- consent and clear preview/handoff boundary.

## Cannibalization controls

- `/paths/logistics/carriers/car-hauling/` must not target the same title/H1 as the commercial owner.
- Capacity modifiers remain sections unless distinct evidence justifies separate pages.
- General truck-dispatch pages must route car-hauler intent to the car-hauling commercial owner.
- Location pages must serve real local transport intent, not repeat dispatch copy with a city substitution.
