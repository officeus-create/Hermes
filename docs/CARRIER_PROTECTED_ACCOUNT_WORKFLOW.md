# Carrier Protected Account Workflow

Status: **REVIEW-ONLY OPERATING PROCEDURE / PRODUCTION SIGNING OFF**

## Purpose

Use this workflow whenever Hermes introduces or materially develops a shipper, dealer, auction, fleet, customer, broker program, or other direct commercial contact for a Carrier.

## Required agreement packet

Before live use, the carrier-specific packet must contain the same immutable versions of:

1. Carrier Administrative & Dispatch Support Agreement;
2. Appendix A with the selected 6%, 8%, or separately approved Carrier Proposal;
3. Carrier Protection & Compensation Addendum;
4. any Protected Account Notice being acknowledged at signing;
5. electronic-signature consent and audit trail.

A later Protected Account Notice may be delivered under the already signed Agreement and Addendum by business email or another agreed written channel.

## Creating a Protected Account record

Log the following in the private CRM or approved records system:

- Carrier legal name and MC/USDOT;
- Protected Account legal or commonly used name;
- account type;
- introduction or material-development date;
- Hermes representative or team responsible;
- evidence reference: email, CRM entry, message, call, meeting, proposal, or other dated record;
- applicable percentage or signed custom fee;
- notice delivery date and channel;
- Carrier objection deadline;
- any objection and supporting dated evidence;
- protection end date.

Do not place carrier identity, account identity, a custom percentage, or evidence records in a public URL, analytics event, GitHub issue, screenshot, or public log.

## Notice and objection

Send the Protected Account Notice promptly after the introduction or material development becomes identifiable. The Carrier has five business days to provide dated proof of a pre-existing active relationship. Record the response or absence of response without using threatening language.

## Covered work and compensation

The selected fee applies to covered transportation revenue during the Agreement and for twenty-four months after termination, regardless of whether the work is later booked or paid through another platform, dispatcher, MC, affiliate, authority, factor, employee, contractor, driver, or intermediary.

The Carrier may serve the Protected Account directly. The restriction is against avoiding or concealing the agreed compensation, not against performing the transportation.

## Weekly invoice workflow

- Work period: Monday through Saturday.
- Prepare the weekly statement or invoice on Saturday.
- Identify covered loads, revenue basis, selected percentage, and calculation.
- Default due date: seven calendar days after invoice, unless signed Appendix A states another schedule.
- Specific written objections are due within five business days.
- Continue collecting undisputed amounts while resolving any specifically disputed item.
- Apply late charges only to undisputed overdue amounts and only at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum lawful rate.

## Records and reconciliation

Request only records reasonably necessary to verify covered compensation. Keep requests limited to relevant rate confirmations, proof of delivery, invoices, settlements, factor reports, payment records, and related transaction evidence. A routine limited reconciliation should not occur more than once per calendar quarter unless a specific discrepancy, nonpayment, or suspected circumvention exists.

## Personnel protection

The twelve-month personnel restriction is narrow. It applies only to Hermes employees, agents, dispatchers, assistants, or contractors who materially served the Carrier and had direct business contact. It does not cover general job advertising, documented pre-existing relationships, or unrelated contact.

## Escalation

Use the following sequence for unpaid undisputed amounts or suspected circumvention:

1. written reconciliation request;
2. specific invoice and evidence summary;
3. reasonable cure notice;
4. pause new support when permitted;
5. documented collection process;
6. accounting, actual-damages, or equitable remedies as allowed by the signed documents and applicable law.

Do not invent a punitive fixed fine, minimum attorney-fee amount, confession of judgment, personal guaranty, blanket lien, or automatic debit.
