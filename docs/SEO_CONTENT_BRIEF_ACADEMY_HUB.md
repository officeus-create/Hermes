# SEO Content Brief — Hermes Business Academy Hub

## Status

Planning brief only. Do not publish until route architecture, claims, application flow, and localized strategy are reviewed against the current codebase and current owner-approved business facts.

## Proposed route

`/academy/`

## Search intent

A multilingual user wants practical online training connected to real business work but has not yet chosen between US Logistics, Marketing/SMM, Sales, or Operations/COO.

## Primary audience

- Ukrainians and Russian-speaking people worldwide with practical conversational English;
- multilingual candidates seeking remote US-facing work;
- beginners and career changers;
- experienced sales, marketing, logistics, recruiting, or management candidates seeking a stronger path;
- future team leads, operational managers, and regional representatives.

## Page objective

Help the visitor identify the right direction, understand the practical model, self-qualify, and start an application or consultation.

## Proposed SEO metadata

### English

Title hypothesis:

`Practical Logistics, Marketing, Sales & COO Training | Hermes`

Description hypothesis:

`Choose practical training in US logistics, SMM, sales, or operations. Learn through structured practice and explore career paths in the Hermes ecosystem.`

H1 hypothesis:

`Choose a practical path into logistics, marketing, sales, or operations`

Metadata must be tested against pixel/length limits and live SERP competitors before implementation.

## Core positioning

Hermes Academy is not positioned as a library of generic recorded courses. It is a guided progression:

`Learn → Practice → Prove → Work → Lead`

The page must explain that this progression is conditional on performance, business need, eligibility, and available opportunities. It is not an employment guarantee.

## Recommended page structure

### 1. Hero

Goal: immediate direction choice.

Content:
- practical training connected to real business workflows;
- international participation;
- English expectations stated honestly;
- CTA: `Choose your direction`;
- secondary CTA: `Request a consultation`.

Avoid:
- guaranteed job claims;
- guaranteed income;
- unverified “global leader” language;
- too many numeric achievements above the fold.

### 2. Direction selector

Four cards:

1. US Logistics
   - dispatch, carrier sales, recruiting, negotiations, operations;
   - suitable for communicators, problem solvers, and people comfortable with US schedules.

2. Marketing & SMM
   - content, organic distribution, advertising, funnels, lead handling;
   - suitable for creative, analytical, and commercial candidates.

3. Sales & Negotiation
   - discovery, SPIN, objections, calls, closing, sales management;
   - suitable for persuasive communicators and future team leads.

4. Operations & Future COO
   - planning, meetings, hiring, delegation, KPI, reporting, department launch;
   - suitable for organizers and emerging leaders.

Each card links to one program hub and displays one honest prerequisite.

### 3. Who this is for

Segment by situation rather than demographics alone:

- starting a new career;
- already selling but entering US logistics or marketing;
- building a portfolio through structured practice;
- moving from specialist to manager;
- looking for a remote international path;
- considering a future regional role.

### 4. How the model works

Five stages:

1. Learn — terminology, systems, scripts, process.
2. Practice — assignments, simulations, calls, or supervised workflows.
3. Prove — measurable tasks, reviews, consistency, communication.
4. Work — eligible candidates may enter available projects or roles.
5. Lead — strong performers may grow into team or regional responsibility.

For every stage distinguish:
- guaranteed training activity;
- performance-based progression;
- opportunity dependent on current business demand.

### 5. What practical means

Examples, only where current:
- role-based scripts;
- call review;
- workflow simulations;
- content and funnel assignments;
- planning and KPI exercises;
- mentor or manager feedback;
- documented deliverables.

Do not imply access to real customer data or real external calls unless confirmed and privacy-safe.

### 6. Language and schedule fit

Explain:
- English is needed for US-facing roles;
- Ukrainian or Russian may support onboarding and learning;
- work/training may follow US Central Time or another program-specific schedule;
- exact availability requirements must be current and program-specific.

Add a quick self-check:
- country/city;
- languages;
- English level;
- available hours;
- current experience;
- selected direction.

### 7. Career-path explorer

Show role paths grouped by program:

Logistics:
- Dispatcher
- Carrier Sales Representative
- Driver Recruiter
- Shipper/Dealer Manager
- Broker Assistant

Marketing:
- SMM Consultant
- Content Strategist
- Marketing Sales Consultant

Sales:
- Sales Consultant
- Sales Manager
- Director of Sales path

Operations:
- Operations Assistant
- Team Lead
- Operations Manager
- Future COO
- Regional Representative

Only show roles currently supported by real training or hiring demand. If uncertain, mark internally and omit publicly.

### 8. Why Hermes is different

Evidence-led differentiators:
- multiple connected business divisions;
- training tied to defined roles;
- cross-functional paths;
- multilingual international audience;
- progression based on demonstrated work;
- practical business communication rather than theory only.

Every numeric result or scale claim requires a source and status.

### 9. FAQ

Recommended visible questions:

1. Can I start without experience?
2. What English level is required?
3. Can I participate from another country?
4. How much time is required?
5. Is the training live or recorded?
6. Does training guarantee employment?
7. How do I choose between logistics, marketing, sales, and operations?
8. Can I grow into a management role?
9. What happens after I apply?
10. What equipment and internet connection are required?

Add FAQ schema only when the same questions and answers are visibly rendered.

### 10. Final qualification CTA

Prompt:

`Tell us where you are now and where you want to go.`

Fields:
- country;
- city;
- languages;
- English level;
- experience;
- selected direction;
- goal: skill, work with Hermes, management, regional partnership;
- availability.

Do not send personal data to GA4. Preserve the repository’s preview-first contact architecture unless live delivery is separately approved.

## Internal linking

Inbound links from:
- homepage;
- global navigation;
- About/Hermes ecosystem section;
- logistics, marketing, and careers pages;
- Ukrainian and Russian localized homepages.

Outbound links to:
- four program hubs;
- careers hub;
- active role pages;
- international/language hub;
- application or consultation flow.

## Schema recommendation

Potentially:
- `EducationalOrganization` or organization-level markup on the main entity;
- `Course` only for real, defined programs with current provider, format, and offer data;
- `FAQPage` only for visible FAQ content;
- breadcrumb markup.

Do not add fake ratings, reviews, offers, prices, start dates, or employment outcomes.

## Content evidence needed from owner/current operations

Before publishing, confirm:

1. Official Academy name.
2. Current programs actually open for applications.
3. Current training format.
4. Current duration and schedule.
5. Current price, promotion, scholarship, or free-track rules.
6. Active mentors and review model.
7. Exact English requirements by program.
8. Current roles Hermes is hiring or training toward.
9. Whether real-call practice is available and under what supervision.
10. Approved results, case studies, scale, and employment evidence.
11. Approved contact/application route.
12. Countries or applicant categories that cannot be accepted.

## Acceptance criteria for implementation

- one clear H1;
- unique title and description;
- no unsupported claims;
- responsive direction selector;
- accessible keyboard navigation;
- visible FAQ if schema is used;
- internal links to all live program hubs;
- correct canonical;
- included in sitemap only when complete;
- build, static validation, unit tests, and Playwright pass;
- no personal data sent to analytics;
- no conflict with existing Academy or localized route intent.
