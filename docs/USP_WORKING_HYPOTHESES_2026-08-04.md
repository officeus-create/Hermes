# A. Hermes Logistics — carrier and dispatch

## USP A1

> Dispatch support that keeps the carrier in control.

**Why it may work:** control and no-forced-dispatch language exists in the market, but many competitors still lead primarily with rates and load volume. Hermes can make carrier approval, agreed rules and clear communication the center of the message.

**Evidence needed:** current agreement, booking approval rule, onboarding questions and real escalation ownership.

**Do not imply:** that every carrier receives the same approval flow or that no decision can ever be delegated under a signed arrangement.

## USP A2

> One coordinated workflow for dispatch, broker communication, documents, invoicing and follow-through.

**Why it may work:** competitors frequently list these services separately. The differentiating meaning is shared ownership across the load lifecycle.

**Evidence needed:** responsibility map, current service scope and examples of handoff between dispatch and back office.

**Do not imply:** a finished universal TMS or full service for every customer.

## USP A3

> Start with the operating problem you need solved—not a one-size-fits-all dispatch package.

**Why it may work:** current public copy allows requests to begin with dispatch, documents, invoicing or another defined need.

**Evidence needed:** current commercial terms and confirmation that modular engagement remains available.

## USP A4

> Human dispatch decisions supported by structured carrier, lane and document context.

**Why it may work:** it differentiates Hermes from generic call-center dispatch and from claims of fully autonomous AI.

**Evidence needed:** actual data fields, current workflow and which recommendations are human versus automated.

**Blocked wording:** “AI chooses the best or most profitable load.”

# B. Hermes Logistics — dealers and vehicle transportation

## USP B1

> Vehicle transportation organized around inventory readiness—not only the pickup request.

**Why it may work:** dealer platforms lead with speed and visibility. Hermes can focus on release status, condition, equipment fit, storage deadline and next-step clarity.

**Evidence needed:** current intake, real review ownership and current dealer workflow.

## USP B2

> One qualification path from auction or seller release to separately confirmed transport.

**Why it may work:** the public intake already gathers facility context, release status, operability and equipment needs while preserving a separate confirmation boundary.

**Evidence needed:** exact internal steps after submission.

**Do not imply:** booking, carrier assignment, rate or timing is confirmed by form submission.

## USP B3

> A practical coordination option for independent dealers that need control without enterprise-platform complexity.

**Why it may work:** large competitors emphasize TMS platforms and integrations. Smaller dealers may prefer direct human qualification and a visible fallback contact.

**Evidence needed:** real target-customer fit, actual support ownership and examples of independent-dealer work.

## USP B4

> Exception-aware vehicle transport review before a move is represented as ready.

**Why it may work:** the current intake separates operable, rolling inoperable and non-rolling conditions and captures release/storage context.

**Evidence needed:** real delay, damage notice, photo/BOL, insurance and escalation procedures.

**Blocked wording:** guaranteed pickup, guaranteed storage-fee avoidance, guaranteed price or real-time tracking unless verified.

# C. Hermes Business Academy

## USP C1

> Training measured by practical responsibility—not course completion alone.

**Why it may work:** the public Academy model progresses from awareness to understanding to application, while many competitors emphasize certification and video modules.

**Evidence needed:** current assignments, evaluation criteria, correction process and completion standard.

## USP C2

> Role-based development across logistics, marketing and operational leadership.

**Why it may work:** Hermes has three public paths rather than a single dispatcher certificate.

**Evidence needed:** current curriculum and active ownership of each track.

## USP C3

> Built for multilingual professionals preparing to work in English in U.S. logistics.

**Why it may work:** Hermes can serve Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking learners internationally while keeping English as the working-market requirement.

**Evidence needed:** current supported languages, language requirements, teaching materials and instructor availability.

## USP C4

> Learn a responsibility, prove the operating standard, then progress toward broader ownership.

**Why it may work:** it connects training to team lead and COO/Operational Director progression rather than promising employment after a course.

**Evidence needed:** current progression criteria, active leadership path and examples that may be published.

**Blocked wording:** guaranteed employment, guaranteed income, guaranteed placement, “job-ready” without a defined standard.

# D. ProgressoPro / Hermes IT Development

## USP D1

> Marketing, sales follow-up and operations are designed as one customer journey.

**Why it may work:** competitors usually bundle channels or tools. Hermes can explain the handoff from positioning and content to website conversion, qualification, CRM and follow-up.

**Evidence needed:** current delivery scope, process map and an approved case or working example.

## USP D2

> Automation begins with a revenue or operating problem—not with a tool.

**Why it may work:** it reduces fear of unnecessary software and positions discovery around an actual business loss.

**Evidence needed:** current discovery process and examples of problem-first scoping.

## USP D3

> Start with one measurable workflow, prove it safely, then expand.

**Why it may work:** a controlled first release is easier for a small business to approve than a large transformation program.

**Evidence needed:** current pilot method, baseline, acceptance criteria, rollback and measurement ownership.

## USP D4

> Human-controlled AI and automation for decisions that still require accountability.

**Why it may work:** many AI agencies market replacement and autonomy. Hermes can emphasize approval, permissions, auditability and rollback.

**Evidence needed:** live system examples, approval boundaries, logs, permissions and incident handling.

**Blocked wording:** autonomous company, replace your staff, guaranteed ROI, guaranteed leads, guaranteed ranking, finished Hermes Connect platform unless live and evidenced.

# Shared ecosystem hypothesis

Status: `OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE`

> Hermes connects disconnected work into a clearer operating system while keeping the responsible person in control.

## Direction-specific translation

- **Carrier:** control over load decisions, operating rules and communication.
- **Dealer:** control over transport readiness, exceptions and inventory movement.
- **Academy learner:** control over skill progression through practical responsibility.
- **Business owner:** control over marketing, leads, data, systems and automation.

## Why this should remain a hypothesis

The statement is strategically coherent, but each direction must prove its own workflow. The ecosystem message must not imply that every system is already integrated, that Hermes Connect is fully released or that every customer receives all four directions.
