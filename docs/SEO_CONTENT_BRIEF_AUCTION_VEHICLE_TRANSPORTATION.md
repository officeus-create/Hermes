## Core positioning

Hermes coordinates the information and operating review required before an auction vehicle move can be accepted by a carrier. A request is not an automatic booking, a pickup guarantee, or proof that the vehicle has been released.

## Page structure

### 1. Hero

Explain that auction moves often fail before dispatch because release status, lot rules, storage deadlines, vehicle condition, documents or equipment requirements are incomplete.

Primary CTA: `Prepare an auction transport request`

Secondary CTA: `Carrier: share auction pickup capacity`

Direct fallback: approved Logistics Sales phone and email.

### 2. Who this is for

- dealership inventory buyers;
- wholesalers and resellers;
- private buyers;
- brokers and shippers;
- carriers with open car hauler, hotshot, multi-car or specialized equipment.

### 3. Information required before review

- auction/facility name and city/state;
- buyer or business name;
- buyer/lot/reference number where operationally required;
- release status;
- storage deadline and known fees;
- pickup window and gate hours;
- origin and destination city/state/ZIP;
- year, make and model;
- operable/inoperable condition;
- whether the vehicle rolls, steers and brakes;
- keys available or missing;
- winch/forklift/loading assistance requirements;
- vehicle dimensions or modifications when relevant;
- open or enclosed preference;
- contact details and consent.

VIN, buyer documents, exact private addresses and release documents should be collected only through an approved private workflow, not placed in public URLs, analytics or public examples.

### 4. Auction pickup coordination process

1. Receive the preliminary request.
2. Confirm the origin, destination and vehicle details.
3. Check whether release and pickup requirements are complete.
4. Review condition and loading requirements.
5. Match the request to compatible equipment and carrier capacity.
6. Verify carrier authority, insurance, operating fit and communication route.
7. Obtain written operational confirmation before action.
8. Track status and required documents through pickup and delivery coordination.

Make clear that each carrier decides whether to accept the move.

### 5. Common auction pickup blockers

- vehicle is not released;
- buyer number or gate pass is missing;
- storage deadline is unclear;
- condition is described only as “inoperable” without rolling/steering/braking details;
- forklift or loader rules are unknown;
- vehicle has oversized wheels, low clearance or modifications;
- pickup appointment is required;
- title or ownership paperwork is confused with pickup release paperwork;
- destination access is unsuitable for the assigned equipment;
- target price is not aligned with lane and equipment requirements.

### 6. Equipment considerations

Discuss, without promising availability:

- open car hauler;
- enclosed trailer;
- hotshot/gooseneck;
- 1–3 vehicle equipment;
- 5–10 vehicle capacity;
- winch-equipped trailer;
- equipment suitable for low-clearance, damaged or non-rolling vehicles.

### 7. Dealer and repeated auction lanes

Explain that repeated dealer/auction lanes can be reviewed over time for:

- recurring origin facilities;
- typical vehicle mix;
- preferred delivery points;
- equipment patterns;
- documentation requirements;
- pickup windows;
- carrier performance history.

Do not promise dedicated capacity or fixed rates.

### 8. Service boundaries

Hermes may support information intake, route review, equipment review, carrier qualification, communication and dispatch coordination. Hermes should not be presented as the auction, auction agent, vehicle seller, title processor, insurer, warehouse, port operator, or guaranteed motor carrier unless separately evidenced.

### 9. FAQ

1. **What information is needed to pick up a vehicle from an auction?**  
   Facility, release status, buyer/lot reference where required, storage deadline, vehicle details, operability, loading needs, pickup window and delivery location.

2. **Can Hermes confirm that my vehicle is released?**  
   Hermes can request or review release information provided through the approved workflow, but the auction/facility controls release status.

3. **Can an inoperable auction vehicle be transported?**  
   Potentially, after confirming whether it rolls, steers and brakes and whether a winch, forklift or other loading assistance is required.

4. **Does submitting the form book a carrier?**  
   No. It creates a request for review. A carrier must accept the move after equipment, lane, authority and operational details are confirmed.

5. **Can you pick up from Copart or IAA?**  
   Requests involving these facilities may be reviewed based on actual release, gate, equipment and carrier requirements. Hermes is not presented as affiliated with either company.

6. **How quickly can an auction vehicle be picked up?**  
   Timing depends on release, storage deadline, facility rules, appointments, equipment and available qualified carrier capacity. No pickup time is guaranteed before confirmation.

7. **Do you offer open and enclosed transport?**  
   Both may be considered when compatible capacity is available and the carrier accepts the request.

8. **What happens if the vehicle has no keys?**  
   This must be disclosed before equipment review because loading requirements may change.

9. **Can multiple auction vehicles be transported together?**  
   The request can be reviewed for multi-vehicle equipment based on origin, destination, dimensions, condition, timing and carrier capacity.

10. **Are storage fees included in a transport estimate?**  
    Storage and facility fees are controlled by the auction/facility and should not be represented as included unless specifically confirmed in writing.

11. **Is the displayed route a truck-specific route?**  
    Any automated route result is a preliminary planning estimate, not truck-specific navigation or a guaranteed transit time.

12. **Who makes the final decision to accept the load?**  
    The qualified motor carrier retains the final decision after reviewing the complete operating details.
