# Hermes SEO — Market Research Wave 1

Date: 2026-08-18  
Scope: Google/Bing SEO only.  
Canonical architecture: `docs/SEO_FOUR_DIRECTION_MARKET_ARCHITECTURE_2026-08-18.md`.

## Objective

Turn the four-direction architecture into a bounded research queue without publishing speculative pages.

This wave separates market attractiveness evidence, search-demand evidence, canonical query/page ownership, commercial funnel readiness and visual/CEO approval requirements.

A strong city, large diaspora, dense industry or important vertical is only a research signal. It is not sufficient evidence to create a landing page.

## Logistics — USA

Current state: national/service owners exist; local acquisition is concentrated in Wisconsin.

Wave 1 research tasks:

1. Rank additional U.S. states/metros by vehicle-sales/dealership/transport-market relevance.
2. Cross those markets with existing canonical owners before proposing new pages.
3. Separate dealer/auction/customer vehicle-transport intent from carrier-dispatch intent.
4. Keep all new location candidates in `research` until query evidence and conversion fit are documented.
5. Do not rewrite the active Logistics SEO service experiment during its observation window.

## Marketing — global cities

Initial research cities: New York, London, Paris, San Jose and Seattle.

These are research seeds only. City economic strength does not establish local SEO keyword demand.

For each city, test only current verified service owners first: SEO, Local SEO, website development, website redesign and existing industry-specific SEO owners where distinct local demand exists.

A city page is justified only if local commercial intent is materially different from the global service owner and the page can offer real city-specific buyer value.

## Academy — Ukraine + diaspora

Initial research markets: Ukraine, Germany, Poland, Czechia and Spain.

The non-Ukraine countries are research seeds because current European statistics show large populations displaced from Ukraine under temporary protection. Population presence is not search or enrollment demand.

Wave 1 tests:

- Ukrainian-language demand for U.S. logistics education/training;
- Ukrainian-language demand for marketing education/training;
- program intent by country/city;
- schedule/time-zone objections;
- application intent;
- local competition;
- whether a localized page can add material value beyond translation.

Russia remains excluded. Any additional exclusion must come from an explicit owner/compliance list.

## IT / Hermes Connect

Vertical order remains Repair Shops, Beauty, Fitness, Education/Courses.

Wave 1 does not create city/district pages. First it maps search and product fit at the vertical/workflow level.

Research questions:

- Which buyer problems match functions already live or truthfully available?
- Which queries describe workflow pain rather than generic software curiosity?
- Which vertical has the clearest path from search to demo/access/registration?
- Which current Hermes Connect module can own the query without overstating readiness?
- Where would a new vertical landing page require visible screenshots/cards/CTA changes and therefore CEO visual approval?

## Publication gate

No Wave 1 candidate may become `approved_for_build` until the registry contains query/market evidence, canonical owner, service/product truth, distinct page value, conversion path, measurement plan and funnel QA status.

If a page requires a noticeable visual change, funnel QA cannot become approved until a working preview has passed desktop + 390px review and CEO approval.
