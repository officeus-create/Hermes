# SEO Content Brief — Car Hauling Dispatch and Direct Freight Development

Status: implementation-ready content brief
Branch: `seo/academy-audience-roadmap`
Owner approval required before production publication

## 1. Purpose

Create a substantive commercial page for carriers, owner-operators, and small fleets that need car hauling dispatch, back-office coordination, and a structured path toward direct freight development.

The page must rank as a real service page, not a thin lead form and not a generic dispatch landing page.

## 2. Recommended URL

`/logistics/car-hauling-dispatch/`

Do not create duplicate variants such as:

- `/car-hauling-dispatch-services/`
- `/auto-transport-dispatch/`
- `/car-hauler-dispatch/`

Use one canonical page and route closely related terms into it.

## 3. Search intent

Primary intent: commercial investigation / service comparison.

Visitor is typically trying to answer one or more of these questions:

- Who can help dispatch a car hauler?
- Can a dispatcher support a 3-car, 5-car, 6-car, 7-car, 9-car, or 10-car unit?
- Can one company support both day-to-day dispatch and direct shipper/dealer development?
- What documents and authority are required before onboarding?
- How are rates, deadhead, pickup windows, auctions, dealers, and brokers handled?
- Is this suitable for a new authority or only an established carrier?

## 4. Audience

Primary:

- owner-operators with open car-hauling equipment;
- small car-hauling fleets;
- carriers expanding into auto transport;
- carriers needing dispatch and back-office support;
- carriers seeking a structured direct-freight development process.

Secondary:

- enclosed auto transport carriers;
- hotshot operators hauling vehicles;
- new authorities preparing for car hauling;
- fleet managers replacing an inconsistent dispatch process.

## 8. Core positioning

Primary message:

**You do not get only a dispatcher. You get an operations team for today and a direct-freight development process for tomorrow.**

**Load boards for today → direct relationships for tomorrow → stronger inbound demand over time.**

This is a positioning statement, not a guarantee.

## 9. Required page structure

### Hero

Include:

- H1;
- one-sentence value proposition;
- primary CTA: `Apply for Carrier Support`;
- secondary CTA: `Request a Dispatch Review`;
- qualification note: subject to authority, insurance, equipment, lane fit, communication, and current capacity.

### Section: Who this service is for

Cover:

- owner-operators;
- small fleets;
- open and enclosed carriers;
- multi-car units;
- hotshot vehicle transport where operationally appropriate;
- established and new authorities, with different onboarding readiness requirements.

### Section: What Hermes handles day to day

Include:

- load search;
- lane and deadhead planning;
- broker and shipper communication;
- rate negotiation;
- setup packets;
- insurance certificate coordination;
- pickup and delivery communication;
- exception handling;
- document collection;
- invoicing and accounts-receivable support when included in the signed scope;
- daily carrier communication.

### Section: Direct freight development

Explain the approximately 90-day working process without promising results.

Include:

- analyze equipment and preferred lanes;
- identify dealers, auctions, ports, shippers, brokers, and direct customers;
- identify decision-makers;
- build a prospect list;
- conduct structured outreach;
- request load lists;
- document objections and demand signals;
- identify repeatable lanes;
- transfer qualified opportunities into operations.

Use wording such as:

- `we work to develop`;
- `subject to prospect response and operational fit`;
- `based on verified equipment and service coverage`.

### Section: Equipment and operating fit

Potential categories:

- 1–3 vehicle hotshot setups;
- 3–5 vehicle trailers;
- 6–7 vehicle car haulers;
- 8–10 vehicle carriers;
- enclosed transport;
- inoperable vehicle capability;
- auction and dealer pickup capability;
- port access when required.

Do not imply Hermes accepts every equipment type automatically.

### Section: Qualification

Required or review fields:

- legal company name and DBA;
- MC and USDOT;
- authority status;
- insurance;
- equipment type and capacity;
- home market;
- preferred lanes;
- driver/fleet count;
- communication availability;
- service history;
- claims/safety review;
- load-board access where relevant;
- documents needed for onboarding.

### Section: How onboarding works

Recommended steps:

1. Submit carrier information.
2. Verify MC/DOT, authority, insurance, and equipment.
3. Review lanes, goals, and operational fit.
4. Confirm scope, percentage, communication process, and carrier approval workflow.
5. Start dispatch operations.
6. Begin direct-freight research and outreach when included.
7. Review results, lane quality, and next opportunities.

State clearly that the carrier makes the final load decision and remains in control of operations.

### Section: Trusted Carrier Network

Explain that after approximately 60–90 days of successful work, a carrier may become eligible.

Qualification dimensions may include:

- verified authority and insurance;
- equipment verification;
- communication reliability;
- pickup and delivery reliability;
- document quality;
- professional customer interaction;
- safety and claims review;
- lane/service fit;
- Hermes performance history.

The network is earned, not purchased or automatically granted.

### Section: Real inbound demand example

Use only the approved anonymized framing:

Hermes received a direct inbound request involving a rare 1961 Maserati 3500 Vignale Spyder arriving from Puerto Rico, with pickup near JFK Airport and delivery to Wilmington, North Carolina.

Required disclaimers:

- label it `Real inbound request` or `Example of direct inbound demand`;
- do not publish customer name, phone, exact address, VIN, or private documents;
- do not say Hermes completed the transport unless completion evidence is available;
- explain the qualification required: port/release details, customs status, operability, open/enclosed requirement, insurance, authority, special handling, storage/demurrage, and budget.

### Section: FAQ

Recommended questions:

1. What types of car-hauling equipment can Hermes support?
2. Does Hermes guarantee weekly gross revenue?
3. Does the carrier approve every load before booking?
4. Can Hermes help a new authority?
5. Does Hermes work only from load boards?
6. How does direct-freight development work?
7. How long does onboarding take?
8. What documents are required?
9. Can Hermes support enclosed vehicle transport?
10. Can Hermes work with multiple trucks or drivers?
11. Is billing or accounts-receivable support included?
12. How does Hermes verify carrier eligibility?

Answers must remain factual and scoped. Avoid guaranteed loads, income, rates, or rankings.

### Final CTA

Primary:

`Apply for Car Hauling Dispatch Support`

Secondary:

`Request a Carrier Operations Review`

Form should collect only necessary data and must not place PII in analytics, URLs, or local storage.

## 13. Claim safety

Approved language:

- `subject to verification`;
- `may become eligible`;
- `designed to support`;
- `we work to develop`;
- `based on current capacity`;
- `scope confirmed during onboarding`;
- `carrier retains final approval`.

Prohibited without evidence:

- guaranteed loads;
- guaranteed income;
- guaranteed weekly gross;
- guaranteed direct customers;
- guaranteed ranking;
- top-paying loads;
- always available;
- exclusive dealer contracts;
- nationwide capacity at all times.
