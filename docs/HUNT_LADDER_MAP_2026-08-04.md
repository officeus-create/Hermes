# Hunt Ladder Map — 5 money pages

Reviewed: 2026-08-04

Purpose: map the buyer's progression from an urgent problem to a qualified inquiry for the five current money pages. This document is an internal conversion and content-planning artifact. It does not authorize publishing unsupported claims, response-time guarantees, named experts, or service procedures that have not been confirmed by the owner.

Status vocabulary:

- `PUBLISHABLE_VERIFIED` — supported by current public copy or confirmed operating model.
- `OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE` — useful working hypothesis; do not publish as fact until evidence or owner confirmation exists.
- `OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED` — cannot be completed without a real process, person, policy, or proof supplied by the owner.

## Ladder model

1. **Trigger** — the event that starts the search.
2. **Problem recognition** — the buyer names the operational or financial pain.
3. **Category search** — the buyer searches for a type of solution.
4. **Approach comparison** — the buyer compares self-service, specialist, agency, platform, or internal hire.
5. **Risk removal** — the buyer checks control, price, process, proof, responsibility, and downside.
6. **Provider selection** — the buyer decides whether Hermes is credible and suitable.
7. **Action** — the buyer submits a qualified inquiry, calls, or emails.
8. **Post-submit certainty** — the buyer understands what happens next without an unsupported timing promise.

# Cross-page Hunt Ladder priorities

## P0 — close immediately after owner process confirmation

1. **Car Hauling Dispatch post-submit process**
   - Who reviews the request?
   - What fields are checked?
   - Does the team reply by phone, email, or both?
   - Is “during U.S. business hours” accurate?
   - What happens if information is missing?

2. **Dealer delay/damage process**
   - Who receives the first notice?
   - What documents/photos/BOL are requested?
   - What is Hermes responsible for versus the carrier/insurance party?
   - How are customer updates handled?
   - What wording is legally and operationally accurate?

## P1 — trust layer requiring a real person

3. **Logistics SEO reviewer**
   - Name, role, evidence-supported experience, photo, approved bio, review responsibility.

4. **Academy instructor/mentor**
   - Name, role, track ownership, evidence-supported experience, photo, approved bio.

## P1 — internal linking and CTA alignment

- Every page should have one primary buyer-stage CTA.
- Informational sections should link forward to the next ladder stage, not only back to the homepage.
- Comparison content must remain neutral and evidence-based.
- No commercial page should end without a clear fallback phone/email route.

## P2 — measurement

Privacy-safe events:

- commercial page viewed;
- comparison section reached;
- objection/FAQ opened;
- primary CTA clicked;
- form started;
- delivery confirmed;
- fallback phone/email opened.

Do not send submitted names, emails, phones, MC/USDOT, VINs, routes, messages, or other lead values to analytics.

# Completion rule

The Hunt Ladder is considered implemented for a page when:

1. each stage has visible or intentionally linked coverage;
2. the primary CTA matches the buyer's current decision;
3. major risk questions are answered without invented promises;
4. post-submit expectations are accurate;
5. direct fallback contact remains visible;
6. conversion events are privacy-safe;
7. any `OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED` item remains visibly blocked in internal documentation rather than filled with placeholder copy.
