# Hermes Business Academy — Language & Compliance Eligibility Policy

Status: approved working policy for current public Academy programs.

## U.S. Logistics Operations

For tracks involving live U.S.-market calls, carrier or broker communication, or negotiation, working spoken English at B2 level or higher is required. Other learning tasks may be reviewed separately, but communication-heavy U.S. logistics work requires B2+ readiness.

## Marketing

For the current international Marketing track, working Russian or Ukrainian is required for internal communication and translation workflows. A second language used in the participant's local market — for example Italian, Spanish, French, German, or another relevant language — is a strong practical advantage. English at B2 level or higher is also a strong advantage and may be required for English-market client communication.

## Compliance eligibility

Participation is subject to applicable sanctions, export-control, payment, and other legal-compliance requirements. Nationality alone is not an automatic exclusion criterion. An application may be declined, limited, or paused where applicable law or sanctions prohibit or materially restrict participation, payments, access, or services.

This policy must be localized by meaning, not by literal word substitution. Future Academy translations must preserve the program-specific language requirement and the neutral compliance rule.
