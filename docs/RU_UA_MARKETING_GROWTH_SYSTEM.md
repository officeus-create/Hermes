# Hermes Marketing / ProgressoPro — Russian- and Ukrainian-Language International Growth System

Purpose: research and serve Russian- and Ukrainian-speaking business owners in eligible international markets without targeting ethnicity, assuming wealth, or mass-generating country/city pages.

## 1. Audience definition

The audience is defined by:

- preferred working language: Russian or Ukrainian;
- ownership, management, or decision-making role in a real business;
- need for a website, SEO, Local SEO, social media marketing, lead generation, CRM, or digital operating system;
- ability to work legally and commercially with Hermes;
- a market where Hermes can provide a useful service and receive payment through an approved method.

Do not target people because they are presumed wealthy, because of nationality, or because they live near affluent residents. Use business need, language, niche, market, and service fit.

## 3. Language-first market score

Score 0–10:

- Russian/Ukrainian business-language demand: 0–2;
- relevant business density and commercial fit: 0–2;
- weak competition for the exact niche/service query: 0–2;
- unique local and language value available: 0–2;
- internal link, CTA, and response-language capability: 0–1;
- approved evidence source and review date: 0–1.

Decision:

- 0–4 reject;
- 5–6 research only;
- 7–8 content brief;
- 9–10 pilot implementation after compliance review.

## 7. Country, payment, sanctions, and compliance gate

A market cannot move beyond research until the following are verified and dated:

- Hermes can legally offer and receive payment for the service;
- payment method is available to both parties;
- sanctions/export-control restrictions have been reviewed where relevant;
- no prohibited territory or counterparty is targeted;
- data/privacy requirements are understood for the planned intake;
- the service language can be supported;
- local office/address claims are not invented;
- taxes, refunds, contract entity, and invoicing are defined before public pricing.

Do not use labels such as “terrorist country” in public documentation. Use a neutral eligibility/compliance status: `eligible`, `requires_review`, or `excluded`.

## 8. Multilingual client intake checklist

Collect:

- preferred coordination language: Russian or Ukrainian;
- company name and public website;
- country, city, and actual customer market;
- business niche;
- services/products;
- target audience and languages;
- current website and social channels;
- current lead sources;
- main marketing problem;
- desired customer action;
- website/SEO/SMM/CRM scope interest;
- decision-maker role;
- examples/reference brands;
- timeline preference;
- access status for website, analytics, Search Console, ad accounts, and business profile;
- compliance or regulated-industry requirements.

No passwords, API keys, payment details, or unnecessary personal data are collected in a public form.

## 9. Russian-language CTA matrix

- Website: `Опишите бизнес, рынок, услуги и задачу, которую должен решать новый сайт.`
- Redesign: `Пришлите текущий сайт и объясните, какие страницы или обращения сейчас не работают.`
- SEO: `Укажите страну, город, нишу, приоритетные услуги и текущую поисковую проблему.`
- Local SEO: `Опишите реальную зону обслуживания и статус Google Business Profile.`
- Social media: `Покажите текущие каналы, аудиторию, контент и коммерческую цель.`
- CRM/automation: `Опишите путь клиента от первого обращения до оплаты и повторной продажи.`

## 10. Ukrainian-language CTA matrix

- Website: `Опишіть бізнес, ринок, послуги та завдання, яке має вирішувати новий сайт.`
- Redesign: `Надішліть поточний сайт і поясніть, які сторінки або звернення зараз не працюють.`
- SEO: `Укажіть країну, місто, нішу, пріоритетні послуги та поточну пошукову проблему.`
- Local SEO: `Опишіть реальну зону обслуговування та статус Google Business Profile.`
- Social media: `Покажіть поточні канали, аудиторію, контент і комерційну мету.`
- CRM/automation: `Опишіть шлях клієнта від першого звернення до оплати та повторного продажу.`

Every CTA begins discovery. It does not confirm scope, price, rankings, traffic, leads, sales, or project timing.

## 11. Page architecture rule

Start with one Russian hub and one Ukrainian hub only after service facts and compliance are approved. Add a niche or city page only when:

- a validated query family exists;
- local businesses and commercial demand exist;
- results are weak for the exact intent;
- unique local/niche content is available;
- response language and market-language delivery are clear;
- the page scores at least 7/10;
- the page has a crawlable path from the relevant marketing/technology hub.

Never create `language × country × city × service` combinations automatically.
