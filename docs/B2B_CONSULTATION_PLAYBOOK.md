# Hermes Connect — 15-Minute B2B Consultation & Conversion Playbook

This playbook provides a structured, highly converting 15-minute consultation script for Hermes Connect account executives and product managers when speaking with SaaS founders, marketplace owners, and local business directors brought in via cold outreach campaigns.

---

## 🎯 Consultation Objectives
1. **Uncover Core Bottlenecks**: High latency, lost booking intent, empty appointment slots, or manual ops strain.
2. **Demonstrate Instant Value**: Show the live Hermes Connect Web V1.1 Operating System and Composio MCP integrations.
3. **Close on Onboarding**: Convert the lead into an active Hermes Connect user, white-label partner, or ecosystem module contributor.

---

## ⏱️ 15-Minute Timeline Breakdown

### Minute 0:00 – 0:02 | Rapport & Context (The Hook)
* **Goal**: Confirm the founder's pain point without sounding pitchy.
* **Script**:
  > *"Hi [Founder Name], thanks for jumping on. I’ve been following [App Name] on GitHub/Reddit and love how clean your booking flow is. As we mentioned in our note, most independent platforms struggle with response latency and passive calendars. Today I want to spend 12 minutes showing you how Hermes Connect injects an autonomous AI layer into existing platforms so you never lose booking intent."*

---

### Minute 0:02 – 0:06 | Discovery & Qualification (3 Key Questions)
* **Goal**: Quantify their lead loss and ops load.
* **Questions**:
  1. *"When a client submits a booking request or inquiry on [App Name], what is your average time to response during peak hours?"*
  2. *"How do you currently handle re-booking for clients whose regular 4-week service interval is expiring?"*
  3. *"If an AI agent could automatically handle 90%+ of your initial DMs and auto-sync slots to Google/Yelp, how much staff time would that save you weekly?"*

---

### Minute 0:06 – 0:11 | Live Product Walkthrough (The Wow Moment)
* **Goal**: Share screen and show **Hermes Connect Web V1.1 OS Command Center** and **AWS AI Sales Roleplay**.
* **Flow**:
  1. Open `http://localhost:3000/` ➔ Click **«Центр ОС V1.1»**.
  2. Show **Inbox Module**: Demonstrate how the AI Receptionist responds to Instagram/WhatsApp DMs in 1.2 seconds with free slot suggestions.
  3. Show **Agent Swarm & Composio MCP**: Highlight the 1000+ app integrations (Gmail, Stripe, Slack, Notion) working in background.
  4. Show **AWS AI Sales Simulator**: Demonstrate how their sales team can practice objection handling with real-time AI scoring.

---

### Minute 0:11 – 0:15 | Proposal & Next Steps (Closing the Call)
* **Goal**: Provide a clear 2-path closing offer.
* **Option A (Direct Onboarding)**:
  > *"We can set up a partner account for [App Name] on Hermes Connect today with 1-click adaptive onboarding. You get our full AI Receptionist & Scheduling infrastructure."*
* **Option B (Bento Grid Module Partnership)**:
  > *"If you want to offer Hermes Connect as a native AI add-on to your existing user base, we can map [App Name] as a core Bento cell via Composio MCP."*

---

## 🛡️ Handling Common Objections

| Objection | Recommended Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Will AI make mistakes or alienate clients?"** | *"Hermes Connect uses Guardrail UX — every action is logged in real-time with a 1-click manual override button. You stay in 100% control."* |
| **"How hard is integration?"** | *"Zero heavy coding. Via Composio Connect MCP, we link your existing tools (Slack, Gmail, Stripe) in under 5 minutes."* |
| **"What is the cost structure?"** | *"We offer a risk-free 14-day trial, after which pricing scales based on active booking volume."* |
