## Current product truth

`/services/hermes-connect/repair-shops/` currently supports:
- public services and shop profile;
- online appointment requests;
- weekly availability;
- booking status workflow;
- customer and vehicle context;
- browser-first owner workspace;
- paid Founding Shop Plan.

It explicitly does **not** present Hermes Connect as a full repair-order, inventory, accounting, payroll, payment-processing or broad shop-management suite.

## SERP fit

Fresh SERP samples show:
- `auto repair scheduling software` is led by scheduling/product pages emphasizing online booking, calendars, availability and customer appointment flows — a truthful fit for Hermes Connect;
- `auto repair CRM` commonly expects reminders, retention, customer history and broader workflow. Hermes can discuss customer/vehicle context and booking history but must retain the CRM-lite boundary;
- `truck repair shop software` is dominated by heavy-duty management platforms promising work orders, technicians, parts/inventory, estimates, invoices and payments. A Hermes truck-software owner would overstate current capability;
- `SEO for auto repair shops` and `auto repair marketing agency` results include specialist service pages selling Local SEO, website, PPC and customer-acquisition work. This is a genuine Hermes Marketing intent;
- `auto repair website design` is a distinct website-development purchase decision and should not be forced into the software owner.

## Repair prospect segmentation

Use existing prospect data as a sales source, not as automatic public-page truth.

Tier A:

- diesel repair;

- mobile truck repair.

Tier B:
- trailer repair;

- DOT inspection;
- fleet maintenance;
- roadside service.

Tier C:
- general independent auto repair;
- collision/body;
- rental/leasing/fleet services.

Every segment can be qualified for Website + SEO/GEO + Ads/SMM. Hermes Connect is offered only where its current booking/scheduling/customer-context workflow is a fit.

## Stop rules

- Do not create fake local repair shops or claim prospect businesses are Hermes customers.
- Do not automatically publish prospect spreadsheets into Hermes Catalog.
- Do not use local consumer search volume as proof that shop owners search for Hermes services.
- Do not create truck/diesel management-software pages until the product can support the SERP's core expected workflows.
- Do not create a separate page for every service, city, vehicle make, or ZIP code.
