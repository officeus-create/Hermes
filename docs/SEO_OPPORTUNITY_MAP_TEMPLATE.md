# SEO Opportunity Map Template

Use one row per keyword/location/audience opportunity.

| Direction | Query cluster | Country | State/region | City/ZIP/neighborhood | Language | Audience | Demand evidence | Competition evidence | Hermes evidence | Business fit | Conversion action | Unique content source | Effort | Priority score | Pilot status | Snapshot dates | Result | Next decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Direction-specific evidence

### Logistics

Include route-history frequency, pickup/delivery ZIPs, lane relevance, equipment type, FMCSA/authority intent, and whether Hermes can serve the carrier or owner-operator.

### Marketing

Include business category, current website/social weakness, platform need, local competitors, language, and whether ProgressoPro can create a specific offer.

### Academy

Include language, English level, student/career-change intent, program fit, hiring need, mentor capacity, and realistic next role.

### IT

Include business niche, website/SEO/automation gap, local competition, available demo/case evidence, and project-value potential.

## Decision labels

- `RESEARCH` — evidence incomplete.
- `PILOT_READY` — demand, fit, competition, and unique value confirmed.
- `BUILDING` — active branch exists.
- `PR_OPEN` — implementation is under review.
- `MEASURING` — production release is collecting evidence.
- `SCALE` — pilot met the success threshold.
- `ITERATE` — promising but needs a stronger page/offer.
- `STOP` — insufficient demand, fit, or conversion.

Do not assign `PILOT_READY` only because SERP competition appears weak. Demand, business fit, unique value, and a conversion path are mandatory.
