# Hermes Unified Brand System V4

Status: **CANONICAL / CURRENT**  
Owner: Hermes Brand / Product / Website  
Design governance mirror: Google Drive `HERMES DESIGN CONSTITUTION V4 — CURRENT`

## One Hermes / Four Visual Engines

Hermes is one technology ecosystem. Logistics, Marketing, Technology and Academy are not four unrelated websites and must not become four copies of the same template with different accent colors.

Target relationship:

- roughly 70–80% shared Hermes DNA;
- roughly 20–30% direction-specific visual character;
- direction character comes primarily from **content, motion, data, imagery and interaction physics**, not page paint.

Hermes Connect, Catalog, Insights, Load Board, Website Factory and future verified products inherit this system. They are products or discovery surfaces, not additional business directions.

## 1. Shared foundation

### Light-first public and product surfaces

Primary public and normal operational surfaces use a bright, readable foundation:

- Pearl: `#F7F6F3`
- Paper: `#FFFFFF`
- Cloud: `#EEF3F7`
- Cloud Strong: `#E4EDF5`
- Ink Navy / Focus Ink: `#172033`
- Muted: `#666C79`
- Action Blue: `#1A73E8`
- Intelligence Violet: `#7C5CFF`
- Ocean: `#1E88FF`

Large black or near-black page shells are not the default Hermes language. Dense software remains readable through hierarchy, grouping, borders, spacing and typography rather than darkness alone.

### Bounded Focus Ink

Focus Ink `#172033` may be used as a contained functional surface when it carries real meaning:

- route/map/control-room visual;
- architecture/system/code scene;
- Hermes Intelligence sequence;
- media scrim;
- product preview where contrast materially helps comprehension.

Normal target on a public page: one bounded focus scene, commonly 5–15% of visual area. Do not use consecutive dark sections or dark long-form reading surfaces.

## 2. Direction signals

Direction colors are orientation signals, not four independent themes:

- Logistics: `#1E88FF`
- Marketing: `#00C853`
- Technology: `#FF7A00`
- Academy: `#7C5CFF`

They may identify current direction, active navigation, small chart/detail accents or a bounded local cue.

Do not turn the full page, header, footer, card family or typography system into the direction color.

Semantic state colors remain separate from direction identity.

## 3. Four Visual Engines

### Logistics — Spatial Operations

**Visual physics:** movement through space.

Prefer:

- roads, yards, routes and maps;
- equipment/capacity/ETA/status;
- movement from origin to destination;
- truthful operational sequence;
- real map/data layers when available.

A signature scene may combine aerial/spatial context with restrained operational overlays.

Never invent live vehicles, loads, rates, capacity, demand or telemetry.

### Marketing — Attention & Demand

**Visual physics:** movement of attention.

Prefer:

- content and channel assets;
- search/SERP fragments;
- social distribution;
- traffic/site actions;
- lead and evidence flows;
- measurable campaign or organic-distribution states when real.

The visual story should explain how attention becomes an attributable business action.

Never use random social-logo confetti or fabricate reach, engagement, leads or revenue.

### Technology — Digital Systems

**Visual physics:** movement of information.

Prefer:

- source → context → API/workflow → result;
- Website Factory;
- connectors;
- real product UI;
- architecture and data flow;
- code or terminal only when it explains a real system;
- build/test/deploy/evidence states.

Technology receives the strongest bounded-dark allowance because architecture/code/system scenes can benefit from it. The public sales layer remains light.

Never use generic cyberpunk, meaningless terminal output or fake autonomy.

### Academy — Human Progress

**Visual physics:** movement of capability.

Prefer:

- Student → Trainee → Specialist → Manager → leadership progression;
- lesson/practice/review;
- call or exercise feedback;
- scores only when real or clearly sample;
- mentor/human review;
- people and real learning context.

HeyGen or other presenter tools may support explanation and localization, but should not replace human credibility.

Never use generic graduation-stock identity or imply certification/results that do not exist.

## 4. Home — Hermes World

Home establishes one Hermes ecosystem before asking a visitor to choose a direction.

Desktop may use one coherent living composition in which all four visual engines are represented:

- spatial movement;
- attention movement;
- information movement;
- capability movement.

Mobile must simplify this into four large clear actions rather than shrinking an immersive desktop scene into unreadable decoration.

The direction markers introduced by the site-navigation pillar system are retained as orientation cues. They are not the visual identity of the four businesses.

## 5. Typography

Use the current self-hosted stack:

- Display: Manrope
- Body: Source Sans 3
- Metadata / technical labels: IBM Plex Mono

Normal body copy:

- desktop target around 17px;
- mobile minimum 16px where practical;
- dense operational metadata may be smaller only when genuinely secondary and readable.

Tiny 10–13px body copy is not a premium-design technique.

Hierarchy must remain obvious without color.

## 6. Shape, surfaces and glass

Default geometry:

- compact controls: 10–12px radius;
- public cards: about 22px;
- large product/workspace panels: 28–30px;
- primary/secondary CTA may use pill geometry where appropriate.

Elevation is subtle.

Glass is a signature material, not a universal component style. It is appropriate for selected overlays, the Hermes Intelligence object or bounded hero treatment, not every card/form/table.

## 7. Hermes Intelligence

Hermes uses one ownable Intelligence geometry / Knot / Core rather than unrelated third-party AI iconography.

The object may represent genuine states such as:

- idle;
- listening;
- processing;
- connected;
- action ready;
- success;
- attention/error.

Only map visual states to real application states. Never fake autonomous processing.

A stateful vector/runtime such as Rive may be evaluated for this object; deterministic SVG/CSS/GSAP remains the fallback. Runtime choice must pass license, bundle, mobile, reduced-motion and fallback review.

## 8. Motion and signature-scene budget

Motion explains a relationship, state or movement. It does not exist to prove that the page can animate.

Normal rule:

- one major signature motion/media scene per page;
- small functional micro-interactions elsewhere;
- after the signature scene, composition becomes calmer.

Avoid simultaneous unrelated parallax, particles, glow, video, 3D and cursor effects.

Baseline implementation:

1. CSS / SVG / native Web APIs first;
2. existing lightweight Hermes motion patterns for ordinary reveal/state;
3. GSAP or another bounded runtime for a named cinematic requirement;
4. isolated 3D only when 2D/SVG cannot express the concept.

Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 9. Product/service pages demonstrate real Hermes capability

The strongest Hermes pages should show sanitized, truthful evidence of systems the company actually builds.

For Web Design / Website Development, the preferred public narrative is:

`business/source intake → Website Factory brief/concept → design system/responsive build → tests/visual QA → SEO/GEO architecture → approved distribution → site action/CRM evidence`

Rules:

- Website Factory private owner data remains private and `noindex,nofollow`;
- public demonstrations use sanitized/sample data and explicit DEMO/SAMPLE labels;
- screenshots/UI must match current product truth;
- no private customer data, secrets or unsupported capability claims;
- product existence does not imply customer adoption or successful business outcome.

Marketing may similarly demonstrate current social distribution/connectors only when the connected state is real and authorized.

## 10. Insights, news and social distribution

Substantial original stories, releases, guides and evidence-backed news should have a canonical searchable owner on the website/Insights layer when they merit indexation.

Preferred flow:

`SOURCE FACT / RESULT / RELEASE → canonical Website/Insights owner → structured metadata/media → channel-native Threads/Instagram/Facebook/Telegram variants → attributed return path → CRM/qualification evidence`

Social channels are distribution/adaptation layers, not competing canonical archives.

Do not create thin indexable “news” pages solely because automation can publish frequently.

Automation may draft or distribute; consequential factual claims and publication truth remain evidence-bounded and owner/human controlled.

## 11. SEO / GEO / AEO by design

Every visual page must remain understandable and crawlable without its decorative runtime.

Non-negotiable:

- server-rendered semantic H1, promise, entity/context, important offer facts and primary links;
- essential content is not fetched only after interaction;
- progressive disclosure keeps the real text in markup;
- semantic headings, internal links and useful anchors remain;
- imagery uses useful alt text;
- video uses poster/captions/transcript where relevant;
- VideoObject or other schema is added only for real media with truthful metadata;
- motion/3D/maps are lazy-loaded when non-critical and should not own LCP on ordinary search pages;
- canonical, hreflang, schema, sitemap, robots, internal links and analytics consent remain correct;
- local/language pages need full owner-quality localization and unique intent/evidence.

Discoverability and correctness outrank visual novelty.

## 12. Tool roles

One tool owns each responsibility:

- Drive / One Brain: design governance and business-readable decisions;
- GitHub current main: implementation truth;
- Figma: component/system design and master visual frames;
- tldraw: fast architecture, wireframes and annotated review;
- prototype builders: bounded exploration only;
- AI image/video providers: asset ideation/production, not runtime truth;
- Canva: channel adaptations;
- Descript: editing/captions/localization;
- PostHog / approved analytics: behavior evidence;
- Playwright + exact-head screenshots + production readback: delivery evidence.

Do not create a second page-builder owner, analytics owner or design constitution.

## 13. Paid-provider and open-source fallback

No production page may require an active paid-generative subscription or remaining credits at runtime.

When a provider is unavailable:

1. reuse approved Hermes-owned assets;
2. use deterministic HTML/CSS/SVG/native Web APIs;
3. use a maintained open-source runtime when the effect has a named purpose;
4. use local/offline generation only after verifying the asset/model license, then store approved output under Hermes control.

Candidate toolbox by need:

- geospatial/data: MapLibre GL JS + deck.gl;
- ordinary motion: native Web Animations / Motion-class runtime;
- deterministic cinematic timelines: existing Hermes GSAP/HyperFrames capability where appropriate;
- interactive brand state: Rive runtime or SVG/GSAP fallback;
- microanimation: dotLottie / CSS / SVG;
- isolated 3D: Three.js only for a proven need;
- local media fallback: ComfyUI-style workflow with separately license-verified models/assets.

Before adding a dependency verify:

- commercial-use license;
- maintenance/activity;
- bundle/render cost;
- mobile and Safari behavior;
- accessibility and reduced-motion fallback;
- whether native CSS/SVG can already solve it.

## 14. Progressive disclosure and content depth

Keep the decision layer visible:

- H1;
- one-sentence promise;
- who it is for;
- 3–5 outcomes;
- proof/status cue;
- material pricing/product truth;
- primary next action.

Deeper services, methodology, FAQs and supporting SEO content may disclose progressively, but remain present in semantic markup.

No accordion-inside-accordion patterns.

## 15. Accessibility and performance

Required representative QA widths:

- 390;
- 430;
- 768;
- 1024;
- 1440.

Requirements:

- no horizontal overflow;
- visible focus;
- 44px touch targets where applicable;
- WCAG 2.2 AA target;
- reduced-motion fallback;
- mobile-specific composition;
- no heavy continuous 3D requirement on mobile;
- no material LCP/CLS regression outside current performance budgets;
- non-critical media/motion lazy-loads.

## 16. Permanent anti-patterns

Do not:

- build four near-identical pages differentiated mainly by accent color;
- build four unrelated sub-brands;
- make the header the main spectacle;
- use full dark shells by default;
- use generic AI planets/spheres/cyberpunk as identity;
- use stock-photo hero as the only proof;
- use tiny text to look technical;
- use multiple unrelated signature animations on one page;
- duplicate headers, button systems or analytics owners;
- hide search-critical content in client-only runtime;
- ship fake metrics/live states/integrations;
- let builder/provider chrome or style leak into Hermes production;
- redesign the whole repository in one PR.

## 17. Migration order

Do not migrate the whole site at once.

Preferred order:

1. reconcile V4 governance across Drive and GitHub;
2. build one Home master scene + one Logistics master scene;
3. verify mobile/performance/SEO/GEO/accessibility;
4. derive Marketing, Technology and Academy from the same shared shell;
5. apply the system to Web Design / Website Development so real Website Factory, QA, SEO/GEO and distribution technology becomes product proof;
6. migrate remaining page families incrementally.

## 18. Product truth

No Lorem Ipsum, leaked token names, fake “live” data, invented results, ratings, awards, integrations or autonomous actions.

Demo/sample states must be labeled.

A beautiful interface is not evidence that the underlying capability exists.


## 19. Public capability showroom

Not every page receives the same visual budget.

Use page intent before choosing media or motion:

- **Identity / Home** — establish one Hermes world and route the visitor; one ecosystem scene, low technical detail.
- **Service / sales** — explain offer, fit, proof and next action; a signature scene is justified only when it proves the service.
- **Capability showroom** — sanitized interactive proof is primary material. Web Design, Website Development and selected Technology pages belong here.
- **Product** — real UI/workflow truth first; effects remain subordinate to usability.
- **Editorial / Insights / News** — reading, evidence, source context, internal links and discoverability first; motion stays minimal.
- **Utility / auth / contact / legal** — clarity, trust, accessibility and speed; no spectacle.

Capability-showroom material may include sanitized representations of Website Factory intake/brief logic, responsive component behavior, build/test/visual-QA states, SEO/GEO/entity/internal-link architecture, social distribution/connectors and CRM/lead routing.

Private owner data, credentials, customer records and private Website Factory drafts never become public proof.

## 20. Evidence-state grammar

Any visual that could be mistaken for real operational state must expose an understandable maturity label:

- **LIVE** — current authorized production behavior verified by fresh evidence.
- **VERIFIED** — capability exists and is proven, while the visual is a sanitized representation rather than a live feed.
- **DEMO** — working sample using synthetic or sanitized data.
- **CONCEPT** — design/roadmap explanation, not a current capability.

Animation, pulses, timestamps, counters, maps, social metrics or AI-processing visuals must never imply a stronger maturity state than the evidence supports.

## 21. Connected content-to-client loop

The website, news/Insights layer, social distribution and CRM are one system:

`source fact / result / release → canonical Website/Insights owner → structured media + schema/internal links → channel-native Threads/Instagram/Facebook/Telegram derivative → attributed return path → site action → receiver/CRM → human qualification → opportunity/revenue evidence`

Design implications:

- searchable depth belongs to the canonical website owner;
- social is adaptation/distribution, not a competing archive;
- each distributable page should have reusable Open Graph/social assets where useful;
- attribution/UTM must survive the return path;
- current authorized connector behavior may become Technology/Marketing proof;
- thin indexable “news” pages must not be generated merely to create activity.

## 22. Adaptive richness and design measurement

A signature scene has quality tiers:

1. **Desktop rich** — fullest approved interactive/cinematic treatment.
2. **Tablet/mobile** — simplified composition preserving meaning and CTA priority.
3. **Reduced-motion / low-power** — static or low-motion equivalent.
4. **Crawler / no-JS** — complete semantic business truth and internal links.

Where privacy rules permit, existing approved analytics/PostHog may measure meaningful interaction such as scene reached, proof opened, demo engaged, CTA selected and disclosure expanded.

Measurement helps decide whether a treatment improves comprehension or conversion. It does not replace brand judgment.

Approved generated media should be stored under Hermes control where licensing permits. No public route hot-depends on a generation provider.


## Canonical implementation source

Semantic tokens live in `src/styles/hermes-brand-system.css`.

Legacy variables remain compatibility inputs and must be migrated incrementally rather than globally redefined in one risky change.
