## Scoring model

- **9–10:** clear commercial path, useful proof, low friction, and measured production evidence.
- **7–8:** clear path and action, but proof, reputation, measurement, or exception detail remains limited.
- **5–6:** useful information exists, but trust, role ownership, or next-step certainty is incomplete.
- **3–4:** visitor is likely to depend on manual clarification or leave before a qualified action.
- **1–2:** no credible task path.

Status labels:

- `VERIFIED_REPOSITORY` — directly supported by current routes, components, tests, or public copy.
- `PRODUCTION_EVIDENCE` — backed by current external smoke or delivery evidence.
- `OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED` — requires a real person, case, process, commercial term, account, or source.
- `FIELD_DATA_REQUIRED` — requires production analytics, Search Console, CrUX/Lighthouse field data, or real user testing.
