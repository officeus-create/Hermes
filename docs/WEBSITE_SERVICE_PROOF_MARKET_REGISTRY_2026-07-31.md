# Website Service, Proof, and U.S. Market Registry

Date: 2026-07-31  
Tracking: Issue #20, draft PR #19, queue tasks 171–180

## Purpose

Define national website, redesign, multilingual, SEO, Local SEO, CRM, and automation scope before any new service or geographic page is published.

This registry also freezes the current public proof labels, defines the evidence required for a verified case study, and applies a 7/10 evidence gate to future U.S. niche/location research.

## National service scope

Approved research clusters:

1. National website creation
2. Website redesign
3. Multilingual website foundations
4. SEO services
5. Local SEO services
6. CRM and automation planning

Every cluster separates:

- included capabilities;
- optional work requiring a reviewed agreement;
- excluded or prohibited claims;
- the human/owner control boundary.

No cluster guarantees rankings, traffic, leads, revenue, ROI, completion dates, bookings, payments, integrations, or autonomous production actions.

## Current proof register

- Hermes Website Platform — `live_product` with the public website case study.
- CRM & Operations Control — `working_prototype` with demo data, not a connected production CRM.
- Hermes Connect — `product_discovery`; no live account, booking, calendar, payment, or AI action.
- Hermes Load Board — `working_prototype`; demo data and no live external-board sync, offer, truck, dispatch, or booking.

A maturity label is not a customer result. Prototype/demo metrics, loads, rates, names, records, or interface labels cannot be presented as production facts.

## Verified case-study standard

A verified case study requires:

- subject identity or approved anonymization;
- date range;
- baseline and measurement source;
- work performed;
- measured result;
- factors outside Hermes control;
- permission status;
- reviewer and review date.

Rankings, traffic, leads, revenue, ROI, percentages, customer identity, and time-to-result cannot be published without this evidence.

## U.S. niche/location research score

Maximum: 10 points.

- Relevant business density: 0–2
- Competition/result-page gap: 0–2
- Demand and service fit: 0–2
- Unique niche/local value: 0–2
- Internal link and conversion path: 0–1
- Dated evidence and review: 0–1

Editorial-review threshold: 7/10.

A market record also requires a real city/state, niche, approved national service cluster, query-family research, source IDs, review date, approved proof reference, at least three unique content angles, confirmed response capacity, and privacy/claims review.

Synthetic records remain `research_only` even at 10/10. Missing evidence is blocked. The highest state is `eligible_for_editorial_review`.

## Publication order

1. Confirm national service scope.
2. Confirm proof and case-study evidence.
3. Build/review the national service hub.
4. Collect dated U.S. niche/location evidence.
5. Score the market.
6. Prepare one pilot brief only at 7/10 or higher.
7. Run route, content, SEO, claims, and conversion tests.
8. Request separate owner approval for merge/deploy.
9. Measure before expanding.

The registry cannot create a city page, national hub, sitemap URL, schema, Search Console record, or public claim by itself.
