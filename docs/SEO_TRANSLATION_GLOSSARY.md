# Hermes Multilingual SEO Glossary

Purpose: keep public terminology consistent across English, Ukrainian, Russian, Spanish, French, and Italian pages while preserving industry language that carriers and logistics professionals actually use.

## Usage rules

1. Use the localized term in headings and explanatory copy when it is natural and unambiguous.
2. Keep the established English industry term in parentheses on first use when candidates, carriers, brokers, or U.S. operators are likely to search for it in English.
3. Brand names remain unchanged: Hermes Logistics, ProgressoPro, Hermes Business Academy, Hermes IT Development, Hermes Connect.
4. MC, USDOT/DOT, BOL, POD, COI, W-9, VIN, API, CRM, GA4, SEO, AI, FAQ, CTA and KPI remain uppercase abbreviations. Explain them where the audience may not know them.
5. Do not translate route or authority identifiers.
6. Do not use automatic literal translation when it changes the operational meaning.
7. Ukrainian public locale uses `uk` in language metadata and `/ua/` in the public URL structure.

## Prohibited translation shortcuts

- Do not translate “carrier” as courier when discussing licensed motor carriers.
- Do not translate “load board” as warehouse board.
- Do not translate “authority” as personal authority or management power.
- Do not translate “lane” literally as a traffic lane in pricing or freight-development content.
- Do not call a preliminary request a booking, shipment confirmation, carrier approval, or account creation.
- Do not translate “dispatch” into emergency dispatch language.
- Do not use “guaranteed,” “best,” “cheapest,” “instant,” or “nationwide fleet” unless the exact statement is approved and evidenced.
