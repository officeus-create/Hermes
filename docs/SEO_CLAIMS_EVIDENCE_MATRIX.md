# Hermes SEO Claims and Evidence Matrix

## Purpose

Prevent unsupported public claims while allowing verified achievements, program details, and hiring opportunities to be used confidently in SEO content.

Every material claim proposed for Academy, Careers, Logistics, Marketing, Sales, Operations, or localized pages should be recorded here or in an equivalent structured source before publication.

## Status definitions

- `VERIFIED_PUBLIC` — supported by a public source that can be cited or linked.
- `VERIFIED_INTERNAL` — supported by internal records but not automatically suitable for public publication.
- `OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE` — owner approves the wording, but the supporting evidence still needs to be attached or recorded.
- `CURRENT_OPERATIONAL_FACT` — true now but likely to change; requires a checked date and owner/operations confirmation.
- `PLACEHOLDER_DO_NOT_PUBLISH` — idea or historical statement without sufficient support.
- `REJECTED` — should not be used publicly.

## Publication rules

1. Numeric business claims require a source, date range, scope, and explanation of what was measured.
2. Training details that can change require a `last checked` date.
3. Employment language must distinguish opportunity, eligibility, selection, and guarantee.
4. Candidate results cannot be generalized from one case.
5. Historical achievements must be dated and must not be written as current capability without confirmation.
6. Internal client, employee, candidate, carrier, broker, or revenue information must not be exposed.
7. Screenshots and analytics exports must be checked for personal or confidential information before use.

## Evidence package template

For each claim approved for publication, attach or record:

- claim ID;
- final wording;
- page(s) where it will appear;
- source owner;
- source location;
- source date;
- measurement period;
- geography;
- organic vs paid contribution where relevant;
- whether the result represents one case or a repeatable operational metric;
- privacy review completed;
- owner approval date;
- next review date.

## Case-study minimum standard

A publishable case study should answer:

1. What was the starting point?
2. What exact work did Hermes/ProgressoPro perform?
3. Over what period?
4. What was organic and what was paid?
5. What spend was involved?
6. Which metric improved?
7. How was the metric measured?
8. What external factors may have contributed?
9. What should not be generalized?
10. Is the client or project identity approved for publication?

## Operational review cadence

- Current program, price, schedule, hiring, mentor, and eligibility facts: review monthly or before each campaign.
- Case-study and historical claims: review every six months.
- Organization/contact/legal facts: review whenever the underlying business changes.
- Search snippets and schema offers: update immediately when terms change.

## Immediate owner-data queue

The SEO team needs confirmation or evidence for:

1. Official Academy brand name.
2. Programs open now.
3. Roles actively hiring now.
4. Current learning formats and schedules.
5. English requirements.
6. Current price and special-access rules.
7. Whether practice uses simulations, internal workflows, or real external communication.
8. Current mentor structure.
9. Approved marketing analytics cases.
10. Approved logistics scale/history claims.
11. Current countries and applicant restrictions.
12. Exact meaning of employment, contractor, partnership, and regional-office opportunities.

Until these are confirmed, content briefs should use conditional, non-guaranteed wording and avoid unsupported numbers.
