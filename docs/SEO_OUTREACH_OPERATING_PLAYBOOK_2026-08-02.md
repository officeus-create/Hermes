# SEO Outreach Operating Playbook

## Revenue purpose

Use external authority to support three canonical commercial owners:

1. Car hauling dispatch: `/logistics/car-hauling-dispatch/`
2. Website development: `/services/website-development/`
3. SEO services: `/services/seo/`

The goal is not raw backlink volume. The goal is relevant visibility, referral visits, branded search, qualified inquiries, partnerships, and editorial links earned through useful assets and real expertise.

## Allowed outreach types

- unlinked Hermes mention reclamation;
- competitor backlink-gap outreach;
- broken-link replacement with a relevant Hermes resource;
- expert commentary for real publications;
- podcast and interview participation;
- carrier, dealer, service-provider, and technology partnerships;
- case-study collaborations with explicit permission;
- directory/profile submissions where the profile is legitimate and accurate;
- editorial guest contributions on real topical sites;
- promotion of free checklists, templates, comparison pages, and worksheets.

## Prohibited practices

- bulk purchased guest posts;
- private blog networks;
- automated mass outreach with irrelevant copy;
- exact-match anchor quotas;
- fake reviews or reciprocal review schemes;
- virtual-office citations presented as physical locations;
- paid links without appropriate `rel="sponsored"` or equivalent disclosure;
- monthly disavow uploads without a documented artificial-link problem or manual action.

## CRM workflow

Every target must be recorded in `docs/seo-outreach-crm-template.csv` before contact.

Required stages:

1. `prospect`
2. `qualified`
3. `contacted`
4. `replied`
5. `negotiating`
6. `placed`
7. `declined`
8. `follow_up_later`

A prospect is qualified only when:

- the site has a real audience relevant to one commercial direction;
- the proposed Hermes asset solves a genuine reader problem;
- the target page and anchor can be natural;
- the site is not primarily selling links;
- the opportunity does not require misleading company, location, or performance claims.

## Anchor policy

Preferred distribution:

- brand: `Hermes Logistics`, `Hermes`;
- URL or page title;
- natural descriptive phrases;
- author/expert name when appropriate;
- occasional partial-match wording only when editorially natural.

Do not manage a fixed exact-match percentage. Review anchors monthly for obvious concentration or manipulation risk.

## Weekly operating cadence

### Monday

- add 15–25 researched prospects across all three directions;
- review new and lost links;
- identify unlinked mentions;
- select one asset for promotion.

### Tuesday–Thursday

- send 5–10 personalized outreach messages per day;
- respond to editors and partners;
- prepare expert comments or contribution outlines;
- record every action and next date in the CRM.

### Friday

- verify live placements;
- check link destination, anchor, and `rel` attribute;
- record referral traffic and qualified inquiries;
- update monthly totals and lessons.

## Measurement

Primary KPIs:

- qualified prospects added;
- personalized outreach sent;
- reply rate;
- positive reply rate;
- editorial placements;
- legitimate directory/profile completions;
- unlinked mentions converted;
- referral visits to canonical commercial pages;
- assisted CTA clicks;
- qualified inquiries influenced by referral or branded search.

Guardrails:

- zero paid undisclosed links;
- zero fake locations;
- zero fake reviews;
- zero PII in GA4;
- zero links to thin or duplicate pages;
- no outreach before the linked asset and commercial CTA are production-ready.

## First 30-day target

- 60 qualified prospects;
- 30 personalized contacts;
- 8 positive replies;
- 3 legitimate placements or profile completions;
- 2 partnership conversations;
- 1 unlinked mention reclaimed;
- first referral-to-CTA baseline established.
