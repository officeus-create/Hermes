- Directional paths or portal-like entrances are preferred over a literal transportation collage.

- IT Development covers CRM, websites, applications, booking, prepayments, automation, dashboards, and integrations.
- Academy uses Awareness -> Understanding -> Application and separates Logistics, Marketing, and COO paths.

## Required website elements

1. Four clear homepage entrances.
2. A direct CTA and next-page flow for every direction.
3. Service or program subdivisions on each business page.
4. Trust through process clarity and real operating context, not unsupported performance claims.
5. Responsive behavior and reduced-motion fallbacks.
6. Evidence classification for claims that are not directly verified.
7. Versioned assets and a traceable design decision log.

## Rejected or deferred

- Generic stock transport imagery as the central ecosystem concept.
- Airplanes, ships, and trucks combined into a literal hero collage.
- Generic course marketplace grids and graduation-cap imagery.
- Unsupported claims about employment, income, fleet size, students, or guaranteed results.
- Heavy 3D or motion that harms mobile performance or obscures navigation.
- Invented social accounts, contact addresses, testimonials, partner logos, or statistics.
