# Hermes website decision register

Updated: 2026-07-13

## Current architecture

- The homepage is the entry point for four first-class businesses: Hermes Logistics, ProgressoPro, Hermes Business Academy, and IT Development.
- Each business has its own page, audience, services, operating model, and CTA.
- Hermes Logistics includes freight dispatch, carrier back office, box truck and cargo van operations, and Car Hauling.
- The registered production domain is `hermeslogisticsus.com`.

## Approved direction

- A visitor should understand the four choices in 3-5 seconds.
- Premium editorial presentation with strong typography, generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, and restrained depth.
- Custom architectural system visuals replace generic stock imagery across all four business directions.
- Directional paths or portal-like entrances are preferred over a literal transportation collage.
- Mobile-first layouts, 44px interaction targets, reduced-motion support, and progressive disclosure.
- Every important page follows a decision journey: recognize the need, understand the offer, verify fit, take one next action.
- IT Development covers CRM, websites, applications, booking, prepayments, automation, dashboards, and integrations.
- Academy uses Awareness -> Understanding -> Application and separates Logistics, Marketing, and COO paths.

## Cross-AI synthesis

- Grok: strongest contribution is visual energy, depth, interaction, and contemporary references.
- DeepSeek: strongest contribution is information architecture and systematic program structure.
- NotebookLM: source grounding and separation of current decisions from historical or rejected ideas.
- ChatGPT: business framing, four-path decision architecture, content consolidation, and claim control.
- Claude: accessibility, risk review, clarity, and adversarial QA.
- Codex: implementation, responsive behavior, testing, evidence, deployment, and rollback.

## Required website elements

1. Four clear homepage entrances.
2. A direct CTA and next-page flow for every direction.
3. Service or program subdivisions on each business page.
4. Trust through process clarity and real operating context, not unsupported performance claims.
5. Responsive behavior and reduced-motion fallbacks.
6. Evidence classification for claims that are not directly verified.
7. Versioned assets and a traceable design decision log.

## Rejected or deferred

- Generic stock transport imagery as the central ecosystem concept.
- Airplanes, ships, and trucks combined into a literal hero collage.
- Generic course marketplace grids and graduation-cap imagery.
- Unsupported claims about employment, income, fleet size, students, or guaranteed results.
- Heavy 3D or motion that harms mobile performance or obscures navigation.
- Invented social accounts, contact addresses, testimonials, partner logos, or statistics.
