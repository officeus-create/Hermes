# Hermes Connect — Founder Outreach + 15-Minute Audit Playbook

Purpose: turn public product observations into high-trust founder conversations without unsupported claims or spammy positioning.

## Research rule before outreach

Every message must include at least one verifiable observation about the target product. Do not claim that a company has “zero customers,” loses “28–30% of leads,” takes “6–18 hours to respond,” pays “25% commission,” or has any other metric unless that specific claim is supported by a reliable source for that target.

Use this structure:

**Observed product detail → likely opportunity → relevant Hermes capability → low-friction invitation.**

# 15-Minute Founder Audit Script

## Minute 0–2 — Context and permission

Goal: show that the conversation is about their product, not a generic sales pitch.

Ask:
- “What are you trying to make this product become over the next 6–12 months?”
- “Which customer segment matters most right now?”
- “What is the hardest part today: acquisition, activation, retention, operations, or monetization?”

Do not demo Hermes yet.

## Minute 2–5 — Find the bottleneck

Choose only the questions relevant to the product:

- Where do most new users come from today?
- What has to happen before a new user reaches the first useful outcome?
- Which step still requires manual work from you or your team?
- Where do prospects usually stop responding?
- Which repeated customer request consumes the most support time?
- What information do you wish the system knew automatically?
- What software does the customer have to use alongside your product?

Output: one clearly stated bottleneck.

## Minute 5–8 — Map the Hermes workflow

Use one simple before/after flow.

Example:

**Before**
Lead → manual reply → missing information → provider / employee follow-up → booking / sale → manual reminder

**With Hermes layer**
Lead → Hermes qualifies → routes → follows up → books / creates next action → alerts human only when needed

Do not present every Hermes module.

## Minute 8–11 — Show one relevant prototype

Possible demos:
- Hermes Connect web command center;
- adaptive onboarding;
- Hermes Intelligence activity feed;
- Sales Coach roleplay;
- industry-specific workflow;
- integrations concept.

Demo rule: show the workflow that answers the bottleneck discovered in minutes 2–5.

## Minute 11–13 — Partnership path

Offer the smallest sensible next step:

### Integration
“We connect your product as a tool inside Hermes.”

### Pilot
“We build one workflow for one customer segment and measure it.”

### Distribution partnership
“We package your capability as a Hermes partner module and test demand.”

### Consulting
“We help redesign the acquisition / activation / retention flow first.”

Do not promise distribution volume or revenue that has not been validated.

## Minute 13–15 — Close

Ask one concrete question:

“If we mapped a pilot around [specific bottleneck], would you be willing to give us technical access / API documentation / one test customer / a follow-up call?”

Then define:
- owner;
- next action;
- deadline;
- success metric.
