# Hermes Connect — Founder Outreach & 15-Minute Strategy Audit Playbook

This document details our hyper-personalized B2B outreach methodology and the structured 15-minute consultation framework designed for founder acquisition and ecosystem partnerships.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Rule: Verified Observations over Unverified Statistics
To build maximum credibility with SaaS founders, indie hackers, and local service marketplace operators, **we do not use generic or unverified claims** (e.g., *"you lose 28% of leads"* or *"your response time is 6-18 hours"*).

Instead, every outreach communication follows the **4-Step Observation Rule**:
```
Verified Specific Observation ➔ Friction / Bottleneck Point ➔ Hermes Connect Solution ➔ 15-Min Strategy Audit Invitation
```
