# Beauty & Wellness Adaptive Onboarding Guide & CJM Scenario

This document defines the adaptive onboarding workflow and Customer Journey Map (CJM) for **Hermes Connect: Beauty & Wellness Edition**.

---

## 1. Core Onboarding Philosophy

Traditional salon management software (Altegio, Yclients, Fresha) forces users to fill out complex 20-field registration forms before showing any value. **Hermes Connect** replaces forms with a **conversational AI setup** focused on delivering the first "Aha-Moment" in under 60 seconds.

```
[ Step 1: One-Click Migration ] ➔ [ Step 2: Service & Master Mapping ] ➔ [ Step 3: No-Show Rule Policy ] ➔ [ Step 4: First Live AI Booking ]
```

---

## 2. Onboarding Steps & CJM Flow

### Step 1: One-Click Migration from Legacy Systems
- **User Action**: Paste Altegio / Fresha CSV export or website URL.
- **Hermes Intelligence Action**: Automatically parses master names, services, pricing, client database, and historical booking frequencies.
- **Aha-Moment**: Visualizing 100% of master schedules mapped onto the Pearl/Obsidian calendar grid instantly.

### Step 2: Dynamic Service & Commission Matrix
- **User Action**: Confirm or adjust staff commission tiers (e.g., Senior Stylist 40%, Junior 25%).
- **Hermes Intelligence Action**: Configures Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) so junior stylists only see their own appointments while salon owners see total shift revenue and payroll.

### Step 3: Automated No-Show Protection & Stripe Deposits
- **User Action**: Select cancellation policy (e.g., 24-hour notice, 30% late fee).
- **Hermes Intelligence Action**: Connects Stripe deposit holding and SMS/WhatsApp confirmation flow.

### Step 4: First Live AI Re-Booking Test
- **User Action**: Send test AI re-booking message to a simulated client with an expiring service cycle.
- **Hermes Intelligence Action**: Simulates a live WhatsApp response, instant appointment booking, and deposit collection.

---

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Time to First Value (TTFV)**: < 90 seconds.
- **No-Show Reduction Target**: -68% via automated SMS/WhatsApp deposits.
- **Smart Re-booking Conversion**: +24% increase in repeat client retention.
