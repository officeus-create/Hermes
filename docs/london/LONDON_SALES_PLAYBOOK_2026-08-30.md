# London Sales Playbook

## 81. Prospect seed-list method
Build the first 100 prospects as 10 groups × 10 businesses: auto repair, beauty, dental, restaurants, contractors, logistics, detailing, cleaning, home services, professional services. Store: business, niche, borough/area, website, Google profile, Instagram, contact route, visible bottleneck, audit date, status, next action. Do not scrape or store unnecessary personal data.

## 82. Free audit template
1. What does the business sell and to whom?
2. Is the offer obvious in 10 seconds?
3. Does Google show the right services and proof?
4. Does the website have a focused service page and CTA?
5. Does Instagram/Facebook show current proof and a reason to contact?
6. Are Pixel/analytics/form events present where access permits verification?
7. Is there a retargeting or follow-up path?
8. Name the single highest-priority bottleneck.
9. Recommend one first test.
10. Define the metric that would justify the next stage.

## 83. Cold email
Subject: 3 growth gaps I noticed for {{business}}

Hi {{name/team}}, I reviewed {{business}} from the perspective of a customer searching in London. I found three specific areas worth checking: {{gap1}}, {{gap2}}, and {{gap3}}. I can send you a short audit showing what I would fix first and why. No obligation and no ranking/lead promises — just a prioritised view of the current funnel. If useful, reply “audit” and I’ll send the outline.

## 84. Instagram / LinkedIn DM
Hi — I came across {{business}} while reviewing {{niche}} businesses in London. Your {{specific strength}} is clear, but I noticed one conversion/search gap around {{specific gap}}. I can send a short 3-point audit if useful. No generic package pitch — I’ll show the first thing I would test.

## 85. Walk-in pitch
“Hi. I’m reviewing how local businesses turn Google and social attention into enquiries. I’m not asking you to buy anything now. Can I show you one thing on your website/Google/Instagram that I think is costing enquiries? If it makes sense, I’ll leave you a 10-minute audit.”

## 86. 10-minute audit checklist
Minute 0–2: offer + CTA. 2–4: Google/local search. 4–6: website/mobile/service pages. 6–8: social proof/content/ads signals. 8–10: one bottleneck, one test, one metric.

## 87. Proposal structure
Problem → evidence → first scope → deliverables → exclusions → measurement → timeline assumptions → client inputs/access → price → optional next stage → no-guarantee boundary.

## 88. Pricing architecture
Use ranges until local conversion data is established.
- Audit / strategy sprint: £250–£750.
- Landing page: £750–£1,500.
- Small-business website: £1,500–£3,500.
- Website + broader SEO/conversion build: £3,500–£6,000+.
- Organic social/content management: £600–£1,500+/month depending on production scope.
- Meta Ads management: £500–£1,500+/month; ad spend separate.
- SEO/local SEO: £700–£2,000+/month depending on competition and scope.
Package names should describe scope, not guaranteed outcomes. Validate pricing against live London sales before treating these as a permanent rate card.

## 89. Follow-up sequence
D0 audit sent → D2 one-question follow-up → D5 add one useful observation → D10 ask whether priority changed → D20 close loop politely. Stop on opt-out or clear no.

## 90. Referral / partner script
“We build websites, SEO, social and paid acquisition around measurable bottlenecks. If you serve London SMBs in a complementary area — photo/video, accounting, print, telecom, software, property, recruitment — we can refer work only when there is a real fit. No paid recommendation without disclosure.”

## Sales rule
Lead with a specific observed problem, not a catalogue. Sell the smallest useful first step. Record source, niche, offer, response, meeting, proposal, close/loss reason and next action so the London offer improves from evidence.
