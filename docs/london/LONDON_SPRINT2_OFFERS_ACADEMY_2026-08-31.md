# London Sprint 2 — Offers + Academy

This file completes actions 1–30 from issue #915. It is an operating spec for London acquisition and Hermes Academy. No claim in this document implies a London office, guaranteed ranking, guaranteed lead volume, employment, or income.

## 1–10 Positioning and entry offers
1. **London umbrella value proposition:** Hermes connects four practical outcomes for London/UK audiences: customer acquisition, websites/automation, US-logistics education, and a measurable handoff into existing Hermes systems.
2. **Marketing promise/boundary:** diagnose the current acquisition path and improve discoverability, content, paid-media readiness, conversion and follow-up; platform results are variable and never guaranteed.
3. **IT/Web promise/boundary:** build or improve a measurable website/automation layer tied to real business objectives; third-party platform fees and custom integrations are scoped separately.
4. **US Logistics Academy promise/boundary:** teach US logistics roles, workflows, communication and operating logic through structured practice; completion is education, not a job offer.
5. **RU audience positioning:** Russian-language explanation may be used to reduce comprehension friction while professional US-market terminology and live-call readiness remain English-led where required.
6. **UA audience positioning:** Ukrainian-language explanation may be used for onboarding and comprehension while US-market practice retains the English terminology required for the role.
7. **Free audit entry offer:** a short evidence-based review that identifies the strongest current growth constraint and one practical next action; it is not a promise of ranking or sales.
8. **Website project entry offer:** discovery → existing-assets audit → scope → conversion path → build → launch QA → measurement handoff.
9. **Monthly growth retainer entry offer:** monthly work is qualified only when there is a recurring measurable workload in SEO, content, paid media, CRO or CRM follow-up.
10. **Academy readiness assessment:** assess role preference, schedule, current English, communication comfort, prior logistics/sales exposure and ability to complete repeated practice.

## 11–20 Commercial package architecture
11. **Website Starter:** focused brochure/lead site for a small business with core pages, mobile QA, conversion CTA, baseline metadata and analytics handoff. Price is quoted after scope rather than hard-coded into evergreen SEO copy.
12. **Website Growth:** larger conversion-focused site with multiple service pages, stronger internal linking, conversion paths and measurement plan.
13. **Website + CRM:** Website Growth plus form routing, lead attribution and defined CRM handoff/automation where supported.
14. **Local SEO Starter:** technical/local audit, Google Business Profile review, page/intent map, citation consistency review and measurement baseline.
15. **SEO Growth:** recurring technical/content/internal-link/local-authority execution with Search Console-led prioritisation.
16. **Social Media Starter:** channel audit, positioning, content pillars, publishing plan, scripts and lightweight reporting.
17. **Social Media Growth:** Starter plus production cadence, testing plan, community/lead handoff and stronger reporting.
18. **Meta Ads management:** account/readiness review, campaign structure, creative/landing-page alignment, measurement and optimisation; media spend is separate.
19. **Full Local Growth System:** website + local SEO + content/social + paid-media readiness + CRM handoff, qualified only when the client actually needs multiple layers.
20. **Package qualification rules:** choose the smallest package that solves the diagnosed bottleneck; do not upsell ads before tracking/landing readiness, do not sell ongoing SEO when a one-time technical fix is sufficient, and do not bundle CRM work without a real follow-up process.

## 21–30 Academy product architecture
21. **US Logistics Foundations:** market map, shipper/dealer → broker/loadboard → carrier/driver flow, terminology, documents, communication standards and role map.
22. **Freight Dispatcher track:** load search, lane logic, carrier/driver communication, rate context, appointment/status discipline and escalation practice.
23. **Load Planner track:** capacity, geography, timing, deadhead, sequencing, service constraints and planning trade-offs.
24. **Carrier Sales track:** prospecting, qualification, discovery, value framing, objection handling, follow-up and compliant contract handoff.
25. **Shipper/Dealer Sales track:** demand discovery, business-context questions, shipment needs, service fit, follow-up and relationship development.
26. **Negotiation track:** preparation, information gathering, anchoring awareness, rate/value discussion, alternatives, concessions and written confirmation.
27. **English Readiness track:** logistics vocabulary, listening, call structure, clarification, numbers/dates/locations and spoken practice; live US-market communication paths require appropriate spoken English readiness.
28. **Role-fit assessment:** score communication, analysis, persistence, comfort with calls, planning bias, sales bias, schedule and English readiness; recommend a track, not a guaranteed career outcome.
29. **Training progression map:** orientation → foundations → guided exercises → recorded practice → feedback → repeated practice → track-specific assessment → next learning recommendation.
30. **Completion boundary copy:** “Hermes Academy training develops practical knowledge and communication skills. Participation or completion does not guarantee employment, income, clients, contracts, loads, rates or business results.”
