## Positioning

Hermes coordinates information and carrier review for enclosed vehicle moves. Enclosed equipment can provide a covered trailer environment and may fit luxury, classic, exotic, low-clearance or specialty vehicles, but it does not eliminate all risk or guarantee capacity, timing or price.

Do not send value, VIN, exact addresses, private documents or personal contact details to GA4 or place them in public URLs.

## Claims and wording rules

Allowed:

- “covered trailer environment”;
- “may be considered for specialty handling requirements”;
- “subject to compatible equipment and carrier acceptance”;
- “preliminary route and capacity review”;
- “carrier retains final acceptance decision.”

Prohibited without evidence:

- zero-risk transport;
- damage-free guarantee;
- white-glove guarantee;
- climate-controlled equipment always available;
- nationwide enclosed fleet;
- guaranteed pickup/delivery;
- guaranteed insurance coverage for a specific value;
- lowest or fixed price.

## Process

1. Receive the preliminary request.
2. Review vehicle characteristics and handling needs.
3. Confirm route, dates and access constraints.
4. Determine whether enclosed equipment is operationally appropriate.
5. Match against compatible qualified carrier capacity.
6. Confirm authority, insurance, communication and documents.
7. Obtain carrier acceptance and written terms.
8. Coordinate pickup/delivery status and documents.
