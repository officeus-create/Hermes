## 2026-07-30 — Owner directive: global zero-competition operating model

The owner clarified the four-direction strategy:

- Logistics: target all US carriers with DOT and/or MC, plus owner-operators seeking MC/authority solutions, across all states; use one-year Hermes route/load history to find low-competition geographic opportunities.
- Marketing: target worldwide low-competition combinations for social media marketing, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, SEO, and website development; strengthen ProgressoPro branded social visibility.
- Academy: first target Ukrainian- and Russian-speaking people worldwide with practical B1+ English; second target English-speaking students, recent graduates, career changers, and working-age candidates in low-competition markets.
- IT: target worldwide industries and local markets with weak competition for websites, SEO, CRM, AI assistants, and automation.
- Operating cycle: run small pilots, take performance snapshots after results, and plan the next expansion from evidence.
