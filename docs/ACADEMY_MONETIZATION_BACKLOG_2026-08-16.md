# Hermes Connect Academy — Monetization Backlog

Date: 2026-08-16
Status: queued immediately after Repair Shops commercial launch gate

## Existing foundation to preserve

The current repository already has a Hermes Business Academy public subsite and curriculum data. Do not create a second Academy runtime or duplicate website.

Public curriculum foundations already present:

1. **U.S. Logistics Operations**
   - dispatch foundations;
   - carrier and broker communication;
   - equipment and lane logic;
   - documents and setup;
   - negotiation practice;
   - operating rhythm.

2. **Marketing**
   - positioning and offer;
   - website-first content;
   - platform distribution;
   - lead journey;
   - sales follow-up;
   - analytics and improvement.

Shared method already present:

- readiness;
- awareness;
- understanding;
- application;
- feedback/correction;
- human progression decision.

## Product architecture for Academy V1

Academy should be one product with reusable learning primitives:

- program landing;
- learner registration/login;
- program enrollment;
- course/module/lesson structure;
- text/video/resource lessons;
- assignment submission;
- reviewer feedback;
- progress state;
- cohort/group metadata;
- completion evidence;
- paid access state;
- learner support / questions;
- analytics for activation, learning progress, completion, purchase intent, and retention.

## Course catalog expansion

### Phase A — publish first because source material already exists

- U.S. Logistics Operations
- Practical Marketing / SMM

### Phase B — derive as structured specializations from existing operating knowledge

- Sales Fundamentals and U.S. Sales Practice

### Phase C — add after curriculum evidence is assembled

- Website Development
- Mobile Application Development
- Selling Marketing Services
- AI and Digital Tools for Operations
- Leadership / Team Management

A direction must not be advertised as a full course merely because the business uses the skill. It becomes a sellable Academy program only after modules, lessons, assignments, progression rules, and the commercial offer are defined.

## Academy monetization model to validate

Start with the smallest number of commercial offers. Avoid a large pricing matrix before users prove willingness to pay.

Recommended validation order:

1. one paid Logistics cohort/access offer;
2. one paid Marketing cohort/access offer;
3. bundle / membership only after both show real conversion and retention;
4. specialist add-ons after learner demand is measured.

## Academy definition of done

Academy V1 is commercially ready when a learner can:

1. understand which program solves their goal;
2. register;
3. choose/apply/enroll;
4. see the curriculum;
5. complete a lesson and assignment;
6. receive or see a feedback state;
7. see progress;
8. understand the price and access terms;
9. request or complete payment;
10. continue learning from phone or desktop without leaving the Hermes Connect ecosystem.

## Design timing

Do not perform the full ecosystem redesign before the Repair Shops and Academy revenue loops exist. When both product flows are functional, unify navigation, typography, card system, spacing, forms, states, account surfaces, and mobile behavior as the third major stage so the redesign follows real product information architecture rather than forcing products into a visual shell too early.
