# 1. Carrier and dispatch services

## Meaning matrix

| Dimension | Dominant competitor meaning | Market saturation | Hermes implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desired outcome | More profitable loads, stronger rates, less deadhead, consistent weekly movement. | Very high | Do not lead with generic “better rates” or “keep moving” alone. |
| Relief | Stop calling brokers, searching load boards and handling paperwork. | Very high | Necessary supporting message, not a differentiator. |
| Control | No forced dispatch; carrier has final say. | Medium and increasing | Hermes can publish carrier approval and agreed decision rules because this is already in current public copy. |
| Process | Lane planning, broker screening, route sequencing, documentation. | Medium | Strong opportunity if Hermes explains actual workflow rather than promising outcomes. |
| Human support | Dedicated dispatcher, after-hours response, multilingual communication. | High | Publish only the support scope that is operationally true for the selected service. |
| Pricing | Percentage of gross, no contract, cancel anytime. | High | Commercial terms must match the current agreement; do not reuse historical percentages without confirmation. |
| Proof | Testimonials, claimed averages, years, trucks, loads and rate metrics. | High but often weakly sourced | Hermes should prefer verifiable workflow proof, documents, screenshots and named accountable roles. |
| CTA | Get started, onboard, schedule call, submit documents. | High | Hermes CTA should request authority, equipment, lanes and operating needs for qualification. |

## Overused market language

- Better rates.
- High-paying loads.
- Keep your truck moving.
- Focus on driving.
- We handle the paperwork.
- Dedicated dispatcher.
- 24/7 support.
- Reduce deadhead.
- No forced dispatch.

## Underused or inconsistently explained meanings

- How a recommended load is evaluated before it is presented.
- What information is verified before commitment.
- How carrier preferences and approval rules are recorded.
- What happens when a load, broker, appointment or document has an exception.
- How dispatch, documents, invoicing and follow-up share ownership.
- What the carrier sees after submitting an inquiry.

## Hermes verified advantages

Status: `VERIFIED_PUBLIC`

1. Carrier approval and clear communication remain part of the operating model.
2. Hermes can begin with dispatch, documents, invoicing or another defined need rather than forcing one universal package.
3. Dispatch and back-office support cover broker communication, rate negotiation, setup packets, insurance-certificate coordination, invoicing and accounts-receivable follow-up.
4. The public service architecture supports multiple equipment categories, including car hauling, dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck, power only, hotshot, box truck and cargo van.
5. A structured direct carrier intake exists separately from the fictional Load Board demonstration.

## Positioning opportunity

`OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE`

> Dispatch support that keeps the carrier in control and makes the operating process visible.

Do not publish stronger claims such as “best load,” “highest rate,” “AI selects the most profitable load,” guaranteed weekly gross or guaranteed response time without evidence.

# 2. Dealer, auction and vehicle transportation

## Meaning matrix

| Dimension | Dominant competitor meaning | Market saturation | Hermes implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desired outcome | Move inventory faster and reduce vehicle downtime. | High | Frame transport as an inventory workflow, not only a shipment. |
| Visibility | Real-time tracking, ETA and one dashboard. | Very high among platforms | Do not claim real-time tracking unless the current customer-facing capability is verified. |
| Scale | Move one vehicle or thousands nationwide. | High | Hermes can qualify one-time and recurring volume without claiming unlimited capacity. |
| Administration | Digital BOL, gate passes, billing, payments and integrations. | High among platforms | Hermes can emphasize coordinated intake and document readiness; integrations remain hypothesis unless live. |
| Network | Vetted nationwide transporters and marketplace access. | High | Do not publish network size or instant capacity without current evidence. |
| Support | Concierge or dedicated dealer support. | Medium | Human coordination may differentiate Hermes for independent dealers if actual ownership is documented. |
| CTA | Instant quote, create account, request demo, get started. | High | Hermes should use a qualification-first transport review rather than imply an instant confirmed booking. |

## Overused market language

- Ship cars smarter.
- Real-time visibility.
- Nationwide network.
- Trusted carriers.
- Faster delivery.
- One platform.
- Hassle-free transport.
- Transparent pricing.

## Underused or inconsistently explained meanings

- The financial effect of a vehicle waiting in storage or outside sellable inventory.
- Who checks release readiness, operability and equipment fit before carrier search.
- How independent dealers receive help without adopting an enterprise platform.
- What happens if pickup or delivery slips.
- What happens if damage is reported and which party owns each step.
- How one-time requests differ from repeat dealer programs.

## Hermes verified advantages

Status: `VERIFIED_PUBLIC`

1. A direct vehicle-transport intake exists for dealers, auctions, shippers, brokers, businesses and private customers.
2. The intake captures route, timing, quantity, vehicle condition, equipment preference, auction context, release status and storage deadline before representing an option as available.
3. Copart, IAA and Manheim are used only as non-affiliated facility-context examples.
4. Sensitive release data, VINs, credentials and private documents are explicitly excluded from the public form.
5. No booking, rate, carrier assignment or timing is represented as confirmed until it is separately reviewed and accepted.

## Positioning opportunity

`OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE`

> Vehicle transportation organized around inventory readiness, release details and exception control—not only a pickup request.

Required before stronger publication: actual delay communication, damage notice, photo/BOL handling, claim escalation and customer-update procedure.

## Hermes verified advantages

Status: `VERIFIED_PUBLIC`

4. The logistics path connects dispatch, carrier/broker communication, documents, equipment logic and operational problem solving.
5. Application and inquiry routes exist without promising admission, employment, income or placement.

## Positioning opportunity

`OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE`

> Training measured by practical responsibility and operating standards—not course completion alone.

Required before stronger publication: current program fee model, active curriculum, screening sequence, named instructors/mentors, feedback ownership and evidence-supported outcomes.

# Cross-direction conclusion

The most defensible shared Hermes meaning is:

`OWNER_APPROVED_PENDING_SOURCE`

> Hermes turns disconnected work into a clearer operating system while keeping the responsible person in control.

Public pages should express the meaning differently by direction:

- Carrier: control over load decisions and operating rules.
- Dealer: control over transport readiness, exceptions and inventory movement.
- Academy: control over skill progression through practical responsibility.
- Business owner: control over marketing, leads, systems and automation.
