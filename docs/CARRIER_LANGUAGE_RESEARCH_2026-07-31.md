# Carrier Language Research Gate

Date: 2026-07-31  
Tracking: Issue #20, draft PR #19, queue tasks 139–144

## Purpose

Maintain an English-first, evidence-gated carrier-query research queue without creating thin translated pages or claiming measurable demand before evidence is attached.

All phrases in the registry are research hypotheses. They are not keyword-volume findings, Search Console findings, rankings, or publication approvals.

## Priority order

1. English — primary research queue.
2. Spanish — conditional research after measurable language-specific demand and response capacity are confirmed.
3. Russian — conditional research after measurable language-specific demand and response capacity are confirmed.
4. Ukrainian — conditional research after measurable language-specific demand and response capacity are confirmed.
5. Romanian, Lithuanian, Hindi, Punjabi, and Gujarati — research-only. No phrases are added until a capable human completes a natural U.S. logistics terminology review.

## Publication evidence required

Every language requires:

- dated source IDs for query or Search Console evidence;
- measurable language-specific demand;
- confirmed ability to respond in that language;
- capable-human terminology review;
- a unique content plan, not translated thin copy;
- canonical and hreflang planning;
- a useful CTA and disclosure of the response language.

Missing evidence produces `blocked_missing_evidence`.

Romanian, Lithuanian, Hindi, Punjabi, and Gujarati remain `research_only` even when a synthetic complete-evidence object is used in tests. A separate owner-approved evidence review is required before their priority may change.

English, Spanish, Russian, and Ukrainian can reach only `eligible_for_editorial_review`. The registry cannot create a route, add a sitemap URL, publish a translation, or change production.

## Terminology rules

Common U.S. logistics terms may remain in English when that is how the audience searches and works: `carrier`, `owner-operator`, `dispatch`, `load`, `broker`, `shipper`, `dealer`, `MC`, `DOT`, `COI`, `BOL`, `POD`, `rate confirmation`, `backhaul`, `deadhead`, and `car hauler`.

Localized text must explain terms naturally rather than force an inaccurate literal translation. Machine output is a draft only and cannot satisfy the capable-human terminology review.

Content must target a documented logistics business need and communication preference, not nationality.

## Data and claims boundary

This registry contains no private carrier or shipment data. It does not use `OFFICE 374 2026`, real Shipment History rows, current load-board observations, names, contacts, companies, MC/DOT records, addresses, rates, documents, or credentials.

No language record proves current loads, capacity, rates, revenue, direct freight, response time, or service availability.
