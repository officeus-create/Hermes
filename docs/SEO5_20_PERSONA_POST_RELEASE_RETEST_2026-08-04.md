## Evidence boundary

This retest evaluates current route architecture, visible commercial paths, forms and fallbacks, decision boundaries, privacy controls, and likely visitor comprehension. It does not establish rankings, impressions, clicks, GA4 collection, real inquiry volume, qualification rate, response time, revenue, ROI, customer preference, or field Core Web Vitals. Those outcomes remain governed by Issues #206 and #220.

## Scoring method

- `1–3`: wrong path or material trust failure;
- `4–5`: discoverable but weak, ambiguous, or proof-poor;
- `6–7`: usable with meaningful remaining friction;
- `8–9`: clear and commercially useful, but production evidence is still required;
- `10`: reserved for strong independent proof plus measured production outcomes.

Scores are internal diagnostics, not public market claims.

## Main conclusion

The original conversion architecture is materially stronger:

- ready carriers have a direct commercial intake separate from the fictional Load Board;
- dealers, auction buyers, shippers, brokers, and customers have a qualified vehicle-transport request path;
- the homepage routes visitors by role;
- Logistics pages have contextual final CTAs and direct phone/email fallbacks;
- website inquiries have a verified protected receiver contract;
- carrier, dealer, Academy, Marketing, new-authority, shipper, and partner objections are addressed without unsupported guarantees;
- analytics events, qualification definitions, and a 7/28-day measurement runbook are documented.

The largest remaining loss is now **proof, production reconciliation, and measurement**, not a shortage of pages.
