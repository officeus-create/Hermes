# Initial SEO Pilot Execution Order

This order converts the owner's global strategy into bounded work.

## Pilot 1 — Logistics

Goal: identify the lowest-competition US markets supported by the one-year Hermes pickup/delivery history.

Deliverables:

- register the exact data source;
- aggregate pickup/delivery city, state, ZIP, lane, and equipment frequency;
- compare the strongest first-party markets against live search competition;
- rank at least 20 candidate opportunities;
- select the smallest first content/landing-page cluster;
- define carrier and owner-operator conversion actions.

Do not expose private shipment or customer data.

## Pilot 2 — Academy

Goal: capture Ukrainians and Russian-speaking people worldwide with practical B1+ English, plus one low-competition English-speaking market.

Deliverables:

- query/language/location map;
- logistics, marketing, COO/operations, sales, and role-specific program fit;
- one localized entry journey and one English low-competition pilot;
- truthful language, schedule, practice, and work-path expectations;
- application fields for country, city, languages, English level, experience, schedule, and goal.

## Pilot 3 — Marketing

Goal: find one global city/industry/platform combination with weak competition and clear ProgressoPro value.

Deliverables:

- compare social media marketing, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, SEO, and web-development opportunity clusters;
- assess business density and competitor quality;
- select one niche/location pilot;
- connect website, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, and LinkedIn as one consistent brand entity;
- define consultation or audit conversion.

## Pilot 4 — IT

Goal: find one underserved business niche/location that needs website, SEO, CRM, AI, or automation.

Deliverables:

- identify outdated/no-website businesses or weak industry solutions;
- compare local and niche competition;
- select one high-value combination;
- use a real Hermes demo or verified internal capability as evidence;
- define project brief or audit conversion.

## Measurement rule

After each owner-approved production release:

1. perform an early technical/indexing check;
2. record first impressions/ranking movement;
3. take a 30-day commercial snapshot;
4. make a 60-90 day SCALE / ITERATE / STOP decision;
5. update the opportunity map and next pilot order.
