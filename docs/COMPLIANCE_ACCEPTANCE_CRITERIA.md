# Compliance Sprint 1 Acceptance Criteria

- `/privacy/` explains scope, categories, purposes, analytics, retention, security, privacy choices, California notice, and international review boundary.
- `/privacy-choices/` provides a usable public request route for access, correction, deletion, and applicable opt-out requests.
- `/company-information/` identifies Hermes Logistics LLC for U.S. logistics and avoids implying that every website direction contracts through the same entity.
- Footer links make Privacy, Privacy Choices, Company Information, and Terms discoverable.
- Existing contact-form consent and privacy-link behavior remains intact.
- Automated compliance regression is included in the standard test chain.
- Internal governance explicitly blocks new live data flows from launching without inventory, purpose, access, retention, security, notice, and jurisdiction review.
