# Hermes Academy SEO Editorial Plan — First 30 Topics

## Purpose

This is the first controlled content queue supporting the Academy program and role pages. It is intentionally limited to 30 high-intent, answer-first topics. Do not mass-publish. Each article must be evidence-based, internally linked, reviewed for duplicate intent, and tied to one clear conversion path.

## Priority model

- P0: directly supports a first-priority program page and strong candidate intent.
- P1: supports a high-value role page or major objection.
- P2: expands authority after core hubs and role pages are live.

## Article quality gate

Every article must include:

- one primary intent;
- a concise direct answer near the top;
- visible author/reviewer ownership when available;
- realistic examples;
- no unsupported earnings or employment promises;
- links to one primary program page and relevant role/support pages;
- one useful CTA;
- last-reviewed date;
- title and description within display-safe ranges;
- FAQ only when genuinely needed;
- duplicate-intent check against existing pages.

## Measurement

Track by article:

- impressions;
- clicks;
- non-branded query growth;
- average position;
- program-page clicks;
- application starts;
- consultation starts;
- assisted conversions;
- country and language distribution.

Refresh first when a page has high impressions with low CTR, ranks between positions 5 and 20, or receives candidate questions that the article does not answer.
