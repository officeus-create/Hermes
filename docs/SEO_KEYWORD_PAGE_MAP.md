# Hermes Academy SEO Keyword-to-Page Map

## Purpose

This document converts the Academy audience roadmap into an actionable search-intent map. It is a planning artifact, not a claim that every phrase has proven search volume. Validate priorities with Google Search Console, Keyword Planner, Trends, and live SERP review before implementation.

Primary commercial objective: attract qualified people who may enter practical training and then progress into work, management, or regional partnership within the Hermes ecosystem.

Primary audience: Ukrainians and Russian-speaking people worldwide with sufficient conversational English for US-facing logistics, sales, marketing, or operations work; then multilingual international candidates in priority markets.

## Operating principles

1. One primary search intent per indexable landing page.
2. Do not create separate pages for trivial keyword variants.
3. Separate program intent, profession intent, informational intent, and location intent.
4. Use English pages for US-facing profession and commercial terminology; use Ukrainian and Russian entry pages for audience-specific explanation and qualification.
5. Do not publish employment, income, duration, price, mentor, office-opening, or result claims until current and owner-approved.
6. Each page must lead to one measurable next step: choose a program, apply, or request a consultation.
7. Do not mass-produce location pages. A location page requires unique language, time-zone, hiring, or regional-partner value.

- English and schedule expectations.
- Practical exercises and performance review.
- Logistics specializations: car hauling first; other equipment only where curriculum exists.

- What English level is needed for US logistics?
- How MC and DOT numbers fit into carrier operations.
- Car hauling logistics: roles, terminology, and workflow.

## Localized audience architecture

### Ukrainian hub

Own audience-specific questions:

- Can I study while living in Ukraine or another country?
- What English level is required?
- How does the US time zone affect the schedule?
- Is prior experience required?
- Which direction fits sales, analytical, creative, or management strengths?
- What is training, practice, selection, and possible employment?

The Ukrainian hub should link to canonical English profession pages when the profession is primarily searched in English terminology.

### Russian-language hub

Serve Russian-speaking users worldwide without presenting the organization as Russian. Use neutral global wording and make Ukrainian company identity and legal/business context clear where relevant. The page should focus on language accessibility, English requirements, program choice, and international remote work.

### Hreflang/canonical note

Do not implement hreflang until the agent audits existing `/ua/`, `/ru/`, and other locale routing. Localized pages must be equivalent enough to form valid alternates; otherwise use self-canonical audience pages and explicit internal links.

## Candidate FAQ inventory

Use these questions on program pages or supporting content only when the answer is current and approved:

1. Can I start without logistics, marketing, or management experience?
2. What English level do I need?
3. Is the training live, recorded, or mixed?
4. How much time per day is required?
5. Which time zone is used?
6. Is practice included?
7. Will I speak with real candidates, carriers, customers, or simulated contacts?
8. Who reviews calls and assignments?
9. What happens if I do not pass a stage?
10. Does completing training guarantee a job?
11. Which roles can I apply for after training?
12. Can I combine study with another job?
13. Can I participate from Europe, Asia, Africa, or Latin America?
14. Can I eventually manage a team or open a regional office?
15. How are paid, promotional, scholarship, or free-track options determined?
16. What equipment and internet connection do I need?
17. How are performance and progress measured?
18. What personal information is required to apply?

## Validation queue

Before turning this map into production pages:

1. Export Search Console queries and landing pages for the last 16 months where available.
2. Check current indexed Academy, careers, localized, logistics, and marketing routes.
3. Review Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, Trends, and live competitors for each cluster and language.
4. Use Keyword Planner or another approved provider to estimate relative demand; do not treat third-party volume as exact.
5. Confirm which roles Hermes is actively prepared to train and hire now.
6. Confirm current schedule, format, duration, pricing, scholarship/free-track rules, mentors, and eligibility.
7. Build a claim-evidence matrix before publishing performance or employment claims.
8. Choose the first minimum release based on business capacity and search evidence, not keyword count alone.
