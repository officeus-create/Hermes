# Proof and Trust Evidence Register

Reviewed: 2026-08-04  
Purpose: define which Hermes trust signals are publishable now, which are private candidates, and which evidence must be collected before stronger commercial claims appear on the website.  
Privacy boundary: private Drive links, Gmail identifiers, customer/carrier names, rates, routes, contracts, VINs, shipment details, screenshots, consent records, and operational records must not be copied into this public repository.

## Status vocabulary

- `VERIFIED_PUBLIC_PROCESS` — visible and testable on the current public website.
- `VERIFIED_PRODUCTION_SYSTEM` — supported by production delivery or external smoke evidence.
- `PRIVATE_SOURCE_REVIEWED` — an owner-controlled private source exists, but it is not publication permission or public proof.
- `CONSENT_WORKFLOW_READY` — a safe process/template exists for requesting and approving proof.
- `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` — no publishable proof asset currently exists.
- `OWNER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` — exact public wording, identity, media, commercial term, or responsible person must be approved.
- `DO_NOT_PUBLISH` — stale, contradictory, confidential, unsupported, synthetic, or rights-unclear material.

# 3. Testimonial workflow readiness

Status: `CONSENT_WORKFLOW_READY`

A private evidence-safe workflow exists for:

1. manager-selected eligibility review;
2. one voluntary manual request through an existing approved relationship;
3. private response collection;
4. process, quantitative, regulated, and prohibited-content classification;
5. exact public excerpt preparation;
6. separate consent for quote, identity, role, company, logo, photo, audio/video, translation, channels, and duration;
7. publication review;
8. periodic review;
9. correction or withdrawal.

Critical rules:

- a reply is not publication consent;
- do not contact someone solely because their details exist in a CRM, spreadsheet, email archive, or chat export;
- no AI or automation may infer consent, send requests, approve, or publish;
- no service, access, payment, application, role, or support may depend on providing praise;
- quantitative claims need evidence, context, date range, baseline, and methodology;
- no full testimonial, personal contact, consent record, or operational proof belongs in public GitHub.

The workflow is ready; an approved recipient list and assigned human requester are not established by this repository.

# 4. Direction-by-direction proof backlog

## A. Carrier / dispatch proof

Current status: `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`

### Minimum viable proof asset A1 — sanitized operating workflow

Needed:

- one real completed load lifecycle or dispatch-review lifecycle;
- authorization to use a sanitized version;
- equipment class and broad region only;
- intake → review → carrier approval → communication/documents → completion/follow-up;
- no carrier/customer identity, exact route, rate, load number, contract, broker packet, VIN, private message, or credential;
- assigned reviewer and date.

Safe public outcome:

> A dated walkthrough showing what information was reviewed and where the carrier retained the final decision.

Do not claim profitability or superiority.

### Minimum viable proof asset A2 — carrier testimonial

Needed:

- genuine current/recent relationship;
- eligibility review with no dispute or pressure;
- private response;
- exact excerpt and identity-level consent;
- context date;
- review/withdrawal route.

Useful qualitative topics:

- clarity of communication;
- usefulness of the intake or checklist;
- understanding of scope and final approval;
- document organization;
- an improvement suggestion.

Quantitative rates, revenue, gross, mileage, loads, savings, or response-time statements require separate evidence review.

## B. Dealer / customer vehicle-transport proof

Current status: `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`

### Minimum viable proof asset B1 — sanitized transport workflow

Needed:

- one authorized completed movement;
- vehicle count and general category;
- broad origin/destination region, not exact addresses;
- release readiness, operability, equipment need, confirmation boundary, and update workflow;
- safe documentation screenshot or reconstructed diagram based on verified steps;
- exact permission for any company/identity/media.

Safe public outcome:

> A dated process example showing how release, condition, equipment, timing, and separately confirmed transport were coordinated.

Do not claim guaranteed pickup, avoided storage fees, damage resolution, price savings, or real-time tracking without evidence.

### Minimum viable proof asset B2 — dealer/customer testimonial

Useful qualitative topics:

- intake clarity;
- visibility of what information was still missing;
- communication and next-step clarity;
- understanding of independent carrier responsibility;
- what should be improved.

## C. Logistics SEO / Marketing proof

Current status: `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`

### Minimum viable proof asset C1 — current Hermes implementation case

Hermes may use its own website as a transparent first-party implementation record if clearly labeled as internal work rather than an external client result.

Publishable evidence can include:

- dated baseline and current route count;

- form-delivery activation evidence;
- specific before/after public screenshots;
- Search Console and GA4 data only after account, property, date range, metric definition, and export are verified;
