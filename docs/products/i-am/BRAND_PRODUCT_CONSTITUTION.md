# I am — Brand & Product Constitution
Status: FOUNDER CONCEPT / PRODUCT INCUBATION. Owner: Hermes Connect / Technology; canonical issue #1776; Italy Tax Free #1773.

## Brand
Working name: **I am**, meaning «я есть здесь, там, везде». Tagline: **Here. There. Everywhere.** Longer explanation: **A personal interface to the world.** Not yet cleared as trademark, company name, domain or app-store mark.
Mission: people describe an outcome in voice/text, see a verified country-aware path, and may opt to pin only the capabilities they need to their private workspace.
Voice tone: direct, source-first, reassuring, truthful about limitations; never promise refunds, licenses, government access, savings or investments.

## The I am loop
VOICE OR TEXT INTENT → USER CONFIRMS COUNTRY/GOAL → OFFICIAL POLICY / SOURCE WITH DATE → MULTI-STEP PLAN → OPTIONAL CONSENTED PINNED WORKSPACE → AUTHORIZED PARTNER → VERIFIED RECEIPT → RESULT.
- The currently proposed browser example is a deterministic local demo, not an AI call, voice recorder, agency submission or regulated action.
- Future model router is replaceable; source, rules, permissions, consent, history and Company/Workspace identity remain in Hermes Connect.
- PII by design: ephemeral session by default; private workspace only with verified auth, minimized fields and explicit consent. No automatic passport/payment/crypto data collection.

## Prioritized modules
1. Italy shopping / Tax Free informational eligibility guidance: actual refunds require merchant/OTELLO/operator/PSP permissions.
2. Travel and vehicle rental: comparison only with verified offers and no undocumented discount claims.
3. Business formation: official country steps and permissioned providers (Estonia and U.S. providers as category references, not I am partners).
4. Documents, visas and state processes: lawful APIs and qualified human/official handoffs only.
5. Digital-asset/token formation: future discovery only, requires MiCA and other applicable laws. No token issuance, fundraising, custody, investment advice or KYC bypass.
Service availability and jurisdiction restrictions derive from applicable U.S./EU/UN sanctions laws, export controls and provider restrictions, not undifferentiated country stereotypes.

## Competitive advantage is measurable, not a slogan
Category leaders: Global Blue (Tax Free and merchant network), Planet (merchant tax-free/payments), Stripe Atlas (Delaware incorporation), Estonia e-Residency (state digital identity). I am currently has a narrower prototype; do not claim category leadership.
Potential differentiation: one goal-oriented conversation, country rules with links, user-controlled modular workspace, open provider abstraction and audited human/official handoffs.
Head-to-head test on identical tasks: correct next step rate, time-to-guidance, task completion after legal handoff, repeat data entry, citation freshness, user consent errors, support quality, cost per actual completed task.
Never describe an untested product as '100% ahead'. Compare verified benchmark scores and iterate.
