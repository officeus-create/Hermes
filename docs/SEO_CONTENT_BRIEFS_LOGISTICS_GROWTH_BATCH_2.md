## Shared rules

- Hermes coordinates, reviews, negotiates, and supports operations; do not imply ownership of trucks, warehouses, ports, auctions, or dealerships unless separately verified.
- Do not guarantee loads, rates, revenue, pickup dates, delivery dates, capacity, rankings, or customer acquisition.
- The carrier retains final approval of every load and operating decision.
- Do not expose customer names, VINs, exact addresses, phone numbers, emails, documents, or private shipment history.
- Use `Service`, `BreadcrumbList`, and visible `FAQPage` markup only when the page visibly supports those entities.
- Every page needs a unique title, description, H1, canonical, CTA, internal links, and reviewed scope statement.
