# Revenue-first product priority

Date: 2026-08-16

Current execution order:

1. **Hermes Connect Repair Shops** — finish the usable owner-to-booking loop and convert real shops into paying customers.
2. **Hermes Connect Academy** — launch paid learning on the existing Academy foundation, starting with U.S. Logistics Operations and Marketing.
3. **Unified Hermes ecosystem redesign** — normalize the site and product UI after both revenue loops are operational, so design follows the real information architecture and user journeys.

Security-hardening work that does not block the customer/revenue loop is not the active product priority in this phase. Payment correctness, customer-facing truth, data integrity, and anything required to safely collect money remain release concerns for any future automated checkout.

Product-development rule for this phase: do not add speculative Hermes Connect modules ahead of observed Repair Shop or Academy customer demand. Prioritize activation friction, usefulness, conversion, purchase intent, payment/close outcomes, retention, and direct user feedback.
