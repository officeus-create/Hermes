# SEO Revenue Priority Backlog

## Objective

Move the highest-value Hermes pages from technical readiness to measurable commercial outcomes.

The operating funnel is:

`indexable page -> organic visibility -> click -> landing session -> CTA -> qualified inquiry -> sales follow-up`

Traffic without a measurable CTA and follow-up path is not treated as a successful outcome.

## Priority order

### Tier 1 — Logistics pages closest to revenue

1. `/logistics/appleton-wi-vehicle-transport/`
   - Revenue action: qualified vehicle-transport request.
   - Primary CTA: logistics request preview/contact handoff.
   - Failure signals: not indexed, no impressions after review window, sessions without CTA, CTA without review owner.

2. Wisconsin state and city vehicle-transport cluster
   - Revenue action: local vehicle-transport inquiry.
   - Primary CTA: request review.
   - Failure signals: duplicate intent, cannibalization, weak internal links, no clear local-to-commercial handoff.

3. `/services/dealer-vehicle-transportation/`
   - Revenue action: dealer or shipper transport inquiry.
   - Primary CTA: dealer/shipper request.
   - Failure signals: informational traffic without commercial CTA, unclear qualification requirements.

4. `/services/car-hauling-dispatch/`
   - Revenue action: carrier/owner-operator dispatch inquiry.
   - Primary CTA: carrier readiness review.
   - Failure signals: clicks without carrier CTA, unqualified generic contact path, unsupported earnings claims.

5. Auction pickup and supporting checklist pages
   - Revenue action: move users from resource intent to transport request.
   - Primary CTA: resource-to-commercial-page click, then logistics request.
   - Failure signals: resource traffic trapped on informational pages, no contextual service link.

### Tier 2 — Digital services with high contract value

6. `/services/seo-for-logistics-companies/`
   - Revenue action: SEO audit or consultation inquiry.
   - Primary CTA: SEO review request.
   - Failure signals: traffic without case proof, vague CTA, no service-fit qualification.

7. `/services/seo-for-independent-auto-dealers/`
   - Revenue action: dealer SEO consultation.
   - Primary CTA: SEO/local SEO review.
   - Failure signals: no link to relevant logistics/dealer proof, weak differentiation.

8. Website development, redesign, SEO and Local SEO national hubs
   - Revenue action: website or SEO project inquiry.
   - Primary CTA: audit, redesign, new build or local SEO review.
   - Failure signals: broad traffic with no niche route, CTA ambiguity, no case-study handoff.

## Required measurement for each URL

- publication status;
- production availability;
- canonical and sitemap owner;
- Search Console inspection state;
- indexation state;
- impressions, clicks, CTR and average position;
- landing sessions and engaged sessions;
- CTA type and event count;
- qualified inquiry count when a reviewed source exists;
- next action and accountable owner.

Unknown values remain null. They must never be converted to zero without source evidence.

## Revenue decision rules

- Not published: release blocker takes priority over content expansion.
- Published but not inspected: submit for review after production verification.
- Inspected but not indexed: diagnose canonical, quality, duplication and crawl paths.
- Indexed with impressions but weak CTR: review title, description and intent alignment.
- Clicks without sessions: investigate analytics or destination problems.
- Sessions without CTA: improve commercial handoff and CTA relevance.
- CTA without qualified review: assign a manual review owner and response SLA.
- Qualified inquiries without sales outcome tracking: connect aggregate stage counts before scaling traffic.

## Initial KPI hierarchy

1. qualified inquiries;
2. sales-contacted qualified inquiries;
3. CTA-to-qualified-inquiry conversion;
4. session-to-CTA conversion;
5. organic clicks;
6. CTR;
7. indexed commercial URL coverage;
8. impressions.

## Release discipline

Do not publish mass city pages to increase URL count. New pages enter the queue only when existing Tier 1 and Tier 2 URLs have production parity, indexation checks, measurable CTAs and a defined follow-up owner.
