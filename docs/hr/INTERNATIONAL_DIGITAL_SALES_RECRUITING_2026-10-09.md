# International Digital Solutions Sales — recruiting launch brief

Status: OWNER-DIRECTED / PREPUBLICATION. This document is not a verified live vacancy, offer, or JobPosting source.
Date: 2026-10-09
Owner: Hermes HR / international sales

## Scope
Recruit sales and business-development representatives for website creation/redesign, SEO/GEO/AEO, CRM, AI automation, business-process digitization, and related ongoing services. Initial priority is the U.S. market; expand to language-qualified country markets based on demand. The 110-country goal is an expansion ambition, not a claim of current presence or hiring eligibility.

## Role description
Title: International Sales Manager — Websites, CRM, SEO & AI Solutions.
Target customers: small businesses through enterprise operations; qualification and technical scoping required before commitments.
Responsibilities: identify ICP and territory; research prospects; conduct permitted outreach; discover operational needs; record CRM pipeline; coordinate solution discovery and proposals; close approved commercial terms; preserve lead source and handoff evidence.
Candidate requirements: professional conversational proficiency in the language of target market; sales experience and measurable KPIs preferred; ability to present and qualify digital solutions; disciplined CRM updates. Ukrainian-language sales requires fluent Ukrainian. Do not assume one candidate can sell in all 110 markets.

## Candidate intake (private)
Name/contact; country and time zone; target country/language and proficiency; product selected; 3–6 month sales results and evidence (redacted); average deal value; outbound channels; sample 14-day plan; availability. Separate training candidates from experienced applicants; never promise employment or unpaid productive work.

## CRM stages
INQUIRY → QUALIFICATION → EVIDENCE_REVIEW → PRODUCT_TEST → INTERVIEW → APPROVED_ROLE / TRAINING_ELIGIBLE / DECLINED → ONBOARDING → FIRST_VERIFIED_RESULT.
Capture source/UTM, vacancy ID, language, market, product, reviewer, consent and timestamps. Deduplicate on authorized identity, do not expose private candidate data in public analytics.
