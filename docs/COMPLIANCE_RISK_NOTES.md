# Compliance Risk Notes

Current baseline deliberately avoids these unsupported claims:

- that CCPA/CPRA applies to every Hermes business;
- that GDPR/UK GDPR applies merely because the site is accessible internationally;
- that Hermes never shares personal information in any legal sense across every future provider configuration;
- that every Hermes direction contracts through Hermes Logistics LLC;
- that the site is fully compliant with every U.S. state or international privacy law.

These determinations depend on the actual business, thresholds, audience, vendors, data flow, and commercial model.
