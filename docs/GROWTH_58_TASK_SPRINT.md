## Publication gate for geo/language pages

A page must score at least 7/10 before implementation:

- verified demand or commercial evidence: 0–2;
- genuinely weak search competition: 0–2;
- clear fit with a real Hermes service: 0–2;
- enough unique local/language value: 0–2;
- crawlable internal link and useful CTA: 0–1;
- approved evidence source: 0–1.

Pages below the threshold remain research records and are not published.

## Safety

- No production merge or deployment without owner approval.
- No unsupported salary, job placement, income, ranking, load, insurance, financing, or client guarantees.
- No mass-generated location pages before evidence and competition validation.
- No secrets, billing, DNS, Cloudflare, or account changes.
