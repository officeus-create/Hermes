# SEO Wisconsin Foundation

Updated: 2026-07-13

## Implemented

- Unique search title and description for Logistics, Marketing, Academy, and IT Development.
- Wisconsin-first service-area statement on every direction page.
- United States availability retained without claiming a physical office in every market.
- Organization structured data on the homepage.
- Service structured data on each direction page.
- Canonical URL, Open Graph, X card, and robots metadata.
- Sitemap `lastmod` dates.
- `llms.txt` business summary and claim boundaries.
- Automated validation for metadata, visible service-area text, and valid JSON-LD.

## Deliberately Not Implemented

- No invented Wisconsin address.
- No mass-generated city pages.
- No keyword stuffing.
- No fake reviews, ratings, statistics, or local performance claims.
- No Search Console or analytics account connection yet.
- No public News / Insights section until the editorial pilot has reviewed content.
