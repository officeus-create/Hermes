# Canonical Company Facts Approval

Status: `OWNER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`  
Purpose: establish one controlled source for public Hermes company facts before updating the website, schema, recruiting platforms, directories, social profiles, sales materials, or AI/entity references.

This file is a public-safe approval template. It must not contain credentials, private account details, private addresses, contracts, customer/carrier identities, rates, routes, VINs, private messages, or unpublished legal records.

## Status vocabulary

- `VERIFIED_PUBLIC` — supported by a current public authoritative source.
- `VERIFIED_PRIVATE` — supported by an owner-controlled source but not automatically approved for publication.
- `HISTORICAL_ONLY` — accurate for a stated past period, not a current company fact.
- `OWNER_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` — exact value or wording requires approval.
- `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` — no adequate source has been established.
- `DO_NOT_PUBLISH` — confidential, contradictory, stale, unsupported, or rights-unclear.

## Approval rules

1. Every approved value needs a source type, verification date, owner, and next review date.
2. Historical personal achievements must not become current corporate claims without separate evidence.
3. Hermes Logistics, ProgressoPro, Hermes Business Academy, and IT/automation services must remain distinguishable.
4. A profile may use shorter wording, but it must not change the underlying meaning.
5. Employee count, offices, fleet, authorities, agencies, countries, customers, shipment/load volume, revenue, rankings, results, technology capabilities, and response times require explicit evidence.
6. No blank field may be filled by inference from an old profile, resume, proposal, social post, screenshot, or AI-generated summary.
