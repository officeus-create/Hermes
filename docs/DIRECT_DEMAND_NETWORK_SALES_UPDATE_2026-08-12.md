# Hermes Logistics direct-demand network — sales and communications update

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: copy-ready after the public Direct Vehicle Transport Network page is deployed
Owner track: #402

## 1. Canonical sales idea

Hermes is developing an additional source of vehicle-transport demand around a simple flow:

`Customer / Dealer / Shipper -> Hermes -> reviewed vehicle-transport request -> suitable carrier/equipment review`

The long-term value is not "we have guaranteed direct loads." The value is that Hermes is building customer acquisition, transport-request intake, carrier operating profiles, and evidence-gated SEO into one system instead of depending only on third-party load boards.

Carrier operating history can help Hermes understand equipment fit, service areas, preferred markets, restrictions, and recurring operating patterns. Verified patterns may become research candidates for useful public route/service content. They do not automatically become public SEO pages and never justify publishing private shipment records.

## 2. What Sales may say now

- Customers, dealers, shippers, brokers, and businesses already have a direct vehicle-transport request path on the Hermes website.
- Hermes already reviews carrier equipment, authority, insurance, operating area, preferences, and availability as part of carrier qualification and dispatch work.
- Hermes is developing a direct-demand layer that connects customer acquisition and public search visibility with reviewed carrier operating fit.
- Verified operating patterns can help identify route/service topics worth researching for public SEO content.
- The strategy is designed to add another source of demand alongside load boards, brokers, dealers, shippers, and repeat relationships.
- Every request and every carrier match is reviewed separately, and the carrier keeps final load approval.

## 3. What Sales must not promise

Do not say or imply:

- "You will get direct loads after 2–3 months."
- "Hermes will rank your routes on Google in 2–3 months."
- "Every carrier gets SEO pages for their lanes."
- "Hermes already has live direct load inventory on the Load Board."
- "We can guarantee customers, rankings, traffic, rates, mileage, utilization, lanes, or revenue."
- "Your private shipment history will be published."
- "A customer request automatically books a carrier."

Use "building," "developing," "researching," "reviewing," and "can inform" for future capability. Use "available now" only for the actual public customer request and carrier intake paths.

## 9. KPI after release

Keep evidence classes separate:

1. page indexed / discovered by search platforms;
2. non-branded impressions and clicks for relevant vehicle-transport/direct-demand query families;
3. CTA clicks from the network page to customer transport intake and carrier dispatch review;
4. completed/delivered transport requests;
5. human-qualified requests;
6. carrier-side inquiries that reference the direct-demand value proposition;
7. eventually, reviewed customer requests that can be matched to approved carrier capacity.

Do not report a form submit, IndexNow HTTP 200, impression, or page view as a direct load or revenue result.
